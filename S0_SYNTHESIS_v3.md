## Verdict (one paragraph)

Two tracks, now carried through their corrections, their composition test, and their
storage comparison, converge on one bounded account. The **corrections** (I1, C1) sharpen
without overturning anything: the boundary successor's production-closure claim is scoped
down to {W, C, B} (the five-component verdict stays with AC105, and AC115 then supplies the
missing composition argument); the "post-onset admission zero" read was post-mortem; and
"supplied space" is reclassified from limitation to permitted substrate, leaving the
non-spatial informational core as the **sole** clause-(ii) limit. The **reliability
correction** (C1) withdraws C0's "not identifiable / closed" in favor of *identifiable in
principle as a question about monitoring the actual estimator*. The **integration study**
(AC115) established that the boundary-exchange role **composes with the five-mechanism
closure at the mechanism level** — six functions exercised in one organism, 16/16 — while
**survival-level composition is NOT confirmed** (G4–G7 retained as seed-dependent failures,
AC68/AC39). The **storage comparison** (AC116) tested the first-order storage question and returned
**F1** (weak dominance at the sign-flip resolution floor, M2-corrected): maintained history does not
demonstrate a *significant* graded income advantage over the strongest tuned memoryless rival at the
organism scale. Assembled: **production closure
(clause i) stands SUPPORTED and bounded; whole-organism unity (clause ii) stands NOT
ESTABLISHED, limited solely by the non-spatial core (a re-architecture, not a run); and the
"maintained" half of the level-(c) representation claim has now been tested and bounded to
"content load-bearing, ongoing repair not tested (M2)."** The single strongest next question
is the **C1 candidate — does the organism maintain a second-order state that monitors its
own estimate's reliability and regulates maintenance in response?** — because it is the one
track this cycle *reopened* (from "closed" to "identifiable in principle"), its answer is
load-bearing in both directions, and every other track is either stable in-model (autonomy)
or closed by a recorded negative (storage). Neither autopoiesis nor consciousness is
claimed; a completed board is neither.

## 1. The corrections, and their effect on previous conclusions

### 1.1 I1 — the boundary correction (three items)

1. **Composition.** A4 and S0-v2 had transferred the five-component production-closure
   verdict (K3, over {W, C, B, description, program} + maintained state) into the AC114
   boundary successor *without a composition argument*. AC114 contains none of the five
   internalization mechanisms (no description store, succession, reconstruction, operational
   memory, or decision allowance — verified in code). **Effect:** AC114 supports closure
   over {W, C, B} only; the five-component verdict stays with AC105. The correction then
   *named the composition requirement* — add the SR-2 admission gate to AC105 and show
   byte-identity — which AC115 (I2–I5) went on to satisfy. So the error is not merely
   scoped down; it produced the integration-dependency list that the integration study
   closed.
2. **Temporal window.** The frozen G3 "post-onset admission zero" of `no_B_retention` was
   read as an actively blocked interface, but that window is **post-mortem** (death at
   t≈379–386 precedes ONSET=512). **Effect:** the mechanism claim survives — the site gate
   refuses admission *while alive* (103–146/103–146 attempts refused, 0 admitted, vs the
   no-gate twin's 57–59/57–59 admitted) — but the honest discriminator is the alive-window
   measurement, not the frozen scalar. The claim travels with the corrected discriminator.
3. **Spatial criterion.** "Supplied space/geometry" was counted as a clause-(ii)
   limitation and called "the unfixable limit (infinite regress)." Supplied coordinates and
   physical laws are **permitted substrate**, not a failure of autopoiesis — every
   formalization bottoms out in supplied laws. **Effect:** clause (ii) is limited by **one**
   modeling declaration (the non-spatial informational core), not two.

### 1.2 C1 — the reliability correction (five inference errors)

C0 had collapsed the "reliability tier" (monitoring one's own estimate) into first-order
inference and declared it "not identifiable / closed." C1 corrected the five inference
errors that produced the collapse: (1) equal marginal productivity rates do not imply
identical observation histories; (2) open diagnostic observations and the persistent latent
cause must enter identifiability; (3) an ideal sufficient statistic is not automatically a
damaged, resource-constrained implementation; (4) reuse of first-order evidence does not
rule out a distinct monitoring *function*; (5) AC110 tested a third-person ablation of
repair, **not** an organism's estimate of its own computational integrity.

**Effect.** The collapse is retained **only as a scoped non-identifiability result under
the ideal-observer assumption** — which the AC architecture violates by construction (the
estimate is damaged, W-gated, spatially and temporally separated from its maintenance
machinery — exactly the Fleming & Daw 2017 conditions for a distinct second-order
computation). The corrected disposition is therefore *identifiable in principle as a
question about monitoring the actual estimator*, not "closed." The one load-bearing caution
is **retained unchanged**: estimating ε (or q) alone does not establish metacognition. No
organism-scale study was authorized *by the C1 card*; the next question is chosen here
(item 7).

### 1.3 Net effect on previous conclusions

Nothing was overturned. Production closure (clause i) is SUPPORTED and bounded — now
*inherited by composition* (AC115 G1 byte-identity) rather than re-derived. The reliability
tier is reopened from "closed" to "identifiable in principle, not yet demonstrated." The
"maintained representation" claim's *maintenance* word is now bounded by a recorded
negative (item 4). A naming collision is noted for the record: two board cycles reuse the
C1/C2/C3 labels (the old N/A/C/I/P cycle vs the current I/C cycle); they are disambiguated
in `EVIDENCE_INDEX_v3.md` item 5.

## 2. Composition: does the boundary mechanism compose with the integrated closure?

**Yes — at the mechanism level; No — at the survival level.** The three judgments are
separated, none standing in for another:

- **Mechanism-level composition — SUPPORTED (16/16).** AC115 (finals 6600–6607, 208 rows,
  untouched seeds) is the first run showing AC114's SR-2 link-specific admission gate
  composed with the AC105 five-mechanism closure **in one organism**. G1 (single-change
  license) holds byte-for-byte (`state_hash`) — `keep`/`rival`/`reference` are identical to

  a puncture zeroes channel-0 admission while the count rival admits) and G3 (D2 local:
  a non-gate puncture leaves both channels admitting) hold 16/16. The admission gate adds
  no resource competition and disturbs none of the five mechanisms when inert.
- **Survival-level composition — NOT CONFIRMED.** The four gates that prespecified a
  survival/completion clause fail on a minority of finals (G4 2/16, G5 2/16, G6 4/16,
  G7 6/16) and are **retained as failures** — a seed-dependent transfer failure, not a
  mechanism failure. Two failure categories, not one (AC115_ERRATA_v1.md): an early W/C
  collapse under B suppression (6602, t=347) and a single-particle retention leak (6606,
  completes), plus late survival misses under simultaneous stress (the AC68 W/C bimodality
  re-entering through the 16,384-tick horizon, plus the puncture leak, per AC39). In every
  failing individual the admission, retention, and reconstruction discriminations hold;
  what does not transfer is the survival clause.

So the licensed composition claim: **one organism renews its produced exchange boundary
while the admission gate is byte-inert at the intact boundary, admission is link-specific
and local, and the five internalization mechanisms are preserved byte-for-byte and
recorded exercised.** This closes the I0/I1 gap — the exchange role rides the produced
boundary that the five-mechanism organism already maintains — one step above AC114, one
step below clause (ii).

## 4. The storage comparison: established vs unresolved

**Established (AC116, F1, frozen finals 6600–6607, 240 rows).** In the pure
occluded-`used_held` world (ε=0, q=0.9), a maintained integer counter does **not**
demonstrate a significant post-cause income advantage over the strongest tuned memoryless
rival: mean +200/seed (≈3 contacts), exact sign-flip **p = 0.0625**, differences
`[0, 0, 0, +128, +192, +192, +512, +576]`. The counter is **weakly dominant** (0 seeds
worse, 5 better, 3 tied) but sub-significant. Its accumulation is causally effective (G3:
drops earlier than scramble under move in 12/16, false-relinquishes under cut in 4/16 where
scramble holds 0/16), and its open-blind latch buys a real cut-safety edge (4/16 vs 16/16
false relinquishments) that this world's economics **do not price** (a false drop merely
re-binds the entry, `life=64`). G1 (world license, byte-identity to frozen AC110 at q=0.5)
and G2 (no-cause identity) pass; G4 is recorded F1, not moved.

**Unresolved / explicitly not erased.** The F1 verdict is a *comparative usefulness bound
in the tested task*. It does **not** erase: AC107's positive content-role (the estimate's
value is load-bearing), AC108's acquisition-necessity (the paid update write is load-bearing
for accuracy), AC110's repair-dependence (repair is load-bearing for post-window storage,
8/16 vs 16/16), or AC113's single-counter sufficiency. The storage question is **suspended at the
resolution floor** (not closed — weak dominance, M2); it does not close the *representational* claims,
which rest on content, not on graded income.

The q=0.7 secondary world is declared, not frozen — left unfrozen rather than run as a
second study.

## 5. The corrected reliability disposition

**The reliability tier is identifiable in principle — as a question about monitoring the
actual estimator — not yet demonstrated, and not authorized (here, named as the next
question, item 7).** C0's collapse is correct *only* under the ideal-observer assumption
(one internal state supports both the decision and its confidence readout, losslessly
maintained). That assumption is false in the AC architecture by construction: the estimate
lives in one substrate (`bel_off`), its repair and reacquisition in distinct ones, triggered
at distinct times, and it can be corrupted or starved independently of the world evidence
(AC110 post-window drift; AC67/71 sticky corruption). Those are the conditions the
metacognition literature identifies as necessary for a *distinct* second-order computation
(Fleming & Daw 2017: separation of decision and confidence states in space/time;
Maniscalco & Lau 2012: meta-d′ ≠ d′ when the readout accesses different information).

The valid core that is **retained**: estimating ε does not by itself establish
metacognition — sensitivity to uncertainty is a first-order Bayesian competence. The
candidate question (item 7) is built to respect exactly that boundary.

## 6. The bounded autopoiesis verdict

- **Clause (i) — production closure: SUPPORTED, bounded.** Every level-(a) component
  {W, C, B, description, program} meets C1–C5 and every maintained-state item {pointer,
  coordination, route memory, decision state} meets S1–S4, in the AC105 architecture, under
  the accepted substrate convention (J1). The integrated successor inherits this verbatim
  by composition (AC115 G1) and adds the exchange role to B (retention + exchange), so the
  material-layer cycle **B → (retention + exchange) → production → B** is established by
  ablation, not asserted. This is clause (i) as the finite criterion defines it — not
  autopoiesis unqualified, not "alive."
- **Clause (ii) — whole-organism unity: NOT ESTABLISHED.** The produced unity is a unity of
  the material (constituent) layer — a produced, semipermeable, self-renewing perimeter. The
  whole organization (material layer + informational core) does not constitute itself as a
  concrete unity in space, because the informational core (program, description, pointer,
  coordination, route memory) lives in fixed arrays never positioned and never passed to
  `tr.move` — "inside" only by declaration. This is the **sole** remaining genuine
  clause-(ii) limitation, and it is a finite successor question (a re-architecture that
  spatially realizes the controller), **not** a permanently untestable fact and not a run
  inside the frozen model.
- **Composition — SUPPORTED at the mechanism level; NOT CONFIRMED at the survival level**
  (item 2), recorded as a caveat, not a closure gap.

Levels (c)/(d)/(e) are untouched; nothing here implies consciousness.

## 7. The single strongest justified next question

**Chosen: does the organism maintain a second-order state `m` — in a substrate distinct
from its first-order estimate `e` — whose content is "`e` is currently unreliable (corrupted
/ stale / starved of repair)", computed from the organism's own maintenance bookkeeping, and
whose causal role is to regulate maintenance, such that `m` and `e` can be dissociated by
selective damage — and does `m` beat a state-blind fixed policy and the raw corruption
reflex?**

This is C1's outcome-(a) candidate question, and it is the natural successor to the whole
cycle for one reason: the cycle's negative results (AC109 storage-inert, AC110 repair not
load-bearing in-window, AC116 storage not income-advantageous) collectively show that the
**first-order** "maintained" half of the level-(c) claim shows **no demonstrated significant graded
advantage** — content is load-bearing, ongoing repair not tested (M2). The reliability correction then
showed that the
*second-order* question (is there a state whose referent is the estimate's own integrity?)
is **identifiable in principle**, precisely because the architecture separates the decision
state from the maintenance machinery in space and time. That is the one track this cycle
reopened with a positive ceiling, and the one whose answer is load-bearing in both
directions.

**Why this, against the named alternatives.**

- **Three-cause integration** (corruption as a third cause for the cognitive estimate) is
  the genuine composition question for a *stronger whole-organism* claim — but AC111 already
  showed the corruption-cause interaction is a seed-dependent, survival-neutral behavioural
  interference (G3/G5 12/16), so a third-cause run is likely to reproduce "named
  interference" rather than a clean verdict, at higher cost and with a murkier gate. It is
  subordinate to establishing whether the *representation* itself earns its keep. Named
  runner-up.
- **Ongoing-repair isolation** (N1 point 3: sustained repair coupling of the estimate) is a
  real gap, but AC110 already answered its specific in-window form (reacquisition carries
  correctness; repair is load-bearing only post-window), and the general "repair is
  load-bearing" question is already answered YES on the autonomy side (AC67: repair cut dies
  8/8; AC91/92: W-catalyzed reconstruction is load-bearing). Re-running it on the estimate
  with a sticky damage model is a robustness re-test of AC110, not a new ceiling — and its
  precondition (exercisable repair) is subsumed by the chosen question's world.
- **Clause-(ii) re-architecture** (spatially realizing the controller) is a modeling
  decision, not a run; the autonomy track is stable in-model, and there is no next in-model
  autonomy run.

**The specific evidence needed to answer it** (prespecified, so a "yes" and a "no" are both
clean):

1. A frozen runner in the world where `e` is sometimes wrong *for implementation reasons*
   (the AC110 post-window drift regime and/or the AC67/71 sticky-`|=` corruption regime),
   adding a stored, maintained state `m` in a substrate distinct from `e`.
2. **Content:** `m` must be a function of the maintenance bookkeeping (the corruption
   signal obs bit 2, whether a repair write was starved by the budget or W=0, whether
   reacquisition recently fired), not (only) of the cause evidence — so `m` reads the
   implementation's degradation, the referent C0 claimed did not exist.
3. **Dissociation:** selective damage to `m` leaves the first-order relinquish-vs-hold
   decision intact while changing maintenance behaviour; selective damage to `e` changes the
   decision. The two directions separable is the meta-d′ ≠ d′ signature and the test that
   `m` is not a re-parameterization of the decision threshold (AC11's "the optimum is a
   level" lesson, at the maintenance layer).
4. **Causal role on the maintenance direction:** scramble/cut `m` changes repair / renewal
   / reacquisition while the first-order decision still works.
5. **Rivals (not strawmen), all implemented:** (i) a state-blind fixed maintenance policy
   (fixed duty), (ii) the raw obs-bit-2 reflex (no stored `m` — the existing AC9/AC110
   watchdog and the null), (iii) an ε-estimator arm. `m` must beat (i) and (ii) on a
   prespecified maintenance-behaviour endpoint.
6. **Consistency gates promoted:** `m` with its maintenance cut must reproduce the reflex;
   the reflex must reproduce the state-blind policy at fixed duty.

**Falsification.** `m` is falsified if scrambling/cutting it leaves maintenance behaviour
unchanged while the first-order decision still works (the organism uses the raw obs-bit-2
reflex, not a stored monitor), or if a state-blind fixed policy reproduces `m`'s maintenance
behaviour (the content is inert — AC109's lesson one level up). Both are prespecifiable and
would close the reliability tier for real, which is itself a definitive, paper-worthy
negative.

**Cost and discipline.** One runner extending the AC110/AC116 world with a stored monitor
state and a damage model that actually flips replicas within the window (the AC67 option-2
regime, rate and read threshold stated together per AC14); engineering seeds first, then a
hashed protocol, then disjoint finals, with audit/replay/unit tests per the extension
checklist. No repair mechanism is *added* beyond what the frozen physics already supports;
the gate is on maintenance behaviour, not survival (survival reported as a bimodality-aware
lower bound). The constraint is respected: this is a representation study, not a repair
study, and the AC116 F1 is not a license for further storage-comparison runs — it is a
recorded finding that moves the question to the second-order level.
