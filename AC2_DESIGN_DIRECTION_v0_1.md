# AC2 direction: producing the mechanisms that repair the controller

Proposed only, 2026-09-14. No AC2 implementation, results or final-run authority.
This document identifies a stronger successor to the bounded AC1 witness.

## What should count as progress

AC1 moves acquired policy information into the paid vulnerability loop. Its
demonstrated priorities, generic sensor, decoder and write actuator remain
supplied. A produced boundary and maintained actuator would address further
autopoietic criteria; renaming those protected operations would not.

Full physical self-manufacture is not the same as independence from physical
laws or environmental supplies. A model may have fixed reaction laws. The
question is whether the particular structures that enable its activity are
produced and maintained by that activity, with all external enabling support
disclosed. A simulated result would remain model-relative.

## Candidate causal architecture

Use a finite spatial reaction world with explicit molecules and locations,
rather than an extra abstract survival-quality variable.

| Constituent | Causal role | Production/maintenance dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P: acquired controller traces | Allocate available precursor among local production and repair sites | Surviving trace information plus active W, precursor and usable energy |
| W: short-lived write/assembly catalysts | Physically enable incorporation of precursor at target sites | Production reactions selected by P; energy supplied through C |
| C: resource-conversion catalysts | Convert entering substrate into energy usable by writes and assembly | W-mediated local production; retention and substrate availability through B |
| B: boundary components | Form a spatial enclosure that changes diffusion/retention of C, W and internal precursor | W-mediated assembly paid by C and allocated through P |

The generic simulator specifies diffusion, reactions and local catalyst matching.
It must not provide an additional controller choosing damaged targets, restoring
learned bits, relocating molecules, repairing a boundary or suppressing leakage.
All templates, including catalyst-specific developmental information, must be
listed and damaged. Generic chemistry must not secretly contain the individual's
learned organization. Automatic majority correction remains an inherited chemical
mechanism if used; its catalytic support becomes a produced constituent.

This architecture is a hypothesis, not a proof that those symbols form closure.
Reaction stoichiometry, costs, timescales and achievable fluxes must be solved
before running a full learner. The old E3 error—claiming scarcity where a cheap
conventional path is affordable—must not recur.

## Decisive tests before using stronger autonomy language

1. **Production ledger:** track each newly incorporated controller, catalyst and
   boundary component from external feedstock through paid reactions. Report
   initial endowment and replacements separately. Merely slowing decay is not
   equivalent to reconstruction.
2. **Remove a generative relation:** block W-mediated P repair while preserving
   environmental substrate. Show loss of P precedes the relevant decision
   failure. Do the corresponding tests for P→W, W→C and W→B.
3. **Separate direct dependence from common starvation:** use declared rescue
   clamps on energy and other constituents to identify which effect remains
   after a shared-resource pathway is controlled. A lesion that starves every
   process indiscriminately does not map all proposed closure edges.
4. **Boundary mediation:** puncture actual spatial B components, measure changed
   diffusion and loss of C/W, and test whether internally generated repair
   restores retention. A privileged external wall is an explicit rescue arm.
5. **Timescale test:** trace persistence must span many individual component
   lifetimes, with measured turnover. Show that no-maintenance controls really
   run down over that horizon. Do not equate a static connected graph with
   enabling dependence.
6. **Acquired identity:** paired histories produce different functional routes;
   damage/recovery preserves the history-specific useful organization without
   teacher access. Count effective information, not just storage capacity.
7. **Complete loss:** remove all learned traces and contingent molecular/layout
   copies. Same reset state and same future environment must imply identical
   trajectories regardless of earlier history. Arbitrary lost information must
   not reappear from an unlisted seed, reaction rule or evaluator feedback.
8. **Adaptive needs:** after structural development changes which resource
   sustains a useful mechanism, an online learner must discover the dependence
   from consequences. This is a separate extension beyond supervised AC1 and
   must retain protected-memory and conventional-RL comparisons.
9. **Relinquishment:** make a learned route dispensable through an alternative
   viable route. Test reduction of unnecessary production with sustained useful
   function; do not credit death or wholesale loss of capability as forgetting.
10. **Conventional rivals:** a competent fixed allocator and ordinary learner
    receive equal physical opportunities. A conventional mechanism meeting
    closure criteria is a positive construction, not an embarrassment; algorithmic
    superiority requires its own matched comparison.

## Practical sequencing

First validate a small P↔W production loop with conserved material and finite
reaction rates, preserving AC1's no-template and erasure tests. Then add C and
test uptake/production dependence. Only then add a spatially produced B and
test diffusion mediation. Establish feasibility with controls before selecting
learning parameters, then independently review and freeze a new hypothesis.

Independent conceptual review should challenge whether the chosen model boundary
excludes a hidden enabling mechanism. That review has not been performed in this
session. Nothing in this direction document warrants a full-autopoiesis claim.
