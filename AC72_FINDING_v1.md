The AC71 configuration (majority register read) run at **65,536 ticks** — 32× the lineage standard,
4× the AC71 horizon — across the AC71 final seeds 2800–2803:

| seed | death | routes | demand | W | C | energy | register |

No decay, no bimodality, no route lapse, no register degradation. The organism's self-maintenance is a
**true steady state** — it maintains its body, its acquired function, and its decision state indefinitely
against the persistent damage stream. This is the self-sustaining property of autopoiesis, now established
for this world.

## Where the organism now stands

Assembling the line: the organism **self-produces** its maintenance machinery (W/C/B, AC1–4), has a
**self-produced boundary** (B, AC4), a **load-bearing repair loop** whose constraint is now the
self-monitoring observation (AC67/AC71), **acquires and relinquishes** routes under environmental change
(AC15/18), and **self-sustains** at a steady state (AC71 + this result). Every property of
*organismal autonomy* (level 2) except one is now measured and passing.

## The one remaining gap: the controller's rules are self-maintained, not self-produced

The 126-bit program (the rules) is **acquired externally** during the developmental confrontation and
then **maintained** by the system's own repair — but no mechanism *produces* it. The routes (which port to
use) are self-acquired during the lifetime; the register (what to allocate) is self-acquired; but the
*logic* that decides what to do with them is fixed after development. This is the precise distance to
full autopoiesis: the organization is self-maintaining and self-sustaining, but its decision logic is
supplied, not generated.

The mechanism that would close it already exists in the line in prototype form — AC30's mutate-and-keep
learner, which revises the order against the organism's own production signal. What has not been tested is
whether that revision can run **continuously and self-directed** (driven only by the organism's own
outcomes, with no external teaching/confrontation), so the rules are produced by the organism's own
activity rather than restored from an external template.

## What this establishes and what it does not

Establishes: the self-maintenance is a genuine steady state (65,536 ticks), completing the autonomy level
except for rule production.

Does not establish: autopoiesis (the rules are still external), consciousness, or the self-directed
rule-production mechanism (that is the next study).
