# AC4: a controller-maintained boundary retains its enabling machinery

2026-09-14. All declared engineering conditions retained. Broad goal active.

## New evidence

The acquired, vulnerable controller selects paid replacement of explicit
perimeter constituents. Interior W catalysts enable these replacements; the
perimeter reflects W/C transport, retaining repair and energy-conversion
machinery. Removing boundary production causes constituent loss through actual
spatial transport. There is no boundary-quality death threshold.

All eight self conditions (four seeds repeated at two damage levels) completed
2,048 ticks with 100% final controller and payload accuracy. Every self condition
produced 205–209 boundary constituents, 252–256 W and 31 C, and exported zero
W/C. Initial B/W/C lifetimes are all shorter than the run. Boundary replacement
is ongoing paid production, not persistence of the initial enclosure.

This is a designed model-relative maintenance network. It does not establish
autonomously discovered needs or full autopoiesis.

## Full results

Mean active fraction of planned ticks:

| Copy-flip rate | Self | No B production | External B rescue | External retention rescue | No policy writes | Protected policy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.00005 | 1.000 | 0.207 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| 0.0001 | 1.000 | 0.191 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

Self-minus-no-B activity differences are 79.3 and 80.9 percentage points.
Every no-B run terminated. Those runs exported 25–109 W/C constituents each,
including internally produced replacements. This contrasts with zero exports
in every self run. Both rescue arms restore activity, supporting retention as
an enabling mechanism; no complete quantitative mediation analysis is claimed.

Final mean controller accuracy:

| Rate | Self | B rescue | Retention rescue | No policy writes | Protected |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.00005 | 100% | 100% | 99.27% | 98.97% | 100% |
| 0.0001 | 100% | 99.76% | 93.16% | 92.68% | 100% |

Activity rescue does not imply full organizational rescue. Persistent low B
can prioritize blocked production over repair in the supplied policy. This is
a candidate explanation for retention-rescue information loss, not a measured
causal mediation result. No-B raw policy accuracy is 100% at termination; those
early frozen states are not evidence of continued functioning.

Blocking policy writes did not prevent completion at either rate. Thus this
short AC4 grid establishes an information-maintenance effect but does not prove
that controller repair is necessary for activity over its tested horizon.
AC1–AC3 results cannot silently replace that missing integrated-system contrast.
Longer horizons and new seeds are needed under a separate prospective protocol.

All six declared engineering criteria passed at each rate. These gates do not
include every requirement of the broader autonomy objective. Four seeds and
two repeated environments are a small exploratory sample, not confirmation.

## Mechanism and verification

Four mechanism tests pass: boundary production payment and spatial accessibility;
exterior catalyst inactivity; complete declared-state erasure and protected-policy
rejection; expiry/export accounting and short paired replay.

A separately labeled post-protocol intervention replaces all 16 acquired B
decision rows with REST, leaving reaction laws and immediate input identical.
The original controller produces one B; the intervened controller produces none.
This verifies that B production is selected by mutable acquired information,
not a protected low-boundary override. It does not show autonomous learning.

The same-author audit verifies all 48 rows, frozen source hashes, resource
balances and exact sampled reruns of all 12 seed-zero conditions. Energy includes
living, writes and W/C/B production; fuel is separately conserved. Material
includes free precursor, bound W/C/B, imports, external B, replacements,
expirations, exported particles and overflow. No hidden constituent remains
active after export. Empty slots are capacity, not molecules.

## Supplied structure and remaining requirements

The 5x5 geometry, perfect membrane selectivity, sensing and generic interpreter
are supplied. Memory is anchored to a supplied scaffold. Interior reservoirs
are protected shared stores; W access to its tagged bank is a well-mixed-interior
approximation. W must be spatially near a perimeter site to produce B, but this
is not a fully microscopic reaction-diffusion model. Boundary constituents are
placed by a specified reaction; shape development is not learned or produced.

The policy is a demonstrated table belonging to 24 priority permutations.
Adding a boundary observation enlarged the table but did not create new learned
needs. The next conceptual challenge remains acquisition and adaptation of
maintenance priorities in this maintained architecture, including relinquishing
an unnecessary action when an external substitute changes the dependency.

Before that broader claim, an extended integrated follow-up must test whether
controller repair becomes necessary for sustained activity, retain failed
conditions, and assess whether maintained organization survives beyond the
short current horizon. Do not tune this frozen model silently to get a pass.
