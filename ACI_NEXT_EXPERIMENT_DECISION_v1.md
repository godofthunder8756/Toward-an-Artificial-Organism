## Recommended next node: analytic world-admission discriminator

**Question:** does a temporally distributed two-stream world require retaining
joint first-order predictive information beyond a factorized estimate, and is a
small ordinary joint statistic already sufficient?

This prepares acquired C2/C3 research without assuming a workspace, metacognition,
self-model or neural maintenance. It is NOT a novelty test of Bayesian filtering.
Its value is to admit or reject an interpretable developmental benchmark before
spending on another learner.

### Proposed frozen world

- Hidden state `(X,Y)` comprises two binary EXTERNAL world factors, initially
  uniform over four states. Neither latent truth nor model parameters are future
  agent inputs.
- Each transition draws independent common flip `C~Bernoulli(1/5)`, private
  flips `Dx~Bernoulli(1/50)` and `Dy~Bernoulli(1/40)`, then sets
  `X' = X xor C xor Dx`, `Y' = Y xor C xor Dy`.
- Eight observations alternate X and Y, each through independent BSC(1/5).
  Observe at state t, then transition before the next observation; no transition
  after the last observation before terminal queries.
- Terminal query processes predict X, Y and XOR(X,Y) from the same acquired
  history. Forecast probabilities are FIRST-ORDER world predictions, not
  second-order confidence.
- Evaluator knows the generator; a future learner would have only permitted
  observation/action/outcome experience. This analytic stage has no learner.

All 256 observation prefixes have positive probability. The positive private
flip rates avoid assuming a permanently fixed latent parity. The world is a
proposal; none of its new results has been computed in this package.

### Exact apparatus and rivals

Enumerate all 256 legal length-eight histories with rational forward filtering:

1. Full joint four-state Bayes filter: EXTERNAL oracle.
2. Factorized estimate retaining the SAME exact current marginals, with
   `p(XOR)=pX+pY-2pX*pY`.
3. Marginals plus ONE covariance statistic: ordinary sufficient-state rival,
   reconstructing `p11=pX*pY+cov`. Audit update closure and all forecasts.
4. Current-observation-only policy/filter.
5. Direct-history oracle table; explicitly charge stored entries/precision.

Match observations, timing, query goals and targets. Known-model oracles are not
acquired neural results. A supplied model cannot secretly become the learner's
answer channel. Proper expected Brier score uses the evaluator's latent law only
for evaluation.

### Prospective admission signatures

Before enumeration, freeze this proposed generator, horizon and signatures:

- **History:** current-only estimator has strictly positive exact excess over
  the full-history oracle.
- **Consequential joint information:** XOR Brier excess of the factorized
  marginal projection is at least `1/1000`.
- **Selective content consequence:** zero-covariance substitution, preserving
  marginals/normalization/current observation/query, changes XOR forecast by at
  least `1/20` on at least `1/4` of history probability mass; X/Y forecasts
  remain unchanged.
- **Strong reduction:** marginals plus covariance match the full joint filter's
  forecasts and update law. If this is sufficient, it defeats a requirement for
  exotic integration machinery; that reduction is success, not a defect.
- Verify every intervention stays in the legal probability simplex. Distinguish
  natural reachable history states from off-support clamping diagnostics.

Failure of headroom/history gates rejects this benchmark under this protocol.
Do not adjust noise/horizon after seeing results or compensate by enlarging a
network. Any successor world requires an additive rationale and new human gate.

### Claim ceiling and critical alternatives

At most: this exact world has identifiable first-order history/correlation
consequences and an ordinary sufficient-state realization.

It does NOT prove:

- a universal minimum of three neural coordinates;
- that any nonlinear two-marginal readout cannot encode history via precision;
- exact infinite-system Hankel rank from a finite submatrix;
- learned acquisition, workspace necessity, C4/C5, organizational autonomy,
  cognitive individuality or phenomenal consciousness.

For a stronger marginal-only necessity claim, demand exact reachable aliases or
an explicit finite-precision/stability/control-class proof. An off-support state
clamp alone is not such a proof. If the strongest same-information rival matches
all claimed roles, retain the ordinary mechanism and drop the embellishment.

### Budget and output, if approved

One solver-free rational enumeration of the specified 256 histories, one
independent integer/rational replay, fixed generator and no parameter sweep.
Record number of histories, probability normalization, exact risks, intervention
mass, arithmetic precision/storage and claim scope. No gradient fitting,
neural initialization, organism simulation or scale-up.

Outputs: immutable prospective analytic protocol/manifest, exact world-admission
certificate or failed signatures, strong-rival/update-closure audit, and a
separate decision on whether a learning protocol is now worth designing.

## Human gates after this node

Even if the analytic world is admitted, neural execution still requires a
separately approved prospective protocol and a Q-EXIST justification for its
chosen family. First C4/C5/C6 work, new architecture families, scale-ups,
organism re-entry, ACO changes and gate relaxation remain separate approvals.

The O-axis remains active as a research question, not a simulation permission.
The eventual intersection requires content -> maintaining organization ->
future cognitive machinery -> future content interventions. None of these
arrows is supplied by passing this first-order world-admission test.
