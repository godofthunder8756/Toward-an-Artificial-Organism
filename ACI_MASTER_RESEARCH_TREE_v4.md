The frozen final protocol compared candidate with **all six**
independently fitted R1/R2/R3/R4/R8/R9 rivals at whole-arm parameter
and declared forward-MAC caps. The independent raw/checkpoint audit
passed; the scientific hypothesis failed. The EXTERNAL exact-belief
R5/R7 controls and selector-headroom R6 are not learned wins.
R9's exact candidate clone establishes expressive inclusion, while
the independently trained R9 is the actual empirical comparator.
The distinction is permanent in this lineage.

Do not explain the result through consciousness, global-workspace
necessity, autopoiesis, or intrinsic semantic occupancy. The positive
part of the record is methodological: serious rivals, frozen
resources/statistics, preserved negative findings, and an explicit
separation between a mechanism's use and its architectural necessity.
The continuing architectural default is **the simplest available
content/coding mechanism that demonstrably supports the required
decisions**, not a progressively embellished workspace.
