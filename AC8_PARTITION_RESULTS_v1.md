# AC8 partition: preserving acquired port selectors matters independently

2026-09-15. All32 prospective conditions retained and audited. Primary gate passes.

After acquiring the unknown resource routes, every individual stops learning at
tick2048. The selective ablation then blocks repair of either the four encoded
port-selector bits, the four inherited action-type bits, or all eight. Uniform
corruption, resource costs, physical machinery and all other repairs remain.

| Repair condition | Completions | Mean active fraction | Correct final routes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Keep all repair | 8/8 | 1.000 | 8/8 |
| Block port-selector repair only | 2/8 | .740 | 2/8 |
| Block inherited action-type repair only | 3/8 | .811 | 3/8 |
| Block both | 1/8 | .657 | 1/8 |

Keeping repair improves mean activity over selector-only exclusion by26.01
percentage points and preserves route information. Six selector-blocked
individuals terminate while all inherited action-type bits remain eligible
for repair. The earlier whole-field effect therefore cannot be attributed
solely to inherited opcode maintenance.

Both subsets matter in this encoding. Do not infer that their effects add:
trajectories diverge after mutations, altering resource consumption and later
maintenance. Small differences between all-route and subset stop times are
retained in the raw data, not rounded into an assumed ordering.

## What the result supports

The port choices were not supplied by a routing teacher; they were selected
through physical resource-contact outcomes. Their encoded selector fields must
subsequently be maintained for this controller to keep functioning reliably
over the tested horizon. That maintenance depends on the W/C/B organization
whose resource access those choices enable. This strengthens the project's
specific acquired-organization maintenance claim.

The four physical selector bits encode only two unknown choices. Two individuals
initially guess both choices correctly; six require changed choices. The encoding
uses00 versus11 for each port selection, so single-bit corruption can redirect
execution to another action. This is the real vulnerable representation under
test, not a proof for all possible encodings or an ideal binary selector.

Learning was intentionally stopped to isolate preservation. AC8 separately shows
reacquisition after environmental remapping. Neither experiment establishes
that memory-based access is the only viable strategy: random-port controls in
AC7/AC8 survive. No new metabolic need, richer developmental individuality,
intrinsic normativity or full autopoiesis is demonstrated.

## Verification

Audit checks every exclusion mask against induced single-replica errors,
source snapshot hashes, all32 unique conditions, incremental logs, energy/fuel/
material conservation and four exact seed900 replays. An additional full-horizon
comparison reproduces original AC8 all-route exclusion exactly. Function-global
copies isolate the masks; original modules and frozen sources are not mutated.
These are same-author checks and need independent confirmation for broader claims.
