## 3. What AC116 establishes, and what remains inconclusive

**Frozen finals 6600–6607, 240 rows, the pure occluded-`used_held` world (ε=0, q=0.9).**
Maintained integer counter (3-bit Gray + open-blind hold latch, drop at N) vs the strongest
tuned memoryless rival (one free ambiguity parameter p = P(relinquish | occluded-
unproductive), no latch).

**Established (F1).** At N\*=4 vs p\*=1.0 the counter does NOT demonstrate a significant
post-cause income advantage: per-seed combined income difference `[0, 0, 0, +128, +192,
+192, +512, +576]`, mean **+200/seed** (≈3 contacts), exact sign-flip **p = 0.0625**. The
counter is **weakly dominant** (0 seeds worse, 5 better, 3 tied) but sub-significant. Its
accumulation is causally effective (G3: drops earlier than the read-forced scramble under
move in 12/16, never later; false-relinquishes under cut in 4/16 where scramble holds
0/16). Its open-blind latch buys a real cut-safety edge (4/16 vs 16/16 false
relinquishments) that this world's economics do not price (a false drop merely re-binds
the entry, `life=64`). G1 (world license: byte-identity to frozen AC110 at q=0.5), G2
(no-cause identity), G3 (causal role) pass.

**Remains inconclusive (recorded, not resolved here — carried to M2).** The F1 verdict is
a *comparative usefulness bound in the tested task*, not a demonstration of absence. Two
facts bound it from below: (1) five positive nonzero seed differences and three ties give
the SMALLEST attainable two-sided exact sign-flip p-value of 0.0625 at n=8 — the observed
result is the most positive it could be — so it does NOT establish that retained state is
useless; "storage line closed" / "maintenance not useful" wording over-reaches the result.
(2) Ongoing repair was deliberately out of scope; the no_write acquisition cut (dies 4/16
under move) is a schedule perturbation, not a clean read-content effect (AC109 rule 3).
Not erased by F1: AC107's content-role, AC108's acquisition-necessity, AC110's
repair-dependence (post-window storage), AC113's single-counter sufficiency.

**Resolved by M2 (`AC116_ERRATA_v1.md`, 2026-09-25).** The inference is corrected and the
controls audited; the frozen primary outcome and the p = 0.0625 threshold are preserved.
M2 (a) proves p = 0.0625 is the *smallest attainable* two-sided sign-flip p at n=8, so the
result is weak dominance, not absence — "the storage line is closed" is withdrawn in favour
of "suspended at the resolution floor of the tested world", and "maintenance not useful" is
withdrawn in favour of "ongoing repair not tested"; (b) shows the `scramble` read control is
NOT a clean readout-only intervention (the write target is computed *from* the forced read,
so the scrambled counter never accumulates — it is a causal-role control, not a read-content
cut), and specifies the clean readout-only design (split the read: true value for the update,
zero only the decision); (c) shows the productive-occluded branch is only reachable under
cut/no_cause in ε=0 (a stale entry never yields under move), so the hard reset-to-0 is
lossless there but would discard usable information under ε>0 (needs a graded decrement).

## 5. The exact architecture proposed for the monitor-feasibility harness

**Name: the Estimator-Monitoring Feasibility Harness — the C1 second-order monitor
candidate on the AC110/AC116 world.** (This is the "monitor-feasibility harness"; M3 pins
its single testable target, M6 carries the scientific claims.)

The proposal (S0 §7 = C1 §3) is a frozen runner in the world where the first-order
estimate `e` is **sometimes wrong for implementation reasons** — the AC110 post-window
drift regime and/or the AC67/AC71 sticky-`|=` corruption regime, i.e. the C2-gated +
damaged world with the repair budget active — adding:

1. **A stored, maintained state `m` in a substrate distinct from `e`** (the estimate
   lives at `bel_off`; `m` in a separate dead-rule/recipe-bank locus), majority-read,
   W-gated paid writes.
2. **Content = maintenance bookkeeping, not cause evidence:** obs bit 2 (the corruption
   signal `min(ones, 7-ones).sum() >= 4`), whether a repair write was starved by the
   budget or W=0, and whether reacquisition (`bel_write`) recently fired.
3. **Dissociation:** `m = "unreliable"` while `e` correct (false alarm) and `m =
   "reliable"` while `e` corrupted (miss) — the `meta-d′ ≠ d′` signature, impossible in a
   single-substrate ideal model.
4. **Causal role on the maintenance direction:** scramble/cut `m` changes repair /
   renewal / reacquisition while the first-order relinquish-vs-hold decision still works;
   scramble/cut `e` changes the decision.
5. **Rivals (not strawmen), all implemented:** (i) state-blind fixed maintenance duty;
   (ii) the raw obs-bit-2 reflex (no stored `m` — the existing AC9/AC110 watchdog/null);
   (iii) an ε-estimator arm.
6. **Damage model that actually flips replicas within the window:** the AC67 option-2
   sticky-`|=` regime, with the rate and the read threshold stated together (AC14/AC69:
   align the READ convention with the REPAIR trigger). Gate on maintenance behaviour, not
   survival (survival reported as a bimodality-aware lower bound).

**Falsification (prespecified):** `m` is falsified if scrambling/cutting it leaves
maintenance behaviour unchanged while the first-order decision still works (the organism
uses the raw reflex, not a stored monitor), or if a state-blind fixed policy reproduces
`m`'s maintenance behaviour (content inert — AC109 one level up). Both close the
reliability tier for real.

This is a **representation study, not a repair study** and not a further storage
comparison (AC116 F1 is not a license for more storage runs); the one load-bearing caution
is retained: estimating ε alone does not establish metacognition.
