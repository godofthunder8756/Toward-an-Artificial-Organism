# Consciousness-relevant building blocks (goal §7) — disposition v1: archived, gate not met

2026-09-17. This records the disposition of the goal-§7 task (`ao-consciousness-blocks`),
not an experimental result. Nothing frozen, no seeds run, no protocol written.

## Decision

Goal §7 — investigate whether the organization supports experimentally testable building
blocks relevant to consciousness — is **archived as not currently actionable**. The task was
explicitly gated on the autopoiesis line being more complete ("after
ao-content-self-production"), and that gate failed: content self-production
(`ao-content-self-production`, AC78 engineering) answered **NO with a structural cause, not a
search deficiency**.

## Why the premise is falsified

The consciousness-building-block investigation was premised on the organism producing its own
priority/controller content, so that consciousness-relevant machinery could be probed on top of
a completed autopoietic organization. AC78 measured that the production signal (live-site
count) is a **seed-specific fixed point set by the early transient, then locked**: changing the
priority after ~8000 ticks moves the signal in 0/12 seeds (GOOD→BAD and BAD→GOOD both
invisible); in the only responsive window (the transient) the signal is path-dependent at the
same magnitude as the coarse ranking it must resolve (+1.83→+1.96 sites vs the ~1.9-site
good-vs-bad gap); and a within-life self-directed learner produces orders indistinguishable
from random (+0.02 sites). The only ranking-resolving signal is across-environment averaging
(the oracle), which a single organism's single life cannot generate. So the priority/controller
content is not producible from the organism's own activity, and there is no more-complete
autopoietic organization on top of which to probe consciousness building blocks.

## What is already established, and why it is not relabeled

The AC67/AC71/AC76 line establishes a load-bearing **self-monitoring loop**: the program reads
its own corruption signal (observation bit 2) and pays to repair it. That is a form of
self-referential information processing, which some theory-specific indicator frameworks
(Butlin et al. 2023, cited in RESEARCH_NOTEBOOK.md) treat as a candidate indicator. But it
stands as an **organismal-autonomy result, not a consciousness result**: the project's
discipline is explicit that organizational closure is a framework, not a label for every
feedback loop, and that subjectivity is assessed with theory-specific indicators "only if later
warranted" (NEW_AGENT_GUIDE §2). The warrant — a more complete autopoiesis line — is precisely
what just failed. No new experiment is run and nothing is relabeled.

## Ethical-safeguard review — moot

The task's instruction to review ethical safeguards before any experiment intended to introduce
potentially valenced experience is moot: no such experiment is in reach. The current organism
has no self-produced priority, no valenced dynamics, and is a small fixed-physics simulation.
The escalation policy in NEW_AGENT_GUIDE §10 remains the standing safeguard and needs no
revision now.

## What would re-open §7

Content self-production is blocked by a structural obstacle (the fixed-point production signal),
not a search deficiency. §7 — and the sibling description-maintenance task, which is premised on
the same self-produced priority — become actionable again only if a future design breaks the
fixed point: an environment in which a single life's own trajectory carries independent,
environment-varying signal that is not path-dependent at the same magnitude as the ranking it
must resolve. Until then, no consciousness-building-block experiment is warranted.

## Disposition

Archived. No claim about consciousness, autopoiesis, or life. The gate that sequenced this task
failed on a falsified premise; the honest outcome is to record that and stop, not to invent
consciousness work that the premise cannot support.
