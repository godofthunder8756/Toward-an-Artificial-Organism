# ACI master research tree v2 — the seven-phase program, advanced past the Phase-II verdict

2026-09-26. Deliverable of the N14 card (t_8b4303fa): *what does the Phase-II
verdict imply for the master research tree and the architecture-by-claim matrix?*
This revision **supersedes** `ACI_MASTER_RESEARCH_TREE_v1.md` (the Q10 planning
deliverable) while preserving it unchanged as the record. It advances exactly one
thing: Phase II is no longer "designed; frozen protocol" — it has been **executed,
frozen, audited, and adjudicated** — and that verdict re-scopes Phases III–V and
the principles they inherit. It prepares Phase III; it does **not** execute it.

**What changed from v1 (the whole delta).** Phase II ran (`N11` finals,
`N12` audit, `N13` verdict) and returned **OUTCOME B (primary) + D
(allocation-specific), jointly**. The explicit maintained self-state V was shown
unnecessary (the strongest same-information rival matches the candidate), and the
learned adaptive allocation was reproduced and exceeded by a hand-coded reactive
threshold rule — so the "maintained self-state / endogenous allocation"
architectural principles failed at the neural level and are revised in §4.3 before
any further experiment is designed. Exactly one narrow claim survives: **N1 active
paid persistence as a necessary-condition/substrate fact** (§4.2), not unique to the
candidate. Phase III is therefore prepared around the surviving content —
"recurrent cross-module availability of a maintained representation" (N2) — not
executed, and not designed until the revised principles are absorbed. The organism
program is **not** reopened: the verdict confirms organism-derived demotions
(D5 "the optimum is a level, not a switch"; the AC109 "same information, mechanism
must do causal work" rule) rather than invalidating any promoted organism principle.

This is a **planning document**. It runs nothing, trains nothing, freezes nothing,
and edits no frozen artifact (runner, protocol, results dir, hash, or ledger). It
is not hashed into any study's `pre_run_snapshot.json`.

**Reading discipline applied throughout.** (1) The level-(d)/(e) boundary
(`DEFINITIONS_CHARTER_v1.md` §2) is absolute: no phase in this tree is, or is meant
as, a path to a consciousness claim. (2) Seeds are the replication unit (N seeds ×
2 histories = N independent units); survival/viability is a bimodality-aware lower
bound (AC68), never a per-seed-family guarantee. (3) Every positive claim is
credited only if the rival that omits it provably fails (P6), gates are prespecified
and never moved after a result (AC16), and composition is licensed by byte-identity
(`state_hash`), never prose. (4) The program's collapse record
(`ACI_MISSION_AUDIT_v1.md` §2.6) is the standing falsification set; the bridge added
one more entry to it (§4.3). (5) Phases are ordered by dependency, not ambition.

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The ACI program remains **seven phases, arranged as a single dependency chain from
the completed organism foundations to one integrated candidate** — but it now has
one *measured* node, not just a designed one. Phase I (organism foundations) is
complete and exited. Phase II (neural self-maintenance) is **complete and closed
with verdict B + D**: the bridge's decisive experiment ran, froze, survived a 14/14
independent audit, and returned the N6/N7 identifiability collapse confirmed
empirically — explicit maintained V is unnecessary (the strongest same-information
rival P_rb matches the candidate, p = 0.64) and fixed/reactive allocation suffices
(a 3-line (s,E,d) threshold rule attains the oracle, above the candidate). The one
load-bearing survivor is **N1 active paid persistence** — future-task information
depends on a representation whose retention is paid per tick and is unrecoverable
without it (0.978 vs 0.000, p = 2/2^12 = 0.00049) — a necessary-condition/substrate
fact, not a learned-behaviour fact, and not unique to the candidate. The
architectural principles that failed (explicit maintained self-state; endogenous
adaptive allocation) are revised (§4.3) before any further experiment is designed.
Phase III (N2 — recurrent cross-module availability of a maintained representation)
is **prepared, not executed**: it is re-scoped onto the content that survived, and
its design is gated on the revised principles. Phases IV–VII are unchanged as
designs. No autopoiesis claim and no consciousness claim is made anywhere.

## 4. PHASE II — Neural self-maintenance (EXECUTED; verdict B + D)

### 4.1 What ran

The frozen bridge protocol (v2 design, v3/v4 corrections; `ACI_BRIDGE_PROTOCOL_v3.md`
/ `_v4.md`, `TBRIDGE_STATISTICAL_PLAN_v2.md`) was implemented (`N9`), engineering-
validated (`N10`), run on the 12 final seeds 2000–2011 disjoint from engineering
(`N11`, 192 rows = 16 arms × 12 seeds, 48 checkpoints), independently audited
(`N12`, 14/14 PASS), and adjudicated (`N13`). The scaffold realizes the **δ-decay
observed-integrity** world, where integrity `I_t = f(d_t)` is a readable age
counter, **not** an inferred latent — the central inferred-integrity question is a
documented STOP (N6/N6b), and this experiment claims nothing about it.

### 4.2 The verdict, and the one surviving claim

**OUTCOME B (primary) + D (allocation-specific), jointly** (`BRIDGE_PHASE2_VERDICT_v1.md`):

- **B — internal information useful, explicit V unnecessary.** The strongest
  same-information rival — the recurrent no-V-slot direct policy P_rb (arm 10) —
  **matches** the candidate (op 0.823 vs 0.895, exact sign-flip p = 0.64, 5/12
  seeds positive). V's integrity bit is a learned re-encoding of the readable age
  `d_t`; its discrete, paid, maintained self-state carries no information the raw
  bookkeeping lacks. **The architectural claim that explicit maintained V is
  causally load-bearing for allocation is NOT SUPPORTED.**
- **D — fixed/reactive allocation suffices; N3 not supported.** A 3-line
  sufficient-statistic threshold rule (`refresh iff s=stable and E>=E_crit and
  d>=L-1`, arm 9_d) attains the oracle (op 1.000) in every seed, **above** the
  candidate (0.895). The learned adaptive allocation is reproduced by a hand-coded
  reactive rule on the raw observable `(s, E, d)`. **N3 (internal-state-driven
  nontrivial allocation) is NOT SUPPORTED.**

**The one surviving claim (N1 — active paid persistence, necessary condition):**
future-task information depends on a representation whose retention is paid per
tick out of a limited budget, and is unrecoverable without it. Candidate stable
slot survival 0.978 vs no_maintenance / free_memory 0.000; exact sign-flip
p = 2/2^12 = 0.00049 (floor); 12/12 seeds; force-hold → probe 1.0, force-drop →
chance. **This is a substrate + architecture fact, not a learned-behaviour fact,
and it is NOT unique to the candidate** (state-blind fixed schedules and the
reward-only arm also hold the cue through paid refresh — N12 finding F2).

**Not A** (allocation not distinctive; integrity observed, not inferred), **not C**
(free-permanence holds — recurrence does not carry the cue), **not E** (N1 is a
substrate fact, not an objective reduction), **not F** (ran and passed 14/14 audit).

**Claim ceiling:** no autopoiesis, subjectivity, or consciousness claim is made,
implied, or supported. This is a level-(b)/(c) representational result, and its
scope is bounded to the δ-decay observed-integrity world; the inferred-integrity
question remains a documented, unsettled STOP.

### 4.3 The architectural principles that failed, revised (the D-clause obligation)

Two candidate principles were falsified **at the neural level** and are now
demoted before Phase III is designed. They join D1–D8 (the organism-derived
demotions are confirmed, not overturned, by the bridge):

- **D9 — "an explicit maintained self-state (V) is necessary for allocation" —
  DEMOTED.** The bridge's N3c contrast is the decisive test and it failed: the
  same-information rival P_rb (raw bookkeeping + history, trained, no V slot)
  matches the candidate, and the sufficient-statistic arm exceeds it. Where the
  current observation plus ordinary recurrence carries the load, a discrete
  paid-maintained self-state adds no demonstrated causal work. Carry-forward:
  design for *information* (what must be held), not for a *self-state slot* as an
  end in itself; add a maintained state only where a rival without it provably
  fails (P6/D2, now re-confirmed on the neural substrate).
- **D10 — "the optimum is a learned adaptive allocation" — DEMOTED (again, at the
  neural level).** The bridge's N3b/N3c show the learned allocator is reproduced
  and exceeded by a reactive threshold rule on `(s, E, d)`. This is the neural
  restatement of organism demotion D5 (AC11: the optimum is a level, not a switch)
  and the AC15 design rule (sweep the state-blind family before claiming a switch).
  Carry-forward: before any "endogenous allocation" claim, sweep both the reactive
  family *and* the learner's own parameters; if any state-blind/reactive rule
  matches or beats the learner, no acquired decision is demonstrated.

**Consequence for the principles doc** (`ACI_ARCHITECTURAL_PRINCIPLES_v1.md`): the
promoted principles P1–P7 stand, but their "neural implementation candidate"
fields must drop the *explicit V slot* as a load-bearing assumption. P1 (cost) and
P2 (internalized) survive as the substrate of N1; P3 (content selector) and P4
(allocation interaction) survive **without** the premise that the allocation must
be a learned, self-state-driven adaptive policy — the bridge shows it may be a
reactive rule on the observable. N1's substrate fact is the load-bearing carry;
N3's "endogenous adaptive allocation" reading is withdrawn for this scaffold.

### 4.4 What success did NOT imply (unchanged, re-stated)

Phase II did not establish "the agent is self-maintaining" unqualified, "the agent
learned to want to preserve its memory", "agency/autonomy" unqualified, or any
consciousness claim. It earned, at most, N1 as a necessary-condition fact in the
observed-integrity world — nothing stronger.

## 5. PHASE III — Recurrent global integration (PREPARED, NOT EXECUTED)

**Status: prepared, not executed.** Per the card's instruction under a simplified
B, Phase III is prepared around **"recurrent cross-module availability of a
maintained representation"** — the N2 property that survived the bridge. It is
**not** dispatched and **not** protocol-frozen: its design is gated on the revised
principles of §4.3 (no explicit self-state slot by assumption; allocation as a
reactive rule unless a switch is demonstrated).

- **Core hypothesis (N2).** One maintained representation's content is available
  to at least two objective-distinct downstream modules that need different
  information and drive different behaviour, and changing the content changes them
  *differently*. The load-bearing fact is the shared, flexibly-consumed content —
  which is exactly what survives Phase II: the *content* is load-bearing (bridge
  N1), while the *explicit self-state and adaptive allocation* that Phase II hoped
  would carry it did not.
- **Decisive experiment.** Benchmark task T3 (cross-module information
  availability): the maintained content W drives S_pol (channel choice) and S_reg
  (relinquish-vs-maintain) differently; intervention I2 scrambles W only, holding
  sensors, readouts, and both specialists' other inputs fixed. **Design change
  from v1:** the maintained W that Phase III builds on is the *paid-persistent
  cue/substrate* established by N1, not the explicit-V self-state that Phase II
  demoted. The two-specialist consumption is the new content of N2.
- **Principal rival explanation.** (i) the two-independent-modules rival (private
  state, no shared W); (ii) the single-objective collapse (one reward-maximizing
  policy with two heads). Both must fail the differential-scramble test. The
  AC109 companion check still applies: where the current observation is decisive,
  a sufficient-statistic rival must match at equal-or-lower cost — which *bounds*
  (does not falsify) the persistence claim, exactly as the bridge's N3c bounded it.
- **Required predecessor.** Phase II (N1 established; the paid-persistent W is the
  substrate a second specialist is added to).
- **Falsification condition.** N2 fails if the two behaviours always co-vary (one
  consumer), if a rival that omits the shared content reproduces the differential
  consumption, or if the single-objective collapse passes the differential-scramble
  test.
- **Approximate compute requirements.** Toy-scale (unmeasured): the bridge
  architecture plus one specialist head, thousands of parameters, single GPU.
- **What success does NOT imply.** "Global broadcast"; "a workspace seat" (O3/GWT);
  "a unified conscious field." A pass earns at most "one content value is flexibly
  consumed by two objective-distinct modules — N2 at the stated degree."

**The gate before Phase III is designed:** the principles doc must first absorb
D9/D10 (§4.3) — the Phase III architecture must not smuggle back an explicit
self-state slot or a learned adaptive allocator as assumptions. Phase III is
prepared; it is not the next executable card until that revision lands.
