## What the earlier verdicts require

- [Phase II](BRIDGE_PHASE2_VERDICT_v1.md): paid cue persistence was real under
  the imposed law, but integrity was readable from age. A fixed threshold
  reached the oracle; a learned explicit integrity register added no
  demonstrated allocation value. Hidden stochastic damage must not be replaced
  by another readable countdown.
- [Phase III](PHASE3_VERDICT_v1.md): shared content supported coordination,
  but ordinary sufficient-statistic broadcast reproduced it without the learned,
  paid workspace. A lesion establishes use, not candidate-exclusive necessity.
- [Phase III-B](PHASE3B_VERDICT_v1.md): verdict **B** stays unchanged. Only
  1/16 final seeds passed the strict best-rival effect gate, versus 14 required.
  The winning mean learned family was unrestricted R9; weak specialist
  competence constrained interpretation.
- [E3](E3_DESIGN.md): restoring conductance while the host retains clean learned
  weights is not maintenance of acquired information. Every surviving copy,
  replay target, optimizer state and inherited rule needs an explicit boundary.
- [Cascade diagnostic](CASCADE_FINDING_v1.md): a measured timing race, not
  simply insufficient capacity, caused route loss in that one diagnostic.
  Raising capacity only delayed it. This motivates explicit event ordering and
  separate terminal skill and survival reporting, not a universal neural law.

These are findings within their original scopes, not evidence that maintenance
is impossible. Conversely, none justifies restoring a failed architectural
claim under a new name.

## First-session disposition

The [draft protocol](SUB_A_PROTOCOL_v1.md) admits only a cheap diagnostic
candidate: live redundant traces with consolidation on every tick. It is
intentionally attacked by ordinary error correction and an external schedule.
The [analytic check](SUB_A_ANALYTIC_CHECK_v1.md) establishes that the external
schedule and paired yoke reproduce this candidate exactly. The primitive
consensus calculation also remains host-supplied: making a gain vulnerable is
not making the entire repair computation vulnerable.

**STOP: this candidate cannot earn comparative H1 or the stipulated repair-
machinery closure.** This is a design-level reduction, not an empirical failure
of trained neural substrates or a whole-family impossibility result. The
[skeleton](sub_a_v1/skeleton.py) is a transparent test fixture exposing these
boundaries, not a compliant neural organism or an implementation of E3.
No pretrained model, optimizer, lifetime engineering experiment, GPU run,
protocol freeze or final run is included.

The next human decision is whether to narrow Phase A to a causal information-
maintenance feasibility question, explicitly allowing ordinary error correction
as its explanation, or redesign a genuinely discriminating repair mechanism
