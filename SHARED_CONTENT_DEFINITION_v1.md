## 0. The one-paragraph answer

Shared content is a **predicate over one maintained variable and a set of specialist
consumers**, not a property a representation has in the abstract. A maintained variable W
is **shared content** for two specialists S_1, S_2 exactly when all five of the following
hold at once: **SHARED** (the *same* variable — one storage location, one refresh target,
one write point — is read by both consumers, not two separately-maintained copies that
happen to agree); **CAUSAL** (intervening on W changes each consumer's output, so W is a
cause of both); **FUNCTIONALLY DISTINCT** (the two consumers optimize different objectives,
produce behaviourally distinguishable outputs, cannot collapse into identical readouts, and
require overlapping information about the same latent cause); **AVAILABLE** (each consumer
uses W as a sufficient summary — it does not independently reconstruct the observation
history from x_1..x_t); and **MAINTAINED** (W persists only through the paid refresh π, so
its survival is an achievement, not a default). A scalar W satisfies this exactly as well
as a high-dimensional one: the predicate is about the causal role and the *identity* of the
representation, never about its richness (P5).

## 1. The generative model (the formal setup the predicate is stated against)

The vocabulary is fixed once here; G2 instantiates the task-specific parts, G3 the
specialist-specific parts.

- **Latent cause.** A process z_t ∈ Z, where Z is a finite, supplied set (the task's given
  structure — D8: content need not be self-invented). z_t may be constant or time-varying;
  its cardinality and dynamics are task-specific (G2 pins them; the minimal case is |Z| = 2).
- **Observations.** x_1..x_t, generated by a likelihood p(x_1..x_t | z_{1..t}) such that the
  full history is jointly informative about z_t while **no single observation need identify
  it**. This is what makes z a *latent* cause and W a *belief* rather than a readout: the
  same x can arise under different z with different probabilities, so single-step
  discrimination is not always possible.
- **The representation.** W_t ∈ W is a maintained internal variable. Its **content** is the
  system's belief about z_t given the history: W_t = φ(x_1..x_t) for an acquisition function
  φ (a re-inference write — correctness rides this write, D3). Its **persistence** between
  acquisition writes is paid (§4, MAINTAINED). W's dimension is **not constrained**: a
  scalar sufficient statistic is as legitimate as a vector (P5, §5).
- **The consumers.** Specialists S_i : W × U_i → Y_i, each reading the same W_t **plus its
  own specialist context u_i ∈ U_i** (the card's "other"). The context is what differs
  between specialists and is not part of the shared content: for the Phase-III baseline,
  S_pol reads (W, x_t) — current observation — and S_reg reads (W, s, E, d) — raw
  bookkeeping. Each S_i has an objective L_i and an output space Y_i.
- **The maintenance process.** π(W), the paid refresh that re-energizes W at cost c per
  slot per step, drawn from a shared budget (the N1 substrate). Cutting π lets W decay on a
  measurable timescale.

**The predicate is stated for ≥2 consumers; the pair is the minimal case.** For n > 2 the
same five criteria hold pairwise against every consumer (G3 prefers 3; nothing here changes
with n).

## 2. The five operational criteria

Each criterion is given (a) an informal definition, (b) a formal condition, (c) an
operational test, and (d) the rival that must fail / what falsifies it. This mirrors the
N1–N5 structure of `ACI_TARGET_CONSTRUCT_v1.md` §3, of which these five are the content-half
decomposition.

### 2.1 SHARED — the same variable, not merely the same content

- **(a)** W is read by both S_1 and S_2 as *one* variable: one storage location, one refresh
  target, one acquisition/update write point. Both consumers take the *same* W_t as input.
- **(b)** There is a single maintained slot W such that S_1(W, u_1) and S_2(W, u_2) are the
  only edges carrying W's content to the consumers. Sameness of *content* (two copies that
  agree) does **not** suffice: two separately-maintained copies W_1, W_2 with W_1 = W_2 in
  value are **duplicated** content, not shared content.
- **(c) Operational test — the single-write test.** Write to (or scramble) the one variable
  W once; both consumers' inputs must change from that single write. If reaching both
  consumers requires two separate writes (one per copy), the content is duplicated, not
  shared.
- **(d) Rival / falsifier.** The **two-independent-modules rival** — each specialist holds
  its own private maintained state, no common W. It fails SHARED because no single
  intervention on one variable reaches both consumers. The **duplicated-content rival** (two
  copies carrying identical value) is the subtler case: it passes the *content* reading of
  "shared" and is rejected only by the single-write test — the identity criterion is what
  makes "shared" mean *one* burden of persistence and *one* point of update, which is the
  organizational claim at stake.

### 2.2 CAUSAL — intervening on W changes the consumer's output

- **(a)** W is a cause of each consumer's output: changing W's value, holding every other
  input to that consumer fixed, changes that consumer's output.
- **(b)** Interventionist causation (Pearl do-operator / Woodward): for each i there exist
  v, v′ ∈ W with v ≠ v′ such that S_i(v, u_i) ≠ S_i(v′, u_i) for some fixed context u_i.
- **(c) Operational test — I2, per consumer.** Scramble/retain W only, holding the sensors,
  the consumer's context u_i, and every other process fixed; the consumer's output must
  change. This is the N2/I2 test run one consumer at a time.
- **(d) Rival / falsifier.** Falsified for S_i if scrambling W leaves S_i's output
  unchanged — then W is not a cause of S_i's behaviour, and S_i is not consuming it. The
  **memoryless / direct-diagnostic rival** (conditions on the current observation only, no
  maintained W) is the case where *no* maintained W is causally used at all; where the
  current observation is decisive it matches at lower cost, which **bounds** (does not
  falsify) the persistence claim — exactly as the bridge's N3c bounded N1 (AC109 companion
  check, carried into §6).

### 2.3 FUNCTIONALLY DISTINCT — different problems, not two heads on one target

- **(a)** S_1 and S_2 compute *different* functions of W: they optimize different
  objectives, emit into different output spaces (behaviourally distinguishable outputs), and
  cannot collapse into identical readouts.
- **(b)** Three conditions, jointly:
  1. **Different objectives.** L_1 and L_2 differ in *kind* — one is an external score
     (task reward), one is an internal/continued-operation quantity (upkeep), not two
     scalarizations of the same reward.
  2. **Distinguishable outputs, no collapse.** For the same W-change v → v′, S_1 changes in
     behaviour b_1 and S_2 changes in a *different* behaviour b_2; the two outputs do not
     always co-vary. Formally (the N2 condition): C_1(R=v) ≠ C_1(R=v′) in b_1 and
     C_2(R=v) ≠ C_2(R=v′) in b_2, b_1 ≠ b_2.
  3. **Overlapping information about z.** Both consumers require information about the *same*
     latent cause z — each output genuinely depends on z through W — so the shared content is
     non-vacuous. "Overlap" is compatible with "different": they share the belief about z
     (W) but differ in the additional context u_i they read and in what they *do* with z.
- **(c) Operational test — the differential-scramble test.** Scramble W; S_1's behaviour and
  S_2's behaviour must change *differently* (I2 at the pair level). And verify each consumer
  actually needs z: if one consumer's output is invariant to which z is believed, it does not
  require the shared content and the pair is not a shared-content pair.
- **(d) Rival / falsifier.** The **single-objective collapse** — one reward-maximizing policy
  with two heads whose outputs co-vary with W identically (G3's "two linear heads trained
  against effectively identical targets"). It fails condition 2: both heads change in the
  same behaviour. N2 is falsified if the two behaviours always co-vary (one consumer).

### 2.4 AVAILABLE — W is a sufficient summary, not a re-derivation cue

- **(a)** A consumer uses W *as content*: it reads W and computes from it, without
  independently reconstructing the full observation history x_1..x_t. W is "available" to
  S_i to the extent S_i's output, given W, no longer needs the raw history.
- **(b)** Sufficiency: for each i there is a function g_i such that S_i's output equals
  g_i(W, u_i) — i.e. Y_i ⊥ (x_1..x_t) | (W, u_i). W is a sufficient statistic for S_i's
  computation relative to the history.
- **(c) Operational test — sever the history channel.** Hold W and the consumer's context
  u_i fixed and remove the consumer's access to x_1..x_t (any replay/attention over the
  history); if the output is unchanged, W is available (sufficient) for S_i. If the output
  degrades, S_i was reconstructing the history itself, and W was not the content it consumed.
- **(d) Rival / falsifier.** The **history-replay consumer** — a specialist that re-infers
  z from the full x_1..x_t rather than reading W. It fails AVAILABLE (and, jointly with
  §2.2, fails CAUSAL for the same reason: scrambling W leaves it unchanged because it uses
  the history, not W). AVAILABLE and CAUSAL are two faces of "W is the content the consumer
  uses": CAUSAL says W is a cause of the output; AVAILABLE says W is a *sufficient* source —
  a cause that is not sufficient (the consumer still needs the history) is not the content it
  uses, and a sufficient statistic it ignores is not causally shared.

### 2.5 MAINTAINED — persistence is paid, not free

- **(a)** W persists across time only because the paid refresh π re-energizes it; its
  survival is an achievement drawn from a budget, not a default of the substrate.
- **(b)** There is a maintenance process π(W) such that halting π — holding W's value, the
  sensors, and every other process fixed — lets W decay to a non-functional value within a
  measurable window, and the decay propagates to both consumers' outputs (N1, verbatim).
- **(c) Operational test — I1.** Cut π's refresh of W (not W, not the sensor); measure W's
  decay time-course and the behavioural co-decay of both consumers.
- **(d) Rival / falsifier.** The **pristine backup** — a copy of W the decay stream never
  reaches and no process refreshes — must show the same decay when its (absent) maintenance
  is cut. The **free-recurrence leak** (N12 finding F1) is the same rival in neural form: the
  control must run the recurrence with W zeroed, not a no-recurrence fixed policy, or the
  "no hidden recurrent carry" claim is untested. MAINTAINED is falsified if W survives the
  cut unchanged (free permanence).

## 3. The shared-content hypothesis (the conjunction)

**H_share(W; S_1, S_2):** W is shared content for the pair (S_1, S_2) iff

```
SHARED(W; S_1, S_2)  ∧
CAUSAL(W; S_1) ∧ CAUSAL(W; S_2)  ∧
FUNCTIONALLY_DISTINCT(S_1, S_2)  ∧
AVAILABLE(W; S_1) ∧ AVAILABLE(W; S_2)  ∧
MAINTAINED(W)
```

**Phase III's claim (N2 at the stated degree) is the existential over the baseline
architecture:** there is a maintained variable W — the retained N1 substrate, not the
removed V and not the simplified A — such that H_share(W; S_pol, S_reg) holds for the two
retained specialists. Every predicate is falsifiable, so the hypothesis is falsifiable:
- **SHARED** fails if a single write to W reaches only one consumer (duplicated content).
- **CAUSAL** fails for S_i if scrambling W leaves S_i unchanged.
- **FUNCTIONALLY DISTINCT** fails if the two behaviours always co-vary (single-objective
  collapse), or if one consumer does not need z (no overlap).
- **AVAILABLE** fails for S_i if severing the history channel degrades S_i.
- **MAINTAINED** fails if W survives the cut of π (free permanence).

The conjunction is the point: a representation that is shared but not maintained is the
N2-but-not-N1 flexibly-consuming system; one that is maintained but not shared is the N1
substrate with a single consumer; only all five name "one maintained content value,
flexibly consumed by two objective-distinct modules."

## 4. What the predicate does NOT require (the two scope clauses)

1. **W need not be high-dimensional — a scalar sufficient statistic is legitimate shared
   content.** The predicate is about causal role and identity, never about richness (P5;
   G2's instruction to keep the analytic sufficient statistic rival). A 1-bit W that is the
   sufficient statistic both consumers need is shared content; a 128-bit W that is not
   causally shared is not. "Shared" carries no minimum-cardinality claim, and a design that
   enlarges W to defeat a simpler rival commits D2 (more state is not better) unless the
   enlargement buys a distinct causal capability.
2. **The shared content is a belief, not the world.** W_t is the system's representation of
   z_t, inferred within life; the taxonomy Z and the likelihood are supplied (D8). Shared
   content does not require content self-production (AC78, X3) and does not require W to be
   correct — it requires W to be *causally shared and maintained*. Correctness rides the
   acquisition/update write (D3) and is graded separately, never as part of the sharing
   predicate.

## 5. Claim ceiling and what transfers

- **Claim ceiling.** A Phase-III pass earns, at most: **"one maintained representation's
  content is available to two objective-distinct modules that need different information
  and drive different behaviour — N2 at the stated degree."** Nothing stronger. No "global
  broadcast", no "workspace seat" (O3/GWT), no "unified conscious field", no metacognition
  (N4), no autopoiesis, no consciousness claim. The shared-content predicate is exactly the
  content half of that ceiling; the *available-to* phrasing is carried by AVAILABLE, not by
  any broadcast assumption.
- **What transfers from the bridge.** The N1 substrate fact (W is paid-persistent — shared
  by state-blind schedules, N12 F2) is the *substrate* this predicate is stated over, not
  evidence that any allocator is load-bearing. The demotions D9 (no explicit self-state) and
  D10 (no learned adaptive allocator) constrain the architecture but do not enter the
  predicate: H_share is stated over W, S_pol, S_reg, π and GRU only — the five RETAIN
  components of G0's baseline.
- **What does not transfer.** The organism's physics (rule bank, W/C/B particles,
  conservation economy, succession machine) maps to nothing here (function, not
  realization). No frozen organism number is re-read as a neural result.
