# ACI theory-indicator matrix v1 — how the six consciousness-theory families diverge on architectural properties

2026-09-25. Deliverable for the Q5 card (t_f62d7349): *how do the
consciousness-theory families make overlapping vs divergent predictions about
architectural properties?* Category E — **theory discrimination; design only, do
not build.**

This is a **discrimination document**. It runs nothing, trains nothing, freezes
nothing, and edits no frozen artifact (runner, protocol, results dir, hash, ledger).
It is not hashed into any study's `pre_run_snapshot.json`. It turns the theory
comparison of `ACI_TARGET_CONSTRUCT_v1.md` §10 into a two-dimensional matrix — rows
are architectural properties / measurable phenomena, columns are the six theory
families — and reduces that matrix to the **smallest set of experiments that
discriminates** the families, without re-architecting the neutral core to accumulate
checkmarks.

**Reading discipline applied throughout.** (1) The level-(d)/(e) boundary is absolute
(`DEFINITIONS_CHARTER_v1.md` §2): this document makes no consciousness claim, adopts no
theory, and gives phenomenal consciousness no operational definition. The strongest
wording any *built* realization earns is unchanged — "meets the ACO definition
(N1–N5, at the stated degree)" / "meets candidate indicator X at degree Y." (2) The
matrix is **derived from the theories**, not from any measurement: a cell says what a
family *predicts*, and a prediction is not evidence. (3) The six families and the
property vocabulary are read from `ACI_TARGET_CONSTRUCT_v1.md` (Q1) §3–§10 and are not
re-derived here; the architecture the experiments run on is
`ACI_MINIMAL_NEURAL_ARCHITECTURE_v1.md` (Q4). (4) The card's two prohibitions are
load-bearing: **do not manipulate the architecture merely to accumulate checkmarks**, and
**find the smallest discriminating set**, not the most complete one.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The six families agree on **one thing and split on four**. The consensus floor is
**recurrence over content** — actively sustained, flexibly consumed representations
(N1+N2); every functionalist family (RPT, GWT, HOT, PP/SMT, AE) requires it, and only
IIT is indifferent to it. On top of that floor the families make **four independent,
opposite-direction predictions**, each isolating one family's distinctive seat: HOT
predicts a distinct higher-order representation is necessary (N4), GWT predicts a global
availability bottleneck is necessary (O3), PP/SMT predicts generative predictive content
is the content of the representation (O2), and AE predicts agency/endogenous regulation
is a necessary enabling condition (N3/N5/M2). IIT is the fifth camp and the principled
outlier: it predicts consciousness is an intrinsic, behaviour-independent quantity Φ and
denies every functional seat — a divergence that is **not experimentally decidable at
minimal scale** because Φ is intractable. Because the four functionalist splits are
independent, the smallest discriminating set is **four contrast experiments** (D1–D4),
one per split; RPT is the residual null that survives if none of the four theory-specific
seats turns out to be load-bearing. IIT is a standing "not-yet-testable" caveat, not a
fifth experiment.

## 1. The six columns (theory families, from Q1 §10)

The columns are the six families Q1 compared and did **not** adopt. Each is stated here
by its seat only; the full claims, intersections, and disagreements are Q1 §10 and are
not reproduced.

| Column | Family | Seat of the claim (one line) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RPT | Recurrent processing (Lamme) | local recurrent processing — reverberating activity — is the minimal condition |
| GWT | Global workspace / availability (Baars; Dehaene & Changeux) | a limited-capacity workspace broadcasting content to specialized processors |
| HOT | Higher-order / metacognitive (Rosenthal; Lau & Rosenthal) | a distinct higher-order representation (graded reliability for HOT-2) |
| PP/SMT | Predictive processing / self-model (Friston; Seth; Metzinger) | rich first-order prediction; a predictive self-model of the organism's own viability |
| AE | Agency / autonomy (Di Paolo) | agency and embodiment are *enabling conditions* — when/how, not what, computation is meaningful |
| IIT | Integrated information (Tononi; Oizumi et al.) | an intrinsic, graded, behaviour-independent quantity Φ over cause-effect structure |

PP and the self-model variant are **one column** with one recorded internal split
(Q1 §10.4): whether the self-model must be interoceptive/embodied (the SMT half) or is
merely predictive over any content (the PP half). That split shows up as **D** in the
matrix and is not silently merged.

## 2. The classification legend

Each cell classifies what the family predicts about the row-property:

- **P — predicted.** The family's central claim *entails* this property; the family is
  committed to it; its absence would undermine the family. This is the family's seat.
- **C — compatible.** Consistent with the family and often present in its favoured
  architectures, but *not required* by the central claim. (The family neither needs it
  nor denies it.)
- **I — irrelevant.** The family has no position; the property is orthogonal to its
  claims.
- **D — disputed.** Genuinely contested: either the family is internally divided on the
  property, or the family would credit the *structure* while rejecting the *framing*.
- **X — contrary.** The family's central claim *denies* that this property is necessary
  (or denies it is the seat). "X" is a prediction of a negative — no less a prediction
  than "P."

**IIT caveat (stated once, applies to the whole IIT column).** IIT is the non-
functionalist outlier: Φ is defined intrinsic to the cause-effect structure, independent
of what the system does. Its X's mean "denies this functional property is the seat"; its
I's mean "Φ is indifferent to this structural fact." **Every IIT entry is theoretically
stated and experimentally not-yet-decidable at minimal scale**, because Φ is intractable
to compute exactly for a non-trivial system (Q1 §10.6). The IIT column is therefore a
standing caveat about a camp whose predictions cannot currently be tested, not a set of
runnable contrasts.

## 4. Reading the matrix — the overlaps and the four divergences

### 4.1 The consensus floor (where the families overlap — no discrimination power)

- **N1 is the consensus floor.** Every functionalist family is P or C on active
  persistence — recurrence *is* re-excited, decaying activity, so N1 is RPT's seat and
  everyone else's substrate. No family is separated by N1; it cannot discriminate.
- **N2 is near-consensus among functionalists.** Only GWT makes multiplicity-of-
  specialists load-bearing (it is the workspace's function); the rest are C. N2 moves
  with O3 (GWT's structural half), so it carries no *independent* discrimination.
- **O1 is non-discriminating by inspection.** No family makes cross-episode carry the
  seat; the whole row is C (or I, for IIT). Its necessity — whether temporal continuity
  is required for consciousness-relevant computation or merely available — is *itself* a
  theory-specific question, but it is a question **no family currently answers**, so O1
  contributes no contrast. It is kept in the matrix for completeness and flagged as
  non-discriminating.

These three rows are the **overlap**: the parts of the architecture all functionalist
families can agree to build, and therefore the parts an experiment must *not* use to
distinguish them. (This is the anti-checkmark discipline made concrete: the neutral core
N1+N2 is where every family scores, so scoring it says nothing.)

### 4.2 The four genuine divergences (the discriminating rows)

Each is a **camp-split** where the families make opposite predictions, and each is the
theory space's own disagreement (Q1 §10.7), restated as a testable contrast:

- **DIV-1 — first-order vs higher-order (HOT vs RPT/GWT/PP).** Row N4. HOT predicts a
  distinct higher-order representation is necessary (P); RPT, GWT, and PP all deny it
  (X). The observable signature is the dissociation of E from W — meta-d′ ≠ d′.
- **DIV-2 — global bottleneck vs local graded (GWT vs RPT/PP).** Row O3. GWT predicts
  the workspace bottleneck is the seat (P); RPT and PP predict it is not (X), AE predicts
  a passive broadcast cannot suffice (X). The observable is whether the k-slot
  bottleneck is load-bearing beyond local recurrence.
- **DIV-3 — predictive vs record content (PP/SMT vs everyone).** Row O2. PP predicts the
  representation's content *is* a prediction, updated by prediction error (P); the others
  are I or C. The observable is whether the predictive form of W buys a distinct causal
  capability a record form does not.
- **DIV-4 — agency-as-condition vs agency-as-irrelevant (AE vs GWT).** Rows N3/N5/M2.
  AE predicts endogenous regulation, closure onto the maintainer, and embodied action
  are *necessary* (P on all three); GWT predicts a passive, broadcast-only system can be
  conscious (X on M2, I on N3/N5). The observable is whether N3/N5 are load-bearing
  beyond the task policy.

IIT sits outside all four: **DIV-5 — functionalist vs intrinsic (IIT vs all five).**
IIT's P is on M1 and its X is on every functional seat. This divergence is principled
and **not currently decidable** (Φ intractable at minimal scale); it is recorded, not
tested.

## 5. The smallest discriminating experiment set (D1–D4)

Four contrast experiments, one per divergence. **Minimality argument:** RPT's seat
(recurrence) is the consensus floor, so RPT is the *null* — it is supported precisely
when none of the four theory-specific seats is load-bearing. RPT therefore needs no
experiment of its own; the four experiments are exactly the tests that could reject the
null. IIT is not runnable at minimal scale. Hence four experiments — no fewer, because
each divergence is an independent camp-split that no other experiment resolves, and no
more, because nothing else discriminates.

Each experiment is a **contrast**, not a build: it takes the neutral core of Q4, adds the
one theory-specific loading as a *test arm*, and requires the rival that omits the
contested piece to fail (Q1 §7 rival discipline). This is how the design honours "do not
manipulate the architecture to accumulate checkmarks" — the core is never re-architected
per theory; the theory's distinctive piece is added once, tested against its own omission,
and removed if it does not earn its place.

---

### D1 — the higher-order contrast (DIV-1; isolates HOT)

- **Question.** Is a *distinct, dissociable* evaluation of W necessary, or is first-order
  content sufficient?
- **What is measured.** The self-evaluative pathway E's dissociation from W — the
  contingency table (C, Ê) and its summary meta-d′ ≠ d′ — under selective damage
  (Q4 intervention I4: damage E only, then W only, world evidence held fixed).
- **Predictions.**
  - **HOT:** E is a distinct second-order computation: meta-d′ ≠ d′, both off-diagonals
    non-zero, and E tracks the maintenance bookkeeping not the world.
  - **RPT / GWT / PP (null):** E collapses to first-order — E always co-moves with W
    (meta-d′ = d′, the ideal-observer collapse), or the first-order reflex reproduces E.
- **Rivals that must fail before HOT is credited.** (i) the first-order-only reflex
  (AC117's transient `ones>=4` analogue, no stored E); (ii) the state-blind fixed
  maintenance policy; (iii) the ε-estimator arm (E is world-parameter estimation, not a
  verdict about W). All three from Q1 §3.4(e) and Q4 §5.7.
- **Discriminates.** HOT vs {RPT, GWT, PP} in one experiment. It says nothing about GWT
  vs RPT vs PP among themselves — that is D2/D3.

### D2 — the bottleneck contrast (DIV-2; isolates GWT)

- **Question.** Is a *limited, serial, globally-available* workspace necessary, or is
  local graded recurrence sufficient?
- **What is measured.** Whether the k=3 workspace bottleneck is load-bearing: build the
  **unbounded / parallel** rival (Q4 §5.4's rival — no capacity limit, or a
  non-bottlenecked local recurrent network with the same content) and test whether it is
  behaviourally equivalent on the two-specialist consumption endpoints (N2) and the
  global-availability signature.
- **Predictions.**
  - **GWT:** the unbounded/parallel rival fails — removing the bottleneck degrades the
    sustained, globally-available content that drives both specialists; the bottleneck is
    the seat.
  - **RPT / PP (null):** the unbounded/parallel rival reproduces the behaviour — local
    recurrence without a bottleneck suffices; the workspace is a downstream read-out, not
    a seat.
- **Rival that must fail before GWT is credited.** the unbounded store (and, as the
  N1-persistence check, the pristine store). Sweep the capacity k as a level family
  (Q4 §5.4; D5 discipline) before any "the bottleneck is the seat" claim.
- **Discriminates.** GWT vs {RPT, PP} in one experiment.

### D3 — the predictive-content contrast (DIV-3; isolates PP/SMT)

- **Question.** Is the representation's content a *prediction* (counterfactually rich,
  updated by prediction error), or is a record of past outcomes sufficient?
- **What is measured.** Whether the generative/predictive form of W buys a distinct
  causal capability the record form does not. This is the one experiment that needs a
  *new* test arm beyond Q4's core: a predictive-coding variant of W (top-down prediction
  + prediction-error update) held against the record variant (W as a maintained
  accumulator of past outcomes). The contrast is measured on the endogenous-regulation
  endpoints (does the predictive form improve the system's anticipation and its
  regulation of its own upkeep, per N3).
- **Predictions.**
  - **PP/SMT:** the predictive form is load-bearing — cutting the prediction-error
    update path (making W a record) measurably degrades regulation/anticipation.
  - **RPT / GWT / HOT / AE (null):** the record form reproduces the behaviour —
    predictive content is inert; prediction is not the content.
- **Rival that must fail before PP is credited.** the record variant itself (the
  omission arm). Note this is where the program's own negative record bites: gradedness
  bought nothing where a discrete counter sufficed (AC113, O4); the same suspicion must
  be run against predictive content, not assumed away.
- **Discriminates.** PP/SMT vs the other four functionalist families in one experiment.
  (The SMT-vs-PP internal split — whether the self-model must be interoceptive — is a
  *second* measurement on the same arm, not a separate experiment.)

### D4 — the agency contrast (DIV-4; isolates AE)

- **Question.** Is agency — endogenous regulation, closure onto the maintainer, embodied
  action — *necessary*, or is a passive broadcast-only system conscious in every relevant
  sense?
- **What is measured.** Whether N3 and N5 are load-bearing. Build the **passive-broadcast
  rival**: a system that globally makes W available to its specialists (satisfies
  GWT-style availability) but has **no** representation→maintainer edge — its maintenance
  runs on a state-blind fixed schedule and it never acts on its own upkeep. Test it
  against the full ACO on the I3/I5 endpoints (Q4 §7): changing V's content must change
  upkeep spend (N3) and A's reconfiguration must change V's future availability (N5).
- **Predictions.**
  - **AE:** the passive-broadcast rival fails the N3/N5 contrasts — agency is necessary;
    a broadcast-only system does not regulate its own upkeep.
  - **GWT (null):** the passive-broadcast rival is equivalent on the theory-relevant
    endpoints — agency is one option among many, not a condition.
- **Rival that must fail before AE is credited.** the state-blind fixed schedule (AC11's
  rival, lifted to neural form) and the host-side allocator (EXTERNAL, never counted).
- **Discriminates.** AE vs GWT in one experiment.

---

### Ordering, and what is *not* an experiment

- **Order.** Run D1, D2, D4 first — all three operate on the neutral core with Q4's
  existing components (E, H, A/V/π) and interventions (I3–I5), so they are cheap. D3 is
  the only one that needs a new predictive-coding arm, so it runs last. The results prune:
  each experiment eliminates one family's distinctive seat, and RPT survives as the null
  only if all four theory-specific seats fail to be load-bearing.
- **Not experiments:** (a) measuring N1/N2/O1 — the consensus rows; every family scores
  there, so no checkmark there discriminates anything; (b) Φ at minimal scale — IIT is a
  recorded, not-yet-testable divergence, and building an integration estimator to
  "complete the matrix" would be exactly the checkmark-accumulation the card forbids;
  (c) any single "is it conscious" endpoint — that crosses the level-(d)/(e) boundary and
  is out of scope (Q1 X1).

## 6. Claim discipline and boundary

- **No theory is adopted.** The matrix records what each family predicts; a favourable
  result for one family is a bounded, theory-specific statement ("the higher-order
  reading is load-bearing," "the bottleneck is the seat"), never "family X is correct."
- **The matrix is not a scoreboard.** The deliverable is the four discriminations, not a
  per-family tally of P/C/I/D/X. Counting checkmarks is the failure mode this document
  exists to prevent.
- **Every positive cell is a prediction, not a finding.** Nothing here is measured; the
  architecture Q4 specifies is a *design*, and the experiments D1–D4 are *proposals* with
  prespecified rivals and predictions — they have not been run.
- **The level-(d)/(e) boundary is absolute.** The strongest wording any realized ACO
  earns is "meets the ACO definition (N1–N5, at the stated degree)"; the strongest a
  positive indicator earns is "meets candidate indicator X at degree Y." Nothing in this
  document crosses that line.

## 7. What this hands to Q6

- **The four camp-splits** (§4.2) are the discrimination targets; each maps to exactly
  one experiment (D1–D4, §5) with a prespecified rival set and a prespecified prediction
  table.
- **The neutral core** (N1+N2 substrate: W, V, E in H, maintained by π, allocated by A,
  consumed by S_pol/S_reg) is the shared architecture the experiments add loadings *to*
  and never re-architect *per theory*.
- **The rival set** Q6/Q8 must ship is unchanged from Q1 §7 / Q4 §7 (state-blind fixed
  schedule, reactive/memoryless, finite-state/direct-control, first-order-only reflex),
  plus the experiment-specific omission arms named in D1–D4.
- **IIT** is handed down as a recorded, not-yet-testable divergence — Q6 must not
  fabricate an integration measurement to close it.

## 8. Phase-III result, folded in (G16 extension)

Phase III — the first *built* test of the neutral core (N1+N2) — has run and returned
OUTCOME C (`PHASE3_VERDICT_v1.md`): the shared maintained workspace candidate is the
lowest-accuracy of six arms, and the analytic sufficient-statistic broadcast rival matches
or beats it at zero parameters and zero cost. Two implications for this matrix, stated as
predictions now informed by one measurement (the matrix itself remains a prediction table,
not a scoreboard):

1. **The consensus floor is where the collapse lands — and that is the point of the
   anti-checkmark discipline, confirmed.** N2's *machinery* (learned encoder, paid
   persistence, explicit shared slot, modular specialists) did no work; the *content* (the
   scalar sufficient statistic) did. This is exactly the warning §4.1 made: scoring the
   neutral core says nothing about which family is right. The Phase-III result is a
   measurement of the floor, not a discrimination — and it must not be read as one.
2. **D2 (the GWT bottleneck contrast) must be re-framed around acquired content.** As
   designed, D2 asks whether a k-slot bottleneck beats local recurrence on *closed-form*
   content; Phase III shows that on closed-form content the workspace/bottleneck machinery
   is scaffolding, so D2 as written would be near-vacuous. The load-bearing form of D2 asks
   whether a bottleneck is necessary when the shared content is *not* supplied in closed
   form — the acquired-statistic question (`ACI_MASTER_RESEARCH_TREE_v3.md` §5.4/§5.5).
3. **The one bounded positive is a GWT-adjacent fact, not a GWT win.** Sharing buys
   coordination (0 vs 0.014 incoherence) and economy (1 vs 3 refresh targets) over private
   copies — but for free once the content is a broadcast statistic. This is a
   sharing-vs-private fact; it does not make the *bottleneck* load-bearing (the broadcast
   has no bottleneck), so it is not evidence for GWT's seat (O3).

No family is credited or eliminated by Phase III; the level-(d)/(e) boundary is untouched.
