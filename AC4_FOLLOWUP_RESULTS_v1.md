# AC4 extended result: maintained decision information enables sustained activity

2026-09-15. Same-author engineering evidence. Broad autonomy goal remains active.

## What changed in the evidence

The short AC4 experiment could not establish that controller repair was needed
for continued activity: its no-policy-write arm completed2048 ticks. The
prospectively specified follow-up kept the simulator unchanged, used eight new
seeds and extended the horizon to8192 ticks. Every self run completed with
100% final controller accuracy. Every paired no-policy-write run terminated.

This supplies the missing integrated controller-repair contrast at the longer
horizon. It does not invalidate the original short-horizon result. Dependence
is measured over a specified time and environment, not asserted universally.

## Rescue limitation retained

External reflection without B production does not provide a durable substitute
for the complete maintained organization. It preserves transport retention but
the supplied controller continues to sense low B; acquired priorities do not
automatically adapt to the changed causal dependency. Three of eight lower-rate
and all eight higher-rate retention-rescue runs terminate. Information also
degrades in surviving runs. The earlier short-horizon activity rescue was real
but not a guarantee of continued maintenance.

The low-B scheduling explanation follows from code inspection; these data do
not isolate every causal step from that behavior to termination. Do not describe
the observations as a completed mediation analysis. They motivate the separate
AC5 adaptation challenge.

## Scope of the resulting claim

AC4 now provides bounded evidence for a vulnerable acquired controller that
maintains its own information and produces a spatial boundary, repair catalysts
and energy converters that enable continued maintenance. Paid boundary production
and leakage were tested in the original grid; controller-repair dependence is
tested here. Conventional majority error correction remains the repair method.
Its conventionality is compatible with the measured maintenance dependency.

This follow-up does not test every constituent-production ablation in the
integrated world. W/C reaction dependencies are explicit and tested mechanistically,
with earlier AC2/AC3 ablations, but those earlier models are not interchangeable
with AC4. Complete integrated causal coverage remains narrower than a claim that
every edge of a closure network has been experimentally isolated here.

The inherited controller family is still24 demonstrated permutations. Memory
scaffold, spatial geometry, generic execution/sensing, reaction recipes and
shared interior reservoirs remain supplied. There is no demonstrated autonomous
discovery of a need, rich individual development, independent scientific
confirmation, full organismal autonomy or evidence of subjectivity.
