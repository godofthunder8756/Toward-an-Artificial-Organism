## Decision

Do not launch the current final experiment, draw final targets, or automatically
build and run the entire VM/product to pursue the stronger H2 interpretation.
The final NO-GO is already binding under E3_INTEGRATION_DECISION_v0_11.md.
It is a scientific and normative stop, not a shortage of tools, user permission,
or proof that the workload can never be executed.

Preserve the completed B02-B07 design work and the implemented component
foundation. Stop escalation of this design toward the original long-term aim
at that bounded foundation unless a specific additional engineering question
justifies its cost. Small correctness, ownership, and optimization checks can
still be useful. They do not close the original scientific aim, establish
organismal autonomy, or restore the refuted universal financial-scarcity claim.

A full engineering benchmark could characterize narrower H1/H2 performance.
That is a different justification from treating it as a necessary route to the
stronger goal. No such large execution is recommended on the present evidence.
Before renewed large-scale investment, require an independently reviewed,
prospective discriminating question or direct alternative whose contrast is
not explained solely by compulsory bookkeeping/conditioning expenditure.
Do not silently replace the goal, remove real costs, weaken a comparator, or
change frozen prices to create a desired result. No redesign is made here.

## Scientific discrimination

### H1 remains an unmeasured narrow maintenance hypothesis

The retained H1 asks whether paid reconstruction from surviving redundancy,
without recovery teaching/answer feedback, improves post-challenge planned
recall by at least 0.05 over writes-disabled storage while meeting functional
guards. There is no E3 phase result establishing that effect in actual trained
parents. A benefit would be meaningful paid-memory-maintenance evidence, but
ordinary error-correcting codes already provide its conventional mechanism.
No novelty or subjective experience follows from paying for that mechanism.

The B06 review gives the following expectation results for its explicit
clean-parent REP reference, not for arbitrary developed bodies:

| Quantity | Reviewed value |
|----------|----------------|
| No-write planned recall | 0.8199493705509089 |
| Periodic I=1 planned-recall lower bound | 0.9225773564136511 |
| Periodic minus no-write lower bound | 0.1026279858627422 |
| Adaptive minus I=1 upper bound | 0.0164042658069421 |
| Retained adaptive increment gate | 0.05 |

Thus paid maintenance has reference headroom, while the adaptive increment has
at most about 1.64 percentage points against competent I=1 periodic maintenance
under those assumptions, below the five-point gate. The precise bound is about
0.0164, not literally at most 0.016. It is neither a universal failure theorem
for trained parents nor a bound on every realized finite-cohort contrast. The
actual selected periodic rival, parent defects, acquisition and learned policy
performance remain empirical questions. Do not weaken I=1 or call the whole
experiment statistically incapable on the strength of this conditional result.

The canonical-empty containment assurance of at least 0.996306 per code is
also conditional mathematical evidence: selected independent inputs, funding,
and conforming service behavior are premises. It is not an observed reset pass,
generator-security proof, or recovery of deleted target information.

### H2 numerical success cannot restore universal financial scarcity

The retained numerical H2 concerns reduced obsolete-region corrective writes
and their material expenditure, useful recall and viability. Its gates include
50% reduction versus continued usefulness, 10% versus periodic, positive paired
lower bounds, useful recall at least 0.80, activity/completion at least 0.90,
and the useful-recall loss guard. These outcomes could in principle pass.
There is no measured H2 outcome here, and the budget calculation alone does
not prove that these numerical gates must fail.

The stronger claim is already contradicted at the financial level:

$$
P_{\mathrm{fixed\ REP,full\ conditioning+max\ correction}}
\le 28+5=33<g=36.
$$

B06 supplies the sourcewise funding induction, not merely a pooled end-balance
comparison. A conventional fixed/frozen REP path can afford all conditioning
and maximal correction under the selected support. This refutes the claim
that the budget universally forces sacrifice of obsolete code. It does not
prove accurate all-region retention, freedom from miscorrection, or positive
survival in every damaged starting state.

The logical distinction is decisive: a finite RL/script comparison can show
that certain policies retain useful information with fewer obsolete writes;
it cannot negate an already affordable conventional path or establish a
universal quantifier over all legal policies. Both statements can coexist:
numerical H2 passes, while universal forced financial sacrifice is false.
No amount of current-version sampling repairs that mismatch in interpretation.

### Bookkeeping and scripts measure a narrower mechanism

All-conditioned no-code learning demand is 40 material per source per ordinary
tick including leakage; fixed/frozen no-record demand is 28. Removing current
TD saves two per source. The twelve-unit learner overhead and fixed-order
automatic conditioner contention are distinct from unavoidable code-memory
scarcity. The necessary learning-path inequality
$8U + W + \Lambda \le 2928$ does not exclude the cheaper fixed/frozen path.

E3_CONFORMANCE_CONTRACT_v0_10.md deliberately retains genuine learning records
and TD work in SCRIPT-U/ALL, even though Q does not choose their actions.
It also executes 270 units of declared unused selection-energy padding.
That padding is transparent and legitimate for the selected diagnostic; it
does not make the scripts efficient conventional rivals. Material bookkeeping
and energy padding must not be conflated.

Consequently, low-budget SCRIPT-U success with SCRIPT-ALL failure could expose
learning-overhead/conditioning interactions. It would not prove that no cheaper
policy can retain all regions. The contract itself states this qualification.
Whether those interactions dominate realized errors or runtime is still
unmeasured; the reviewed accounting identifies a plausible, already sufficient
alternative explanation, not an empirical attribution of every outcome.

Missing useful responses receive zero whereas useful wrong emissions receive
-64. That supplies a potential avoidance incentive, not evidence that avoidance
is attainable, learned, or optimal. Keep missingness, emitted errors, all planned
denominators, viability and undefined zero-write comparisons separate. The
short discount scale, gamma=15/16, also does not guarantee distant retention.

### E3b and the original aim remain open

E3a intentionally implements conventional ECC plus ordinary allocation. Its
executor, decoder, clock, generic update law and routing remain protected, and
power/material support is external. Shared vulnerable storage does not make it
one developing neural organism. E3b's materially vulnerable substrate generating
both recall and maintenance remains deferred; no E3a result establishes that
architectural claim, self-produced boundaries, autonomous motivation, life, or
consciousness. No evidence here demonstrates novelty or deliberate avoidance
of novelty. The interpretation must follow what was measured, not expectations
about what further engineering ought to discover.

it would clear state between regions or mis-handle envelopes/admission.
`execute_fast()` preserves that limited scope; it is not a compiled full VM.

## Runtime feasibility without a false impossibility claim

The host-feasibility note reports a noisy serial baseline of about 0.400 seconds
per BLOCK scan and 0.00639 seconds per REP scan, averaged over its two fixed
fixtures. Its selected engineering arithmetic is 91,358,208 logical due
responses and 77,627,392 offered controllers. Assuming every response and every
controller scans gives 84,492,800 scans per representation and a conditional
397.343619 serial scan-days in that old implementation.

That is not measured full-experiment runtime, a forecast, a lower bound, an
upper bound, or proof that execution is impossible. Validity/death/rejection
and proved sharing can reduce executions; services, faults, teaching, transport,
archive work and replay add other costs. Fused services also differ from a
standalone fragment that constructs/validates ROM and pays C/S each call.
Combined engineering-plus-final counts must not replace the engineering roster.

The user reports an optimized scan near 0.21 seconds. Current fast source and
its bounded benchmark adapter exist, but no durable measurement of that exact
number was located in the reviewed notes and it was not rerun here. Treat it
as reported component timing, not independently verified full-service throughput
or a reason to rescale the 397-day illustration into a new prediction.

One historical BLOCK trace had about 775 KB of recursively attributed objects,
not 276-byte snapshots at every instruction. Multiplying retained traces into
tens of terabytes is likewise conditional, not resident-memory necessity.
The archive expects compact records/commitments, not retention of every Python
event object. Present fragments accumulate and return events; bounded external
streaming and exact replay remain integration work.

A compiled backend, generic ROM reuse, tighter event encoding and reduced host
dispatch overhead may make execution practical. None is prohibited in principle.
Require exact effects, sourcewise costs, failures, input identity and observer
noninterference; native speed must not forgive simulated work. The current
ten-second benchmark admission check does not preempt an already started scan
and is not a hard wall-time/process-memory cap.

Full engineering is therefore not runtime-ready, but lack of a fast integrated
backend is a remediable implementation gap. It is not the primary reason to
stop confirmatory H2. Even instantaneous exact execution would leave the
scientific counterexample and v0.11 final prohibition intact.

## Residual information and observability boundaries

The runtime-boundary research demonstrated trusted-code binary pipe and guard
probes on Windows, including both generic scan fragments. It did not establish
an adversarial sandbox or production worker. Python audit hooks left `os.stat`
outside the tested open-event guard, and indirect host `os`/registry references
existed. Restricted-token/AppContainer support was not integrated or tested;
there is no basis to invent an unavailable OS feature or mandatory elevation
as the blocking reason.

Current source keeps policy/trace/host roles distinct at the component level,
but the following whole-system claims remain unverified:

* Actual operation ownership, paid scalar site and one-time delivery coincide;
  host permits cannot stand in for those facts.
* Scheduler G/header/tails, grants and stopping are independent of withheld
  targets/outcomes, and the worker receives no extra root/history/scorer state.
* Original events are streamed one way, with bounded buffering and no trace,
  digest, metadata, callback or timing feedback into model decisions.
* Full-state reset terminates old processes/pipes and cancels every acquired
  reference/capability; byte equality alone is not reference cancellation.
* Technical crash/timeout/malformed input is retained as technical failure,
  never fabricated death, normal S, or favorable missingness.
* SCRIPT-U/ALL's 32-state truth tables, genuine learning paths, 270 padding
  charges, feedback/ordinary-capability limits, and paid gate rejections are
  checked in the actual runtime, not only in a pure selector or wire codec.

These are known integration obligations and possible failure modes to test,
not new demonstrated component exploits. The current protocol guard prevents
one already discovered accidental removal of script learning bookkeeping;
it cannot by itself prove that the paid learning services ran.

### Conditional engineering order, not a launch plan

If a separately justified bounded engineering deliverable is pursued, preserve
the following dependency order. These are acceptance conditions, not a runnable
phase schedule, expenditure commitment, or instructions to execute a large
product. Stop after any useful finite slice; report its exact scope.

1. Close the residual paid-service/operator and process boundary. Reuse existing
   upkeep/TICK/CONDITION/ADMIT; do not recreate them. For a genuinely complete
   worker, remaining teaching/COMMIT, RESPONSE/TERMINAL and controller paths need
   actual gates, state/scalar ownership and linked public L/T. Small individual
   operator or process fixtures are legitimate standalone deliverables.
2. Establish actual runtime conformance before scientific use: complete paid
   prefixes, malformed packets, sole-body ownership, target-access boundaries,
   production-sized resets, capability cancellation, original trace and exact
   replay. Paper witnesses and codec acceptance are not substitutions.
3. Measure bounded complete-service runtime, peak memory, trace/IPC and archive
   cost. Set an explicit practical acceptance ceiling before committing to large
   engineering; no user time/compute budget is specified here, so none is
   invented. Treat technical caps as technical aborts. Optimize exactly, or
   stop if the justified cost threshold is not met. A compiled backend remains
   an option, not an assumed speedup.
4. Only if the selected engineering product is actually undertaken after those
   gates, retain its mandatory oracle/controls and all 512 declared script
   cases, including dead/failed/undefined outcomes. A convenient favorable
   subset is not completion. This obligation is conditional on that product;
   it is not a requirement to run months of work to reconfirm the binding NO-GO.
5. A future confirmatory claim needs a separate prospective scope/design review,
   meaningful discrimination from ordinary ECC/allocation and bookkeeping
   artifacts, and its own source/configuration/test/analysis/archive freeze
   before new independent final individuals. Passing items 1-4 cannot promote
   this version automatically. E3b remains separately deferred.

Reusable component tests, a bounded process prototype, or differential backend
work are optional research infrastructure. They become essential only to the
specific runtime/conformance claim being attempted. Implementing every service,
all scripts and the giant roster is not essential to close the accepted B02
design task or to make the present scientific stopping decision.
