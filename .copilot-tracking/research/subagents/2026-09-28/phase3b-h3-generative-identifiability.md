# Phase III-B H3 generative-task research

## Research questions

* Does the proposed four-factor noisy-evidence task defeat a fixed task-clock or identifier router?
* Is occupancy decision-theoretically superior to unconditional belief-state broadcasting under the stated channel?
* Can the occupancy address transmit an arbitrary aggregate bit and defeat a specialist-content interpretation?
* What exact equations and counterexample histories establish formal H3 identifiability, or a STOP?

## Evidence and references

* `PHASE3B_TRANSITION_v1.md`: before neural training, analytically show that the channel forces distinct optimal contents in matched situations; stop if this fails. Winning fixed routing or unconstrained broadcasting is an acceptable negative architectural result.
* `COMPETITIVE_ACCESS_DEFINITION_v1.md`: the on-wire alphabet is four addresses times two payloads (eight messages, three bits); address, payload, sentinel and timing count. Selector sees observation-derived private states and context; each consumer sees public context, its own local input and the same message. It expressly warns that address may encode unrelated information and that an aggregate-bit router needs an equal-status rival.
* `ACI_RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION_v1.md`, sections 3-5: admissibility requires a falsifiable hypothesis and a strongest simpler rival; an analytic collapse is recorded rather than rescued by a new architecture.
* `PHASE3_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_v1.md`, part 3, and `PHASE3B_TRANSITION_v1.md`: prior same-content/intervention effects were largely source-wiring facts, not established behavioral advantage over analytic broadcast.

## Findings

### Generative model and Bayes actions

Let `Z_j ~ Bernoulli(1/2)` independently for `j=1,...,4`; two observations `Y_j1,Y_j2` at different earlier ticks are conditionally independent BSC(0.2) copies of `Z_j`. Let `n_j=Y_j1+Y_j2`. The common current observation is the same null symbol; a public context `c` rotates which three factors the specialists predict and sets positive payoff weights. The posterior and odds are

`O_j = P(Z_j=1|H)/P(Z_j=0|H) = 4^(2 n_j-2)`, `q_j=O_j/(1+O_j)`; for `n_j=0,1,2`, `q_j=1/17,1/2,16/17`. The conditional distribution of all four factors is the product of these Bernoulli posteriors, so `Q=(q_1,...,q_4)` is a sufficient belief state for losses depending on the factors and context. If a specialist additionally receives a BSC(0.2) local observation `L_j`, its posterior odds become `O_j 4^(2L_j-1)` (assuming an independent local sensor).

For a minimal explicit witness use context `c=0`: specialists `k=1,2,3` independently predict `Z_k`, loss `ell_k=w_k(c) 1{a_k != Z_k}`, weights `(1,1,1)`. At other contexts rotate the ordered active triple among four factors and vary the positive weights (e.g. weights `(2,1,1)`, `(1,2,1)`, `(1,1,2)`); the inactive fourth stream can be a fallback address when no active factor has nonzero decision value. Each candidate stream `j` supplies one observation-derived bit `B_j=1{n_j=2}`, truthful as a MAP bit whenever `n_j` is 0 or 2; its private state makes `n_j` available to the selector, but consumers see only `(j,B_j)`. At `c=0`, select the first active stream with `n_j != 1` and otherwise the inactive fourth. The chosen active address marks a strong posterior, and the payload distinguishes 0 from 2. The inactive address says no active stream was strong. This is one of four addresses and one payload at a fixed decision tick, no extra blank symbol or external history.

With no local evidence, prior risk for each active factor is `1/2`. A selected strong factor has posterior error `1/17`, hence value `1/2-1/17=15/34`. `P(n_j != 1)=17/25` and `P(n_j=1)=8/25`. For three equal-weight factors the adaptive policy's expected reduction in total loss is `(15/34)[1-(8/25)^3]=0.42672`. A fixed context/clock/identifier router that always selects one specified factor and emits its best one-bit summary has expected reduction `0.3`: the two earlier observations give Bayes error `0.2`, compared with no-signal error `0.5`. Fixed deterministic rotations cannot exploit independent evidence; their expected gain remains `0.3` per decision. The adaptive gain exceeds that by `0.12672` in this constrained, no-local example. With unequal positive weights, transmitting the strongest available weighted factor can likewise strictly beat any history-blind selection on events where its preferred target has conflicting observations but another target has agreeing observations.

Matched witness histories (both have the same public context, clock, identifiers and null current observation): `H_A=((1,1),(0,1),(0,1),(0,1))` and `H_B=((0,1),(1,1),(0,1),(0,1))`. Their posterior vectors are respectively `(16/17,1/2,1/2,1/2)` and `(1/2,16/17,1/2,1/2)`; optimal *factor-specific transmission under the stated restricted menu* selects stream 1 then stream 2. Both histories have strictly positive probability. A clock/context-only router must choose the same address for both and loses the `15/34` conditional opportunity in one history. If local BSC observations are held fixed across histories, an agreeing earlier pair still makes the MAP action constant even against a contradictory local bit (odds `16/4=4`), whereas mixed earlier observations make the optimal action follow the local bit (odds `4` or `1/4`). These histories also separate context-only routing when private local inputs are matched, subject to the exact policy and receiver codebook.

### Important limitations and side channels

* The above is a witness against *fixed-address routing*, not a proof that this occupancy organization outperforms every three-bit encoder. In general the Bayes risk is `R(pi)=E[sum_k min_a E(ell_k(a,Z,c)|c,L_k,W_pi)]` for the policy-induced message `W_pi=(A,B_A)`; an all-history shared encoder with the same eight-symbol alphabet can implement exactly the composite map `(H,c)->(A,B_A)` and match the candidate, even before considering stronger codes. Without local evidence, three binary specialist actions yield at most `2^3=8` joint action vectors: an unrestricted three-bit encoder observing all earlier evidence can broadcast that whole Bayes action vector, regardless of the four factors. The fact that there are `16` factor assignments alone does not prove a decision-relevant bandwidth shortage.
* If an unconstrained broadcaster sends the full `Q`, every receiver can ignore the extra information and simulate any occupancy decision; thus its optimal Bayes risk is at most that of selective occupancy. Sending `Q` exactly requires representing up to `3^4=81` distinct posterior vectors, not just three bits. The meaningful comparison is either against a *matched three-bit* history-blind code, or else a separate cost/latency resource constraint, not an asserted decision-risk advantage over unlimited broadcasting. With independent local BSC inputs, `q=1/17,1/2,16/17` induce three distinct specialist decision functions of `L`: always 0, follow `L`, always 1. Three independently targeted specialists have `3^3=27` possible conditional decision-function triples, so even an unrestricted three-bit code cannot losslessly supply all such mappings. This helps make the bounded channel decision-relevant, but it does not privilege an address/content architecture over a general three-bit encoder.
* The address carries two genuine information bits: choosing `A=1+2g_1(H,c)+g_2(H,c)` transmits any two selector-computable Boolean aggregate functions regardless of what the payload represents. In particular, `A=1+g(H,c)` encodes any one arbitrary aggregate bit while each selected stream still truthfully outputs its own observation-derived payload. If the streams may each observe the full history, let every stream's payload equal the third binary Bayes action and choose the address to encode the first two: the same nominal occupancy channel then communicates all three actions without any content-specific stream selection. Restricting each stream to its own factor prevents that particular construction but not the address-only aggregate bit, and is an additional assumption rather than a consequence of four stream names.
* If the three heads only differ by assigned binary target and scalar weight, verify they satisfy the contract's independently defined specialist criteria; labels and weights alone do not establish different decision processes or organizational necessity. Predeclare local-information partitions, genuinely distinct losses/actions, bounded encoder and selector access, and causal source-swap and decoded-content tests; compare with equal-budget nonoccupancy shared encoders, fixed routes, private consumers, and full belief broadcast. No purely observational behavior can prove unique semantic provenance when an unrestricted eight-symbol encoder is a superset of the candidate's input-output maps.

### Verdict on H3 and STOP

The narrow *existence* criterion (matched current/context/clock, different past evidence, different optimal choices under a specified factor-truthful stream menu) passes by the two histories above. Do not call that a general architectural victory or a proof of semantically identifiable learned contents. If H3 demands a strict decision-risk advantage over unconditional **full** belief-state broadcast, the criterion is mathematically impossible: STOP that claim before training. If H3 instead requires an advantage specific to occupancy over equally budgeted arbitrary three-bit coding, the proposed unrestricted selector/address protocol cannot identify it; STOP or narrow the claim and add semantic/source restrictions and a co-equal general-encoder rival before freezing a study. A fixed router winning empirically would be a *negative experimental result*, not a reason by itself to call the narrow contrast unidentifiable.

## Follow-on questions and clarifications

* Clarify whether H3 names only the pretraining matched-history existence test, or also requires rival-exclusive architectural advantage over an unrestricted three-bit code or full-information broadcast.
* Clarify whether the four streams see all past observations or separate per-factor histories, and whether the selector can see their unquantized private states; these change what aggregate encodings can be realized.
* Specify the three specialists' action spaces, loss asymmetries, independent local evidence and primary endpoint before claiming genuinely distinct specialists or computing an exact final rival ranking.

No neural training, execution, or experiment was performed; the numerical results above are exact algebra for an illustrative, explicitly specified task, not measured performance.
