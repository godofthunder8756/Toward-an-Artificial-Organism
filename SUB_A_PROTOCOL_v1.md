## 1. One hypothesis, four questions

Proposed comparative H1-A:

> After acquisition, a vulnerable system's own live-trace consolidation retains
> its acquired associations better than information-, vulnerability- and
> resource-matched simpler maintenance mechanisms under hidden uneven damage.

This is an engineering/substrate hypothesis, not a consciousness indicator.

- Q-EXIST: can live redundant information support partial-loss reconstruction?
  Ordinary coding already supplies a constructive answer in restricted regimes.
- Q-LEARN: can permitted task training acquire a usable skill and repair
  machinery? Not tested by initializing diagnostic fixtures.
- Q-CAUSE: does cutting actual repair computation reduce future skill?
  Requires a neural implementation and prospective causal assay.
- Q-NECESSITY/VALUE: does the candidate add retention or recovery beyond matched
  ordinary error correction and maintenance schedules? The proposed cheap
  candidate fails admission here; see Section 10.

The immortal arm is a reference ceiling, not an arm the candidate must beat.
The yoked arm is a regulation/control-ownership test, not evidence of regulation
in a fixed-schedule Phase A. Equality must not be called failure of repair's
causal role. It does defeat any candidate-exclusive comparative claim over it.

## 2. Pretrained skill and sufficient-information check

Propose 4,096 independent fair binary cue associations acquired during an
initial, damage-free task-training segment. Subsequent queries choose cues
uniformly. Acquisition is within each individual's one continuous history,
not a clean checkpoint reloaded after damage. Task labels are evaluator-only
after acquisition. No performance feedback, labels, examples or answer-bearing
prediction targets may enter maintenance. Cue identities alone are permitted.

A recurrent delayed-query extension would need a separately specified,
vulnerable state and the monolithic recurrent rival before admission. It is
NOT implemented here. A random associative task alone is not evidence that
recurrence, neural scale or an integration architecture is useful.

The unknown table has 4,096 bits of entropy. Exact recall of all uniformly
possible tables needs at least 4,096 bits of acquired information; the cue
index alone is insufficient. This is not a dimensionality lower bound for
unbounded-precision real numbers, or proof that a neural network is needed.
**An ordinary learned table plus redundant coding is the strongest sufficient-
information rival and trivially realizes the skill.** Three traces per
coefficient are a standard repetition representation, not a novel mechanism.

The test fixture uses eight entries and engineering seed families only. Its
values are supplied synthetic fixtures, not acquired skills or pretraining
evidence. Scaling this fixture to millions of parameters would add no scientific
discriminator.

## 3. Proposed damage law and why it matters

Reject pure positive multiplicative weight shrinkage as the sole damage law:
for sign-decoded associations `sign(a w) = sign(w)` for every `a > 0` in exact
arithmetic. Shrinkage alone can leave the measured skill unchanged; rescaling
is then a sufficient repair rival. Underflow is not a principled damage regime.

For a later admissible neural proposal, the provisional law is

`theta_next = (1 - lambda_region(t)) theta + sigma_region(t) xi`,

followed by local destructive erasures to zero. All task, replay and repair
parameters, biases, persistent recurrent states and eligibility states must
receive damage. There is no preserved base weight plus reversible conductance.
Transient arithmetic registers are discarded; persistent optimizer moments
cannot become undeclared protected templates.

Provisional values, NOT selected by performance:

- Base shrinkage `0.0002`, additive Gaussian drift standard deviation `0.002`.
- Each region's hidden multiplier is in `{1, 4, 16}`; per-tick switching
  probability `1/200`, uniformly redrawn, including the current value.
- With probability `1/2000` per tick, choose one region uniformly and erase
  independently 10% of its coefficients; events are unannounced.
- Three spatial trace replicas span different regions; correlated complete-
  replica and complete-information deletions are separate interventions.

This is a computational fault model, not a model of biological synaptic
turnover. Additive drift changes stored values; erasure destroys information;
region changes attack stationarity. The numerical values have no empirical
biological justification. Their adequacy requires pre-freeze engineering of
the regime, not retrospective tuning to make the candidate win.

Damage draws and true region modes live only in the evaluator. The agent may
read its live traces; it cannot compare them with originals or inspect damage
events, elapsed-since-lesion counters or true mode labels. Trace disagreement
is partial interoception, not true integrity: three consistently wrong copies
can agree perfectly. Demonstrate this aliasing before freeze.

Event order: external query using current state; evaluator-only scoring;
damage to all persistent banks; maintenance using only post-damage live state;
advance the single lifetime clock. No reset, restoration or final-state
selection is permitted within a life.

These values describe a proposal, not an executed regime. The diagnostic
skeleton accepts explicit damage events to test laws and pairing; it does not
generate this world or expose those events to repair.

## 4. Repair, replay and the closure boundary

Cheap candidate for admission testing:

1. Store each association in three vulnerable traces.
2. A live replay bank selects cues; no stored clean teacher or cached logits.
3. Move live traces toward their current median on every tick, using a gain
   encoded in a vulnerable repair bank.
4. Apply the same consolidation to the replay and gain traces.

All persistent fixture banks are damaged. Complete erasure of all task,
replay and repair banks leaves no path to reconstruct arbitrary associations.
Restoring a generic gain alone cannot restore them.

**But:** median decoding, addressing, arithmetic and the update law are
supplied by the host. A damaged gain gating that law is not a damaged circuit
that performs the law. This fixture fails mandatory constraint 5 as a neural
production-closure implementation. No claim of production closure is licensed.

A compliant successor must specify a physically modeled, vulnerable circuit
for replay generation, target inference and consolidation, show its selective
lesion changes its actual computation rather than only its authorization, and
declare the primitive supplied substrate. Ordinary distributed error correction
is allowed as an explanation; it must not be hidden by neural naming.

## 6. Proposed measurements and gates -- NOT frozen

Admission gates must precede training:

- No clean state, teacher, target cache, external example or optimizer backup
  available after acquisition.
- Nonstationary hidden faults actually damage function; fixed schedules and
  the ordinary code are tested before selection/freeze.
- Performing repair machinery is vulnerable, not just a gain or action gate.
- A comparator can produce a different observable outcome for the claimed
  mechanism. Exact cloned transitions are a reduction, not a neural experiment.
- All missing rivals and prospective compute accounting implemented.

If a successor passes admission, propose primary per-seed retention as accuracy
averaged over all planned post-acquisition uniform queries, including failures.
Pair seeds and query/damage streams across arms. Primary effect is candidate
minus the within-seed best *admissible simpler decaying maintenance rival*.
Proposed meaningful improvement: strictly greater than 0.02 in at least
23/32 seeds (two-sided exact sign test against 1/2 has resolution adequate for
this rule; tail probability at 23 wins is approximately 0.02006). Equality at
the effect threshold counts as failure; failures and ties are never discarded.
This is a proposed effect rule, not data or an equivalence test.
Immortal is descriptive. Yoke remains diagnostic unless a distinct ownership
contrast is defined prospectively.

Secondary per-seed endpoints: last-quarter and terminal accuracy; retention
curve/AUC; damage-to-recovery latency; catastrophic-collapse incidence;
first repair-circuit lesion effect versus sham; live replay coverage; task
and maintenance cost. No active-only deletion of failed timepoints. Recovery
must not receive external answers.

Controls: intact/no-op replay, repair-performing-circuit lesion, replay-
generator lesion, generic-material/gain restoration without learned values,
partial loss, complete information deletion with independent random tables,
and intact computations with null writes. Chance after complete deletion is
an expectation over independent unknown tables, not exactly 50% on each seed.

Report H1 causal maintenance separately from comparative advantage. No L1,
anticipation, metacognition, meta-d', type-2 AUROC, novelty or H3/H4 endpoints
are promoted into Phase A. Survival will be separate from accuracy in Phase B.

## 7. Seed, sample and compute proposal

- Unit-test fixtures only: engineering seeds `0..3`; no training.
- Proposed future engineering: `0..7`, all reported, disjoint from finals.
- Proposed future finals: 32 independent acquisition/model seeds `1000..1031`;
  separate evaluator streams, paired across arms. Never drop a failed seed.
- Acquisition budget, learning rule, recurrent architecture, delayed-query
  horizon and lifetime length: unresolved; must be fixed in a successor before
  engineering permission or freeze. No hidden tuning grid.

For perspective, a dense 1M-parameter model with 10,000 maintenance steps costs
roughly `10^10` parameter-visits per life before inference and replay learning;
32 seeds x 7 arms gives `2.24 x 10^12` visits. At 10M parameters that is
`2.24 x 10^13`. These are illustrative storage-touch counts, NOT MACs,
GPU hours, physical memory traffic or an approved run budget. Forward/backward
graphs and hardware must be measured in a capped pilot to estimate GPU time.
Float32 live weights alone would be 4-40 MB; redundant traces and replay/
repair circuits must be inside the whole-arm 1-10M budget, not added for free.
No credible GPU-hour estimate is available before an admitted architecture.
This session uses CPU diagnostic tests only and records their wall time.

## 10. Admission result and stop rule

**ADMISSION_STOP / REDUCED_TO_SIMPLE_RIVAL for this diagnostic candidate.**

external scheduling and ordinary live consensus. It also identifies the supplied

result. Hidden damage alone cannot break an exact transition equivalence.

Do not run acquisition, neural engineering, scale-up, a freeze or finals.

and data-loss boundaries. It is not the 1-10M recurrent neural substrate.
