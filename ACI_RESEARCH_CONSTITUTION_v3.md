# ACI research constitution v3: minimum causal organization

Prospective successor to [v1](ACI_RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION_v1.md) and
[v2](ACI_RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION_v2.md), implementing the user-approved long-horizon
research mandate. Historical protocols, claims, errata and verdicts are unchanged.
This governance update authorizes no downstream experiment.

## Two goals, no consciousness score

Investigate separately:

- **O-axis:** supplied organization, internal maintenance, production/reconstruction
  closure, cognitive organizational closure, and constituted functional unity.
- **C-axis:** self-maintaining organization, regulation, representation,
  integration, internal evaluation, temporal self/world organization, theory
  convergence, and the separate phenomenal/philosophical boundary.

Autopoiesis, intelligence, representation, metacognition, a workspace, a self-model
and their conjunction do not establish phenomenal consciousness. Higher layer
numbers are research dependencies, not a consciousness meter.

The intersection is a hypothesis: cognitive processes may help maintain and
reorganize their own enabling organization. Every arrow needs a causal test.
Ordinary sufficient statistics plus control and memory remain the principal
alternative. An ordinary mechanism can instantiate a genuine functional role;
it need not be consciousness-specific to be scientifically informative.

## ACO is a falsifiable working target

[ACO v1](ACI_TARGET_CONSTRUCT_v1.md) is retained as a historical/versioned
specification, not protected doctrine. After major cycles review which
N-properties survive, reduce, need splitting, are architecture-dependent or
were badly operationalized. Publish proposed changes before a human-approved
ACO successor; do not retrofit old results.

This prospectively broadens v1's ACO-exclusive admission vocabulary: a card may
address an O/C question, a bounded competence prerequisite, or falsification of
the ACO target itself. It need not pretend to test an N-property. This changes
future admission policy, not the old target definition or claim ceilings.

Controlled creativity can question representations, modules, workspaces, RNNs,
weights, learning rules, boundaries and the controller/controlled split. Invent
freely, then reduce aggressively. Brain resemblance and novelty are not evidence.

## Four questions, three claim types

Every node separates:

1. **Q-EXIST:** can the declared class represent/realize the property?
2. **Q-LEARN:** can the specified development process acquire it from permitted
   experience?
3. **Q-CAUSE:** is it causally used? A probe is not enough.
4. **Q-NECESSITY/VALUE:** what does it add against the strongest simpler rival?

Keep **causal role**, **property-relative necessity**, and **engineering
advantage** separate. A lesion can establish use without necessity. Better loss
can establish engineering value without distinctive organization. Worse scalar
loss does not alone falsify a prespecified organizational role.

Credit an architectural principle only when its claimed organization survives
the strongest plausible reduction. A rival must match information, permitted
experience, substrate, vulnerabilities and relevant resources; it must be judged
on the claimed property, not its appearance. No protected copy, oracle or supplied
answer may be smuggled into an endogenous claim.

## Thirteen admission questions

Retain the ten questions' function from v1, with v2's claim-type clarification
and the broadened destination above. Every future card answers:

| # | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| Q1 | Which ultimate ACI/O/C question or bounded prerequisite does this address? |
| Q2 | Which ONE bounded, falsifiable architectural hypothesis/question does it test? |
| Q3 | What prespecified result would falsify it or defeat the card's proposed conclusion? |
| Q4 | What is the strongest simpler explanation, with information/resource parity? |
| Q5 | Which frozen evidence makes it necessary rather than a repetition? |
| Q6 | What would we do differently after success? |
| Q7 | What would we do differently after failure or unresolved results? |
| Q8 | What is the minimum apparatus: analytic proof, neural model, organism model, or none? |
| Q9 | Is this organizational cognition, substrate competence, or engineering/robustness? |
| Q10 | What is the exact claim ceiling, including what is NOT established? |
| Q11 | Which cognitive layer is addressed, and which prerequisite remains unsupported? |
| Q12 | Which organizational layer is addressed, and what substrate remains supplied? |
| Q13 | Which architectural belief would change under each decisive result? |

No answer, no active experiment. For a synthesis card, its falsifier is an
evidence/traceability failure, not an invented experimental hypothesis. For an
analytic card, state certificate coverage and its bounded UNRESOLVED rule.

Classify each card as **analytic**, **engineering**, **confirmatory**, or
**synthesis**. Engineering/discovery cannot be promoted into confirmatory
evidence. Analytic construction proves existence, not acquisition. New metadata
applies prospectively; do not rewrite completed historical cards.

## Evidence and substrate discipline

Use operational, scoped statuses: SUPPLIED, SUPPORTED_BOUNDED, PARTIAL, REDUCED,
NOT_ESTABLISHED, UNRESOLVED and PHILOSOPHICAL_BOUNDARY. Preserve original study
verdict words separately. These are synthesis assessments, not new experiment
outcomes or mutually exclusive claims about all architectures.

Every evidence row names system, property, evidence/counterevidence, intervention,
strongest rival, supplied substrate, ceiling and next falsifier. Distinguish
organism, harness, analytic/EXTERNAL and learned neural scopes.

- Production of realizing components is not invention of new content.
- State repair is not automatically reconstruction of cognitive machinery.
- N5 closure onto maintenance is not C5 temporal self/world organization.
- Paid cue retention alone is not constitutive C0 self-maintaining organization.
- Workspace non-necessity is not falsification of generic C3 integration.
- O3 does not require spatiality; O4 unity requires its own meaningful boundary
  test, not merely a Python object or an old spatial-model limitation.
- Fixed primitive substrate is permitted and explicit. Do not redeclare a
  contested protected mechanism as substrate after observing its failure.

Seed-level replication, prospective gates, independent replay, frozen final
protocols and strong rivals remain binding. Analytic proofs need coverage and
exact/rigorous checking, not replication by optimizer restarts.

Report behavior, generalization, adaptation, sample efficiency, latency, memory,
compute, modeled maintenance and robustness separately. Declared MACs, measured
committed memory and physical memory traffic/energy are different quantities.
Unmeasured quantities remain unmeasured; costs are findings, not exceptions.

## Collapse, surprise and theory queues

After each major cycle record:

- the strongest explanation that survived;
- what actually surprised us and what prior assumption changed;
- causal use versus necessity/value;
- every reduction, null result and unresolved boundary;
- whether another run would change an architectural decision.

Maintain a small inactive theory-only queue of radical alternatives, each with
its known equivalent, falsifier and cheapest discriminator. It is not a build
backlog. No failed mechanism is restored because a consciousness theory likes it.

## Immediate and human gates

The [A6 protocol](a6_decision_v1/PROTOCOL.md) governs the current finite
analytic/MILP effort. The full same-model joint-and-specialist gate is unchanged.
PASS needs actual legal finite weights, rigorous positive margins and graph
replay; FAIL needs full-family impossibility; exhausted uncertainty is UNRESOLVED.
Neither solver luck nor timeout is certification.

No neural training is authorized by this effort. PASS only permits considering
a separately approved prospective learning protocol. FAIL blocks this target
family; UNRESOLVED closes the effort, preserves the open mathematical question
and moves the program to an identifiable next question.

Human authorization is required for fundamentally new architecture families,
scale-ups, organism re-entry, first C4/C5/C6 studies, ACO-definition changes,
gate relaxation and phenomenal/philosophical inference. No authorization
licenses an unsupported empirical consciousness claim.

C7 remains separate: document empirical facts, philosophical bridge assumptions,
competing interpretations and what remains unknown. Do not manufacture a final
empirical criterion or equate C0-C6 success with phenomenal consciousness.
