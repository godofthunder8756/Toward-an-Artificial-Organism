## Verdict (one paragraph)

The K-series reverses the single most consequential error in the BASELINE_v2/S1 record and
then lands a positive where that record declared a negative. **Level (a)** organizational
closure is no longer "unresolved on J1": K3 issues a bounded **SUPPORTED** verdict — production
closure (Maturana & Varela clause i) within the declared model and operating range, under the
accepted substrate convention, with J1 resolved (substrate) and J4 (spatial unity) flagged
unresolved but outside the criterion's scope. **Level (b)** adaptive autonomy was already
established (AC99–AC105). **Level (c)** — the maintained cause-estimate — is now a real,
maintained, ceiling-accurate discriminator (K6) whose coupling to the maintenance machinery is
established in **both directions** by selective interventions (K7): the first time a level-(c)
representation is shown causally coupled to the level-(a/b) machinery, not merely co-present.
The residual negatives are sharp and honest: the representation's *survival advantage* is
seed-bounded (K6 Q4), its causal role in the cut is behavioural not survival-level, and the
**reliability tier (K8) is blocked, not falsified** — the estimator is too good to have an error
rate to monitor. The next bounded step is therefore **not** another mechanism. It is a
**stop-and-write** decision, with the reliability tier left blocked (reopening conditions on
record) and exactly one gated follow-on experiment named.

## 1. The five buckets, separated

### 1.1 Established mechanisms (the strongest wording each earns)

- **Level (a) production closure — SUPPORTED, bounded (K3).** Every level-(a) component
  {W, C, B, description, derived program} satisfies C1–C5 and the maintained state
  {pointer, coordination, route memory, decision state} satisfies S1–S4, forming a single
  strongly-connected production-dependency network with no external root, *under the substrate
  convention and within the AC105 operating range*. This is the charter's own ceiling for level
  (a): "meets the finite closure criterion within the declared model and operating range." Not
  "autopoietic" unqualified, not "alive." J1 resolved as substrate; J4 (spatial unity, clause ii)
  unresolved and outside scope.
- **Level (b) adaptive autonomy — ESTABLISHED (AC99–AC105).** Gray-coded relinquishment streak
  (not a reserve) plus the allowance-42 material budget coordinates the paid reconstruction write
  with the paid decision write; the organism acquires and relinquishes route content on its own
  viability conditions. Wording ceiling: "acquired an instrumental priority for adaptive
  relinquishment," never "wants"/"needs."
- **Task identifiability — ESTABLISHED (K4).** The two causes (E_world route move / E_machinery
  read cut, identical immediate failure) produce disjoint action-observation histories on the
  organism's own `(bound, used_held, productive)` triple: F1 (held-entry failure, one-sided ⇒
  E_world) and F2 (resumption-via-bound vs -unbound, two-sided). The separation is a property of
  the task, demonstrated by a diagnostic harness, not assumed.
- **Representational discrimination — CONFIRMED (K6 Q1).** The maintained one-bit cause-estimate
  reads E_machinery at the cut-window end 16/16 and E_world at the first drop 16/16, with **0
  post-intervention mistakes across 32 finals individuals**, on a fresh family (6000–6007). The
  discriminator transfers to the untouched family unchanged.
- **Storage-maintenance dependence — CONFIRMED in the content sense (K6 Q3).** Observer-discard
  per-tick byte-identity 16/16 (the value is read from vulnerable maintained state, not host
  state); no-cause identity 16/16 (the estimate is inert absent a cause); vulnerable-storage audit
  (bit in the sticky damage stream, written by the paid W-gated `bel_write`, excluded from
  `reg_from_active`). Scope: the maintenance is a *single paid write* at cause-onset; an
  ongoing-repair loop is not exercised in this world (the AC13 wall) and is not claimed.
- **Direction 1 — maintenance is load-bearing for representational accuracy and use (K7).**
  Cutting the estimate's paid write (`no_write`) leaves it inaccurate 16/16 in the cut and
  mis-used (relinquishes a still-valid route on 4/8 seeds where the candidate holds), with the
  write actually cut (`bel_writes == 0`, `bel_attempts == 0`).
- **Direction 2 — representation content causally drives adaptation and production/viability (K7).**
  Forcing E_machinery (`force_machinery`) reverses the adaptation (holds the stale route 16/16
  where the candidate relinquishes 16/16) and collapses production/viability 16/16 in the move;
  forcing E_world (`scramble`) relinquishes a valid route on 4/8 seeds in the cut where the
  candidate holds. Each effect isolated by a single-flag intervention against the maintained
  candidate and externally supported fixed-threshold rivals (r2/r4).

### 1.2 Model-specific negatives (properties of this world/design, not refutations of the class)

- **The survival advantage does not transfer (K6 Q4).** The candidate's cut-survival edge is
  vacuous on the fresh family (r2 survives the cut 16/16); the move-side edge is seed-bounded
  (candidate 14/16 vs r4 10/16, with a both-die seed 6002). The estimate's content is
  *behaviourally* causal but not *survival*-load-bearing on this family.
- **The cut's death bite is priority-corner-specific.** It bites only on priority `[3,0,2,1]`
  (engineering seed 4); no final seed in 6000–6007 or 6100–6107 carries it. On a fresh family a
  mid-cut relinquishment is recoverable (the route is still valid; a blind re-bind re-acquires
  it), so the survival contrast is vacuous by construction (K6 G2/G5, reproduced by K7).
- **Configuration limitation (carried, not inherited past).** K6/K7 run in the AC100-derived
  `corrupt=False` world — no corruption challenge, no persistent-trigger reconstruction, no
  allowance-42 budget. Whether the discrimination and coupling survive the AC105 combined
  corruption+move grid is a *separate, untested* question (K7's residual), not a verdict on the
  AC105 body.
- **The move-side deaths (6100/6107 candidate; 6002 both-die) are the re-acquisition boundary** —
  the organism relinquishes correctly but fails to re-bind in a starved economy (the AC83/AC74
  path). An operating-range limit, not a coupling failure.

### 1.3 Implementation defects (all found and fixed before the freeze)

- **AC106's update rule read `productive` alone and dropped `bound`** (K1 P2) — confounded by
  blind re-acquisition, so a dropped-and-rebound stale route was mislabelled "productivity
  resumed without re-binding." Fixed: K5/K6 gate every conclusion on the organism's own memory
  (`bound & not used_held` ⇒ E_machinery; `used_held & not productive` ⇒ E_world; `not bound &
  productive` ⇒ E_world).
- **AC106's r4 advertised a longer threshold but implemented `HOLD_N == STREAK_N`** (K1 P1) — the
  sharpest-falsification rival was never run. Fixed: r4 is now a genuinely longer counter
  (HOLD_N=24), verified distinct (G7).
- **AC106's scramble arm was a three-way confound** (read forced + writes suppressed + proactive
  renewal suppressed; K1 P5). Fixed: K6/K7's `scramble` is read-only (writes intact), so it is a
  clean causal-role control.
- **Reporting errors** (K1 P3): "reads E_machinery in both causes" was wrong (10/16 in move, not
  16/16) and read the horizon instead of the decision tick. Corrected; discrimination is now
  reported at decision times.

### 1.4 Untested hypotheses (open, not refuted)

- **Reliability monitoring / the HOT-2 metacognitive-monitoring tier (K8) — UNTESTED, blocked.**
  The first-order estimate is ceiling-accurate (0 mistakes), so there is no error variance for a
  reliability state to predict and no reliable-vs-unreliable regime to regulate. This is the
  mirror image of C3's wall (no cause information ↔ perfect cause information: both mean "no
  varying accuracy"). Reopening requires a non-zero, non-trivial error rate, via one of three
  named routes: (1) relax K4's perfect identifiability (partially-observed, overlapping
  histories); (2) degrade the observation interface (noise on the triple); (3) add a third cause
  or an ambiguous case the discriminator structurally misattributes. Each error must be
  predictable in principle from the organism's own observations and behaviourally consequential.
- **The cause-estimate's discrimination under the AC105 combined-challenge grid** (corruption +
  move + cut, i.e. a *third* cause) — K7 scoped it out; untested.
- **The re-acquisition boundary** (candidate move deaths) — an operating-range question, untested
  as a mechanism.

### 1.5 Philosophical assumptions (modeling judgments, explicitly adopted — not findings)

- **J1 (substrate/component boundary) — RESOLVED substrate (K3).** `prog.choose` (interpreter)
  and `advance()` (succession semantics) are supplied substrate, not undeclared coordinators.
  This is a modeling choice, not a measurement: the infinite-regress argument (any
  self-maintenance formalization bottoms out in a fixed substrate) makes it empirically
  unsettleable. The level-(a) verdict *carries* this classification. `advance()` is recorded as
  a larger substrate concession than `prog.choose`.
- **J4 (spatial unity) — UNRESOLVED, outside the criterion's scope.** B is produced and on the
  closure cycle (retention is load-bearing, AC10), but retention is substitutable by substrate
  (kernel flag / external B matter reproduce `keep` with zero internal boundary). The verdict is
  production closure (clause i) only; it does not assert M&V clause (ii).
- **J5 (interoceptive representational content) — adopted affirmative, flagged contestable.** The
  roadmap holds that route-level interoceptive content is representational (not assumed away). A
  reviewer may contest this; if contested, the verdict falls to "unresolved," not to "the
  mechanism is not worth building." This caps every level-(c)/(d) claim.
- **Inherited content is permitted (C4); content self-production is not required** (blocked at
  AC78 and not required even for the autopoiesis claim).
- **The indicator-framework reading is absolute and never crossed:** "meets a candidate
  indicator" ≠ "conscious." The level-(d)/(e) boundary is claim discipline, not a stop.
- **Montevil & Mossio "constraints" vs M&V "components" (charter v2):** a pointer/counter/register
  *value* is a state variable, not a constraint; C5/C6/C7/C8 are maintained state (S1–S4), not
  produced components. This is the adopted reading that makes the component set {W, C, B,
  description, program}.

## 2. Novelty assessed against primary prior work

The prior work named in the charter — Maturana & Varela 1980 (autopoiesis), Montevil & Mossio
2015 (organizational closure), Di Paolo 2005 (adaptivity), Butlin et al. 2023 (consciousness
indicators), plus the maintenance prior art (cascades, EWC, ECC, synaptic tagging) — is
unchanged. The K-series does **not** add a concept to any of them. What it changes is the
*material available to demonstrate*.

- **Against M&V / M&M:** the closure criterion (C1–C5, S1–S4) is a *disciplined application* of
  "closure of constraints," not a new operationalization. The contribution remains the negative
  finding *about the criterion's limits* — the residual to a closure verdict is a named modeling
  judgment (J1), not missing data — now sharpened by a machine-checkable criterion applied
  unchanged and yielding a bounded "supported" verdict. **No conceptual novelty.**
- **Against Di Paolo (adaptivity):** level-(b) adaptive relinquishment is an *instantiation* in a
  toy model, not an advance. **No theoretical novelty.**
- **Against Butlin et al. (HOT-2 metacognitive monitoring):** the K-series tests a concrete HOT-2
  candidate and now shows it *works* at two tiers — discrimination (K6, ceiling-accurate) and
  coupling to maintenance (K7, both directions) — but is *blocked* at the reliability tier (K8).
  This is a worked instance of the framework's indicator structure, including the precise point
  at which the instance saturates; it is informative, and it falsifies nothing in the framework.
  **No advance on the framework** — the mapping's flagged adaptation (interoceptive, not
  perceptual, content) remains the theory-specific judgment it was declared to be.
- **Against maintenance prior art:** reconstruction/renewal remains a toy instantiation of
  occupied territory. **No absence-of-prior-art claim.**

**Honest novelty statement (revision of S1 §5).** The project advances no concept in
autopoiesis, adaptivity, or consciousness science. Its legitimate claim is narrower and has now
grown one positive component on top of S1's "methods-and-negatives" framing: (i) a worked,
falsification-disciplined example in which a fully-specified simulated organism *earns a bounded
production-closure verdict* whose residual is a modeling judgment rather than missing data; (ii)
an established adaptive-autonomy result; (iii) the first demonstrated **causal coupling, in both
directions, of an internally maintained representation to its own maintenance machinery** — each
direction isolated by a single-flag intervention and matched against state-blind and
externally-supported rivals; and (iv) a reproducible catalog of *structural* walls (the economics
wall, the gate-shape wall, the locked-fixed-point signal wall, and now the ceiling-accuracy
reliability wall — an estimator too good to have a second-order state). Nothing here is an AI
breakthrough, and nothing should be described as one.

## 3. What supports a paper now; what would justify further architecture changes

### 3.1 Paper-ready now (bounded claims, with their exact scope attached)

1. **Level (b) adaptive autonomy.** Established and publishable independent of any reviewer
   permission and independent of the closure verdict (K3 §7). Positive substance: Gray streak +
   allowance-42, two-way relinquish/restore, the produced-W/C/B load-bearing ablations
   (AC10/91/92).
2. **Level (a) production closure, with exact scope.** Publishable *only* with the scope attached:
   "meets the finite closure criterion within the declared model and operating range, under the
   accepted substrate convention," J1 = substrate, J4 (spatial unity) flagged unresolved. Not
   "autopoietic" unqualified.
3. **Level (c) representational coupling (K6 + K7).** Publishable as a bounded positive: a
   maintained cause-estimate that discriminates two causes at ceiling accuracy on an untouched
   family and is causally coupled to its maintenance in both directions — **with the survival
   caveat attached** (the advantage is seed-bounded; the cut's bite is priority-corner-specific;
   the causal role in the cut is behavioural). This is the *new* result the K-series produced; it
   reverses the "level-(d) FALSIFIED" overreach of S1 and replaces it with a scoped
   level-(c) positive.
4. **The structural-negatives catalog.** Defensible now (S1 §6-A), now with a fourth wall (the
   ceiling-accuracy reliability wall, K8) and the sharper statement that the AC106 negative was
   an implementation defect, not a capacity absence.

**Not supportable as a paper:** any unqualified "autopoietic," "alive," "self-sustaining,"
"conscious," "metacognitive," or "self-aware" claim; any survival-advantage claim for the
cause-estimate; any reliability-monitoring claim.

### 3.2 What would justify a further architecture change

- **Re-opening the reliability tier (K8)** — only by one of the three named routes that
  introduces a *non-zero, non-trivial* error rate (§1.4). This is an architecture change (it
  degrades a currently-perfect estimator or broadens the task), and its warrant is *not yet
  present*: the estimator's ceiling accuracy is a positive result, and no evidence shows the
  reliability tier is the load-bearing open question.
- **The three-cause integration** (corruption + move + cut, §1.4) — warranted only by a decision
  that the survival-advantage or composition question matters, because it is the only remaining
  configuration that could both restore a survival consequence (attribution consequential in a
  combined-challenge economy) and, as a side effect, supply the error variance K8 needs. It is a
  *new task design* (three causes), not a re-run.
- **J4 (spatial unity)** — would require a boundary whose retention is *not* substitutable by
  substrate; a different world, not a parameter change.
- **J1 organism-specific reading** — only if a review overturns the substrate convention; then
  `advance()`/`prog.choose` become components with no production edge and the criterion fails on
  them (charter v2 §9). This is an argument, not data.

## 4. The next bounded step (chosen, with its warranting evidence)

**Decision: stop mechanism-building; write up. Do not re-open K8 now. Name one gated follow-on
experiment.**

1. **The immediate step is consolidation, not another experiment.** Three bounded claims are
   paper-ready (§3.1) and the K-series has no unresolved empirical dependency that could change
   the closure verdict (K3 §6) or the coupling verdict (K7, both directions gated 7/7). The
   strongest single positive — level-(c) representational coupling — is now established, and the
   next tier (reliability) is *blocked by success*, not by failure. The warrant for stopping is
   therefore the inverse of S1's: S1 stopped on a negative (level-(d) falsified); K9 stops on a
   bounded positive that has exhausted its tier's next step.

2. **K8 stays BLOCKED, not falsified.** The reliability tier is untested, and its reopening
   conditions are on record (§1.4). Weighing them against the rest of the evidence, they are not
   authorized now: each route amounts to degrading a working mechanism or enlarging the task,
   and the value of the reliability tier over the demonstrated discrimination+coupling has not
   been shown. This is a *block with conditions*, not a stop on the consciousness direction.

3. **The one gated follow-on experiment** — to be run only if a review or the project decides the
   survival-advantage/composition question is load-bearing — is the **three-cause integration**:
   does the maintained cause-estimate's discrimination and its two-direction coupling to
   maintenance survive the AC105 combined-challenge body (corruption @8192 + route move), where
   corruption is a *third* cause? Its warrant: it is the only remaining configuration that (a)
   tests whether the level-(c) mechanism composes with the level-(a/b) body, and (b) could
   naturally supply the error variance that un-blocks K8. Its absence is not a gap: without a
   decision that the survival consequence matters, running it is most likely to re-measure a
   known wall (S1 Branch 3, now stronger — the K-series has shown the survival consequence is
   seed-bounded in the clean two-cause world, so adding a third cause is a *different* question,
   not a repair).

**The single-sentence decision:** *Write up the three bounded claims (level b; level a with
exact scope; level-c coupling with its survival caveat); leave the reliability tier blocked with
its reopening conditions on record; authorize the three-cause integration only if the
survival-advantage question is judged load-bearing, and not otherwise.*
