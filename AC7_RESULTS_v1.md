## Causal role and alternative strategy

Learning stops at tick4096 in both hold and erase arms. Hold retains the acquired
routes; erase externally restores only their action fields to the initial port0
guesses. All six nonzero-mapping erase individuals then terminate, while their
hold counterparts continue. Mean whole-horizon activity difference among those
six is47.26 percentage points. In the two mapping00 cases the intervention is
inert and both arms survive. This is a targeted causal test of the acquired
route information within this controller, not just correlation with success.

Random port selection also survives every condition. Learned routing is therefore
not the only viable solution and must not be called universally indispensable.
It reduces mean contacts from1192.75 to603.875 for approximately the same number
of productive contacts. That is a contact-efficiency benefit, not proof of lower
total material cost: adaptive mean material spending is29667 versus29631.25 for
random selection. Learning and changed maintenance allocation have costs.

## Claim boundary

The system acquires access to enabling resources while maintaining the acquired
actions in its vulnerable organization. It acquires only two unknown routing
bits using an engineered two-choice failure-elimination rule. It does not create
a new metabolic need, discover the feedback semantics, or autonomously learn
the supplied maintenance priorities. The environmental mapping is not live
agent state, and is consulted only by the physical contact reaction; the learning
function receives resource identity and observed productivity.

This supplies a bounded bridge from demonstrated organization to experience-
acquired enabling information. It is not rich developmental individuality,
intrinsic normativity, independent confirmation, full autopoiesis or subjectivity.
The acquired action fields are vulnerable and repaired, but targeted erasure
does not replace a dedicated natural-decay ablation of those particular fields.
