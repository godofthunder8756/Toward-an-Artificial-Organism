# ACI master research tree v3 — the seven-phase program, advanced past the Phase-III verdict

2026-09-27. Deliverable of the G16 card (t_a299aaef): *what do Phase II + Phase III
jointly teach, and does consciousness-oriented architecture require expensive
persistence / shared representation / explicit workspace / modular specialists / only
sufficient-statistic availability / none of these yet?* This revision **supersedes**
`ACI_MASTER_RESEARCH_TREE_v2.md` (the N14 deliverable) while preserving it unchanged as
the record. It advances exactly one thing: Phase III is no longer "prepared; not
executed" — it has been **executed, frozen, audited, and adjudicated** — and that
verdict (OUTCOME C, the sufficient statistic wins) re-scopes Phases IV–VII and the
principles they inherit. It prepares G17; it does **not** design or execute it.

**What changed from v2 (the whole delta).** Phase III ran (`G11` implementation, `G12`
engineering screen, `G13` finals, `G14` independent audit, `G15` verdict) and returned
**OUTCOME C — the sufficient statistic wins**. The Shared Latent Workspace candidate (a
learned encoder + paid persistence π + an explicit shared slot, read by three
objective-distinct specialists) is the **lowest-accuracy arm** on raw inference (0.9632
vs 0.9634–0.9648 across the five rivals), and the analytic sufficient-statistic
broadcast rival R4 (and the identical-copies rival R5) reproduce everything the candidate
does at equal-or-better quality and **zero** parameters / refresh / incoherence (0.9645,
0 cost). The one genuine measured advantage the candidate holds — coordination (0 vs
0.014 incoherence) and maintenance economy (1 vs 3 refresh targets) over the
private-copy rival R1 — is narrow and **free once the content is a broadcast statistic**.
Phase III therefore confirms at the workspace level exactly what Phase II established at
the self-state level: the load-bearing object is the **availability of sufficient
content**, not any of the machinery (learned encoder, paid persistence, explicit
shared-slot identity, modular specialists) the theory hoped would carry it. The joint
teaching is stated in §5.4. Phases IV–VII are re-scoped onto sufficient-content
availability, not workspace complexity. The organism program is **not** reopened.

This is a **planning document**. It runs nothing, trains nothing, freezes nothing, and
edits no frozen artifact (runner, protocol, results dir, hash, or ledger). It is not
hashed into any study's `pre_run_snapshot.json`.

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The ACI program remains **seven phases arranged as one dependency chain**, but it now has
**two measured nodes, and both returned the same verdict shape**. Phase I (organism
foundations) is complete and exited. Phase II (neural self-maintenance) executed with
verdict **B + D**: explicit maintained self-state V unnecessary (re-encodes the readable
age), learned adaptive allocation unnecessary (a reactive threshold rule attains the
oracle), and the one survivor is **N1 active paid persistence** as a necessary-condition
substrate fact. Phase III (N2 — recurrent cross-module availability of a maintained
representation) has now executed with verdict **C**: the shared maintained workspace is
scaffolding over a sufficient-statistic broadcast; the candidate is the worst of six arms
on inference, and the analytic broadcast rival matches/beats it for free. **The joint
teaching**: across two consecutive neural levels the load-bearing object was never the
machinery (self-state slot, encoder, paid workspace, modular specialists) but the
**availability of sufficient content** — a scalar sufficient statistic, carried for free
by a register/broadcast — with paid persistence present throughout but load-bearing only
as a *necessary-condition substrate fact* (N1), never as an architectural advantage.
The answer to the card's question is therefore **"only sufficient-statistic availability
survives as load-bearing"**, with the boundary that the statistic here is closed-form and
known; whether an *acquired* (non-closed-form) sufficient statistic carries the sharing
properties is the one open forward question. Phases IV–VII are re-scoped accordingly; no
autopoiesis claim and no consciousness claim is made anywhere.

## 5. PHASE III — Recurrent global integration (EXECUTED; verdict C)

### 5.1 What ran

Phase III implemented the Shared Latent Workspace (SLW) candidate and five reduction
rivals R1–R5 per `ACI_PHASE3_PROTOCOL_v1.md`, ran an engineering screen (G12), froze and
ran the finals on 12 model seeds 100–111 (G13, 72 rows, `state_hash` 6327948c…), passed
an independent audit that reproduced the run byte-for-byte (G14), and was adjudicated
(G15). The task is the fixed-cause i.i.d. token task (5-symbol alphabet, mirror λ, probe
window) where the sufficient statistic is the scalar log-likelihood ratio, known in
closed form. The candidate is the five-predicate conjunction **SHARED ∧ CAUSAL ∧
FUNCTIONALLY DISTINCT ∧ AVAILABLE ∧ MAINTAINED** over one b-bit scalar W read by three
objective-distinct specialists (S_pol, S_plan, S_reg).

### 5.2 The verdict (OUTCOME C — the sufficient statistic wins)

Per-arm means over the 12 final seeds (`PHASE3_VERDICT_v1.md`, from the G14 table):

| arm | probe_acc | clean_acc | incoherence | refresh events | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| candidate (SLW) | **0.9632** | 0.9639 | 0.000 | 32768 | 393 |
| r1 (private copies) | 0.9634 | 0.9631 | 0.0140 | 98304 | 309 |
| r4 (suff-stat broadcast) | 0.9645 | 0.9645 | 0.000 | 0 | 0 |
| r5 (identical copies) | 0.9645 | 0.9645 | 0.000 | 0 | 0 |
| r2 (monolith RNN) | 0.9643 | 0.9645 | 0.0038 | 0 | 1242 |
| r3 (raw history) | 0.9648 | 0.9652 | 0.0129 | 0 | 3450 |

The candidate is the **lowest-accuracy arm** on raw inference. The reduction verdicts
(G15 §1, restated): **content** largely succeeds (W *is* the scalar sufficient statistic;
R4 holds it for free); **sharing** partially refuted, one-sided (load-bearing for
coordination 0 vs 0.014 and economy 1 vs 3 over R1, but free via broadcast); **monolithic**
succeeds on task (R2 beats the candidate at ~3× params, no W/π/shared state — modularity
is interpretability/intervention-only); **transfer** is trivial (S_conf = σ(W), S_bias =
W>θ are fixed functions of W); **causal** is mis-framed (the interventions overwrite a
scalar and re-apply fixed functions — they pass by construction and are candidate-only);
**distinctness** partially succeeds only as framing (distinct by fiat, not learned);
**maintained** is genuine but not load-bearing (the π-cut decay is clean, 0.9632 → 0.5000,
p = 0.00049, but a free register carries the same content).

**What survives (the bounded positive):** a maintained scalar representation can serve
multiple fixed, objective-distinct consumers with perfect structural coordination at 1×
maintenance cost, where the private-copy rival incurs nonzero incoherence at 3× cost —
while not outperforming any rival on inference, and while the fixed sufficient-statistic
rival does the same content work for free. This reduces to: **when the content is a
sufficient statistic, sharing it is free and automatic** (broadcast it); the workspace
machinery — learned encoder, paid persistence, explicit shared-slot identity — adds
nothing over the broadcast.

**Not A** (no Phase IV continuation of the workspace — the candidate is the worst arm and
R4/R5 tie/beat it for free); **not B** (sharing *does* add measured value over R1; B
undersells it); **not D** (the sufficient-statistic broadcast is a simpler/stronger
collapse than "use one RNN"); **not E** (private modules *lose* coordination + economy;
sharing is load-bearing there); **not F** (the maintained representation works — what
fails is the claim the *paid-maintained workspace* is needed); **not G** (no
identifiability/optimization blockage; byte-for-byte reproduction confirmed).

### 5.3 Two new demotions (absorbed into the principles doc)

Phase III adds two neural demotions to the set v2 §4.3 recorded as D9/D10:

- **D11 — "a learned encoder + paid-maintained shared workspace is necessary for flexible
  cross-module content" — DEMOTED.** The analytic sufficient-statistic broadcast (R4) and
  identical copies (R5) match or beat the candidate on every accuracy endpoint at zero
  parameters, zero refresh, zero incoherence; the monolith (R2) beats it at ~3× params.
  The workspace machinery is a pure cost the broadcast rival does not carry. Carry-forward:
  design for **sufficient-content availability**, not for a shared-slot/encoder/paid-
  workspace as an end in itself; add workspace machinery only where a rival that omits it
  provably fails (P6/D2/D9, now re-confirmed at the workspace level).
- **D12 — "modular specialists add value over a monolithic or broadcast architecture" —
  DEMOTED to interpretability/intervention-only.** R2 (one RNN, no W/π/shared state) beats
  the candidate; the three specialists are three fixed functions of one scalar with
  distinct invariance classes — distinctness by fiat, not demonstrated as learned/emerged.
  Modularity survives only as an intervention/interpretation affordance (a scramble-able W
  slot, a separable π), not as a performance property.

Both are the neural restatement of the standing organism lessons D2/D5/AC109; they do not
invalidate any promoted organism principle (P1–P7 stand).

### 5.4 The joint Phase-II + Phase-III teaching (the card's question)

Two consecutive neural levels, two verdicts, one shape. Stated so G17 can carry it:

1. **Sufficient-content availability is the load-bearing fact; the machinery around it is
   scaffolding.** Phase II stripped the explicit maintained self-state (V re-encodes the
   readable age `d_t`); Phase III stripped the shared maintained workspace (broadcast of
   the sufficient statistic suffices). What survives both is N1 (paid persistence as a
   necessary-condition substrate fact) plus the fact that the *content* — when it is a
   sufficient statistic — is what does the work, carried for free by a register/broadcast.
2. **The same collapse recurs across levels.** "Paid/learned/maintained X adds nothing
   over a sufficient-statistic or reactive rival" is now the program's signature at three
   sites: organism (D5 level-not-switch; AC109 same-info; D2 more-state-not-better),
   neural self-maintenance (D9/D10), neural workspace (D11/D12). It is not bad luck; it is
   a property of tasks whose relevant history compresses to a closed-form statistic.
3. **Sharing is load-bearing, narrowly and only vs private copies — and free once content
   is a sufficient statistic.** Not "sharing useless" (B) and not "private modules
   suffice" (E): sharing genuinely buys coordination (0 vs 0.014, p = 0.00049) and economy
   (1 vs 3 refresh targets) over R1. But a broadcast delivers it for free. So the answer
   to "shared representation?" is: the *content* is shared; the *machinery* is not needed.
4. **The card's question, answered.** Does consciousness-oriented architecture require
   expensive persistence / shared representation / explicit workspace / modular specialists
   / only sufficient-statistic availability / none of these yet? **Only
   sufficient-statistic availability survives as load-bearing.** Expensive paid persistence
   survives as a necessary-condition substrate fact (N1), not as an architectural feature;
   shared representation survives only as a free broadcast; explicit workspace and modular
   specialists are scaffolding (the monolith R2 and the broadcast R4/R5 both match/beat the
   candidate). This is **bounded** to the closed-form-statistic task; it does not settle
   whether an *acquired* (learned, non-closed-form) sufficient statistic carries the
   sharing properties — that is the one open forward question (see §12).

### 5.5 Re-scoping Phases IV–VII

- **Phase IV (N4)** and **Phase V (O1)** are unchanged as *designs* but inherit the
  re-scoping note: the "maintained representation" they build on is the N1
  paid-persistence substrate, and neither may assume an explicit self-state slot (D9), a
  learned adaptive allocator (D10), or workspace machinery (D11/D12) without re-deriving
  the assumption against a rival.
- **Phase VI (theory discrimination D1–D4)** — the D2 "bottleneck contrast" (GWT's seat)
  is now pre-loaded with a strong empirical prior: on a closed-form-statistic task the
  workspace/bottleneck machinery is scaffolding, so D2 as originally framed (does the
  k-slot bottleneck beat local recurrence on *closed-form* content) would be near-vacuous.
  D2 must be re-framed around **acquired** content — whether a bottleneck is load-bearing
  when the shared content is *not* supplied in closed form — before it is run. This is the
  same open question §5.4 names.
- **Phase VII (integration)** retains F1 as the standing terminal risk (AC82/83: paid
  capabilities do not compose unconditionally), now joined by a new risk: any integrated
  architecture whose content is a supplied sufficient statistic will collapse to a
  broadcast, so the terminal candidate must carry **acquired** content to be discriminable.
