## What survived

1. **Internal paid maintenance and bounded process-production closure.**
   The [closure verdict](CLOSURE_VERDICT_v1.md) and
   [integrated verdict](I5_INTEGRATED_ORGANIZATIONAL_VERDICT_v1.md) support modeled
   production/replacement and maintained-state dependencies under an explicit
   supplied substrate. AC115's mechanism composition is supported; its
   survival-level conjunction is not. Production is not invention of content.
2. **Content can causally matter without a novel storage architecture.**
   [AC108](AC108_RESULTS_v1.md) isolates maintenance -> estimate accuracy/use,
   and forced wrong content -> adaptation reversal. AC109's same-information
   direct diagnostic matches behavior while removing storage overhead.
   [AC116 plus errata](AC116_ERRATA_v1.md) preserves causal accumulation and
   cut-safety, but its income comparison is suspended at the sign-flip resolution
   floor, not proven equivalent and not a null mechanism verdict.
3. **Sufficient-content availability, not impressive implementation.**
   [Architectural principles](ACI_ARCHITECTURAL_PRINCIPLES_v1.md) and the frozen
   neural verdicts reduce explicit self-state, learned allocation and paid
   workspace machinery to observables, reactive control and sufficient-statistic
   broadcast. Generic content-sharing roles remain possible.
4. **Rivals and scope distinctions are load-bearing methodology.**
   Seed-level replication, exact replay, information parity, claim-matched
   falsifiers, negative controls and preserved corrections repeatedly change the
   interpretation. A valid audit can accompany a negative scientific result.

## ACO review: proposals, not enacted definition changes

N1 survives as a bounded persistence fact, not a paid-memory architectural
advantage. N2 should distinguish semantic content use from availability across
consumers. N3 should distinguish homeostasis, learned regulation and dependence
on constitutive organization. N4 remains unestablished and must be separable
from first-order uncertainty. N5 should distinguish reciprocal state/control
coupling from maintenance of enabling cognitive machinery.

These are review questions. ACO v1 is not silently amended and no ACO v2 is
declared. Any change needs its own human-approved version and intervention plan.

## Surprise review and next frontier

The largest recurring surprise is how little machinery survives strong rivals.
Costs and correctness separate; repeated contexts can be non-identifiable;
representation can matter while storage does not; repairing activity is not
maintaining its enabling organization.

The next active frontier is first-order cognitive identifiability, NOT another
workspace, a confidence head, or a self vector. The
[three-route audit](ACI_PRIOR_ART_AND_REDUCTION_v1.md) keeps predictive estimation,
constraint dynamics and local reconstruction distinct. The
[decision package](ACI_NEXT_EXPERIMENT_DECISION_v1.md) recommends a cheapest
discriminating prerequisite and explicitly separates design from authorization.
