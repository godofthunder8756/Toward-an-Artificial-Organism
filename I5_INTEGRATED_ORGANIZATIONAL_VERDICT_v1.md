## 0. The verdict, stated first

Three separate judgments, none standing in for another:

**(a) Production closure (M&V clause (i), the adopted finite criterion) — SUPPORTED.**
Unchanged from K3, and now *inherited by composition rather than re-derived*: the
integrated successor reproduces the AC105 five-mechanism architecture byte-for-byte at the
intact boundary (G1), so the C1–C5 verdict over {W, C, B, description, program} and the
S1–S4 verdict over {pointer, coordination, route memory, decision state} carry forward
intact. The integration adds a role, not a component, and changes no production edge.

**(b) Composition of the six integrated functions — SUPPORTED at the mechanism level;
NOT CONFIRMED at the survival level.** The six functions — exchange admission, retention,
renewal (boundary production/turnover), reconstruction, succession, paid updates — are
present AND actually exercised in the SAME organisms (the `keep` arm, 16/16), with the
admission discriminations (G2 link-specific, G3 local) and the single-change license (G1)
uniform. This closes the previously-open gap (I0/I1): the exchange role rides the produced
boundary that the five-mechanism organism already maintains. But the four gates that
prespecified a survival/completion clause (G4–G7) FAIL on a minority of finals and are
RETAINED as failed — a seed-dependent transfer failure (AC68 W/C bimodality, AC39), not a
mechanism failure.

**(c) Whole-organism unity (M&V clause (ii)) — NOT ESTABLISHED.** The produced unity is a
unity of the **material (constituent) layer** only: a produced, semipermeable, self-renewing
perimeter. The whole organization (material layer + informational core) does not constitute
itself as a concrete unity in space, because the informational core is non-spatial (§5).
This is the sole remaining genuine clause-(ii) limitation, and it is a finite successor
question, not a permanently untestable fact (§6).

Neither part implies consciousness; levels (c)/(d)/(e) are untouched. The strongest wording
licensed is unchanged from the charter's ceiling, with the exchange role now shown to
compose with the informational layer.

- **G1 (single-change license) — 16/16.** `keep`/`rival`/`reference` are byte-identical

  (corruption applied, `fw_at_corrupt == 8`, `successions == 6`, `flipped_still_wrong == 0`).
  The admission gate adds no resource competition and disturbs none of the five mechanisms
  when inert. This is exact byte equality, not a margin.
- **G2 (D1 link-specific) — 16/16.** Under an identical puncture, `puncture` (site) zeroes
  channel-0 alive-window admission while `rival_puncture` (count) admits — SR-2 has not
  collapsed into SR-1.
- **G3 (D2 local) — 16/16.** A non-gate puncture leaves both channels admitting — the
  dependence is local, not global.
- **The six functions are actually exercised in the same body** (the `keep` arm, 16/16):
  reconstruction `fw 8→0` (recovery 8193–8202), 6 successions with `desc 130/130`, 2

  `ctrl` 1672–1689 writes), boundary production (`B_births` 1674–1679, export 0 on 15/16).
  These are mechanism records (each (b) claim backed by its firing record), not endpoint
  agreement.

So the composition claim that is now licensed, at the mechanism level: **one organism renews
its produced exchange boundary while the admission gate is byte-inert at the intact boundary

## 4. What the produced boundary constitutes and sustains

The boundary component B is produced (action 8, W-anchored, program-selected, 2 M + 2 E),
finite-lived (decays every tick, ~10× turnover vs a 20-link complement), and it now carries
**two coupled causal roles**:

- **Retention (outward barrier).** The 20 perimeter links retain the produced W and C
  constituents inside the bounded interior; export is irreversible (a crossing to the bath is
  death). Retention is load-bearing and separable from mass (the rescue controls identify the
  function, they do not refute it — A2).
- **Exchange admission (inward gate).** The *same* produced links admit the material/fuel
  intake that funds all production (SR-2: intake iff the channel's gate link is live). A dead
  interface is strictly costly, which is what makes its renewal load-bearing.

Assembled (A4, re-shown in the integrated body): **B → (retention + exchange) → production → B**
is the material-layer closure cycle, established by ablation (no_B, no_B_retention, permeant,
B_rescue), not asserted. The integrated successor extends this by showing the cycle composes
with the informational layer — killing B production kills both entry and retention (G4/G5
discriminations hold in every individual), and the admission discriminations hold *through*
active reconstruction and succession (G7's mechanism portion holds in every failing individual).

## 6. Production closure supported vs. whole-organism unity unestablished

**Supported (clause (i)).** Every necessary component {W, C, B, description, program} meets
C1–C5 and every maintained-state item {pointer, coordination, route memory, decision state}
meets S1–S4, in the AC105 architecture, under the accepted substrate convention (J1 resolved
substrate). The integrated successor inherits this verbatim and adds the composition of the
boundary-exchange role with it. The single strongly-connected network has no external root.

**Not established (clause (ii)).** The whole organization is the material layer *plus* the
non-spatial informational core (§5). Clause (ii) — "constitute the system as a concrete unity
in space" — is met only at the material layer (a produced, semipermeable, self-renewing
perimeter). It is not met for the whole organism, and the reason is the non-spatial
informational core, which is the **sole** remaining genuine limitation after the I1 corrections
(supplied space/geometry is permitted substrate; the substitution fact is struck).

On the constraint against calling this permanently untestable: the non-spatial controller is
**not** permanently untestable. It is a finite successor question — addressable by a successor
architecture that realizes the controller spatially (positions it and subjects it to the same
transport/retention the constituents face). It is out of the current line's scope (a
re-architecture of the supplied physics, not a run inside the frozen model), but nothing here
forecloses it. It is recorded as a modeling limitation of *this* architecture, not as a
standing impossibility.

never folded into an admission gate.

A successor study that wants a pure-mechanism gate (admission discrimination without the
completion clause) must write a new protocol on fresh seeds; AC115's G4–G7 stay failed.

## 9. Disposition (unblocks S0)

The integrated successor establishes, at the mechanism level and on untouched seeds: the
exchange role composes with the five-mechanism closure in one organism, the produced boundary
is the site of both retention and exchange admission (material-layer semipermeability), and

establish whole-organism spatial unity (the non-spatial informational core is the sole
remaining genuine clause-(ii) limitation, a finite successor question), and it does not

failed gates, not moved). Production closure (clause (i)) stands SUPPORTED and unchanged.

SUPPORTED at the mechanism level only; clause (ii) NOT ESTABLISHED, limited solely by the
