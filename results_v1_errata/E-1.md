# E-1. Claim assembly

The frozen results file is unchanged. This erratum replaces the published claim paragraph. No network was retrained.

## Adopted paragraph

In W2, recurrent training produced a GRU-8 core whose predictive advantage over its matched initialization survived replacement and retraining of the readout in 10/10 fresh seeds. Hidden-to-hidden matrix reversion did not meet the prespecified joint criterion for any matrix. Advantage over the selected tuned frozen GRU-8 with 843 trainable readout parameters was observed in 10/10 seeds. That rival met A-FIT in 0/10 seeds within the declared grid. Selective variation reduction was not demonstrated under the gated criterion.

This is the earned headline. The stronger, preregistered matrix-specific claim is not earned.

## Formatting bug in the frozen assembler

`assemble_claim` writes `Tuned-rival results: %s. %s (%d/10).` and fills the second slot with the bare constant `not met`. The label being negated is never named. The variation sentence is then appended with no final period. The recorded claim therefore ends:

> Tuned-rival results: ADVANTAGE OVER TUNED FROZEN GRU-8 (843 TRAINABLE) OBSERVED. not met (0/10). Variation results: r: selective variation reduction not demonstrated; z: selective variation reduction not demonstrated; n: selective variation reduction not demonstrated

`recurrent_core_transfer_verdicts.assemble_claim` is left unchanged. `assemble_claim_v2` is a new wrapper. On these inputs it names the rival label and closes the paragraph:

> Tuned-rival results: ADVANTAGE OVER TUNED FROZEN GRU-8 (843 TRAINABLE) OBSERVED. TUNED FROZEN GRU-8 MEETS A-FIT AT THIS BUDGET: not met (0/10). Variation results: r: selective variation reduction not demonstrated; z: selective variation reduction not demonstrated; n: selective variation reduction not demonstrated.

The wrapper does not emit the adopted paragraph above. That paragraph is the reviewer's presentation, including the 10/10 counts and the grid qualifier. The wrapper only repairs the two formatting defects.
