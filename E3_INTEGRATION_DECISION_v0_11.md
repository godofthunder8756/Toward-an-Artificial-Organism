## Decision

Accept the integrated specification for engineering-only implementation and
conformance work after its design snapshot is verified. Do not launch the full
final experiment or draw final targets. The current design does not establish
the stronger H2 premise that the material budget forces abandonment of some
code regions. An affordable conventional counterexample already refutes its
universal financial interpretation. Script success cannot reverse that result.

This is a scientific stopping rule for the current confirmatory interpretation,
not a declaration that the long-term research goal has been reached or that
all possible models have been exhausted. There is no E3 empirical result, new
motivation, demonstrated organismal autonomy or subjective-experience evidence.
The user authorized sustained autonomous work; no extra routine permission is
needed for the allowed engineering stage. Authorization does not waive tests,
either freeze, or the scientific limitations below.

All task state is managed through the .copilot-tracking folder files. The
[current plan](.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-09-10/e3-b02-plan.md) records progress,
every tracking artifact, percentages, reviews and ordered remaining work.

## Scientific conclusions and corrections

B02 pricing and lifetime questions are resolved as an abstract design with
reviewed finite witnesses. HS-AC supply bounds pay both codes' service paths;
they do not establish accurate memory. Required power/material support is
external. The fixed executor, physics, decoder, clock and code routing remain
protected generic machinery. E3a remains a conventional benchmark, not E3b.

The canonical-empty erasure design has a conditional sufficient containment
assurance bound of at least 0.996306 per representation under its selected
independent inputs and conforming funding. This is not an observed test pass,
a generator-security proof, or a claim for arbitrary reset-output dependence.
Primary n remains 32 with eight panels; no favorable analysis seed or sample
increase is selected after outcomes.

The clean-parent repetition reference leaves at most about 0.0164 expected
adaptive advantage over a competent I=1 periodic policy under the reviewed
assumptions. That is below the proposed 0.05 adaptive gate. It is not a theorem
for every trained parent or every finite sample, and does not eliminate a
narrower paid-maintenance benefit over no-code-write storage.

For H2, fixed/frozen repetition requires at most 28+5=33 material per source
for full conditioning plus maximal correction, below g=36. Thus the claim that
this budget universally forces sacrifice of obsolete code is already refuted
financially. Learned-bookkeeping scarcity and automatic-conditioner contention
are different mechanisms. Even SCRIPT-U success paired with SCRIPT-ALL failure
cannot show that no conventional policy retains all regions. The current
confirmatory H2 interpretation and full final launch are NO-GO. A future
confirmatory claim requires separately reviewed prospective scope/design,
not automatic promotion after scripts or retuning to a desired outcome.

Retain the selected bounded engineering scripts as diagnostics and retain every
failure. Positive performance is not required to freeze a falsifiable
engineering design. Technical conformance is required before interpreting any
run; script/plant feasibility requirements precede any future final launch.
Do not weaken a rival, change g from its diagnostic grid, or change thresholds
to erase the counterexample.

Adopt the reviewed qualifications explicitly:

* AN-001: conditioning protects mandatory TERMINAL clearing, not the optional
  last TD update. That update may reject after conditioning; count actual work.
* AN-002: the illustrative H2 normal-planning probabilities at paired SD20/40
  are 0.619/0.204, not 0.611/0.198. They are not measured or exact bootstrap power.
* POL-001: missing responses receive zero whereas useful wrong emissions
  receive -64. This creates a potential avoidance incentive, not evidence that
  avoidance is feasible, learned or optimal. Keep missing/emitted-error rates
  separate and use planned-denominator recall/viability gates independently.
* POL-002: gamma=15/16 has a 16-tick discount scale and about 10.74-tick half-life.
  It need not optimize distant retention. H1 supplies no task feedback; offline
  engineering selection is not hidden online teaching.
* POL-003: engineering targets can inform fitted population constants. Freeze
  those constants before independent final target draws. Reset counterfactuals
  hold G and future inputs fixed, not retuned separately for each history.
