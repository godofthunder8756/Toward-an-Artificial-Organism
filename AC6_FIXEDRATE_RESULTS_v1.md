# Fixed rates explain local behavioral success, but not net material savings

2026-09-15. All40 prospective conditions completed and audited. Goal active.

## Main finding

Fixed exploration probabilities1/1024 and1/2048 pass all original AC6 behavioral
criteria on eight new seeds. Thus experience-dependent adjustment of exploration
is not necessary to explain viable local relinquishment and reacquisition in
this environment. The commitment itself still changes from crossing feedback;
these are fixed exploration laws, not entirely nonlearning systems.

No tested rate achieves the additional net-material-efficiency criterion.

more material than frozen in every seed. All four spending-difference intervals
are positive. Intervals bootstrap paired seeds,10000 resamples; these are small,
descriptive engineering comparisons, not corrected confirmatory inference.

The protocol's net-material criterion is additional to AC6's behavioral gates.
Do not retroactively classify the intermediate behavioral successes as failures
of the original gates, or mistake them for passing the added efficiency gate.

## Implication for the research direction

This closes a simpler alternative explanation before adding learned probe
regulation. More controller state is not justified merely to cross the behavioral
threshold that fixed-rate controls already meet. A future regulator must show
an additional benefit under declared conditions, such as generalization across
unknown support durations or improved resource use after paying its own costs.
Equal-probe-count schedules remain an untested comparator if that line proceeds.

Material savings are not themselves the definition of autonomy. A living
maintenance network may pay for exploration. Conversely, a cheaper fixed policy
does not erase the observed paid, feedback-dependent revision of an acquired
commitment. Keep economic efficiency, learned regulation and organizational
maintenance as separate claims.

The more substantive open target is acquiring a maintenance dependency absent
from demonstrations, rather than repeatedly optimizing one engineered link's
probe schedule. The next design should identify an experience-acquired mapping
that becomes causally necessary for producing/retaining enabling constituents,
with no protected copy or external correct-action teacher. This is a design
requirement, not an implemented result. The same maintained organization must
support its acquisition and later preservation; dead training histories must
not be silently discarded or restarted.

The current local site, crossing-response direction, interpreter, scaffold and
reaction laws remain supplied. These results do not establish new-need discovery,
rich development, intrinsic normativity, full autopoiesis or subjectivity.
