Verdict: FREEZE WITH NAMED EDITS for the oracle-only package. This does not clear checkpoint inference or establish a complete neural acceptance protocol.

The revised scope substantially follows #35. The remaining corrections concern the mathematical interpretation of the losses, validity and pooling, fixture expectations, and the timing sequence. Nothing needs a new human approval under the stated standing authorization.

1. Q1: Conformity with E35-1..12

The eps_syn rename correctly separates synthetic-coordinate edits from native GRU-state edits. Keep that distinction throughout the manifest, output fields and report.

Three qualifications remain:

- Oracle H* does not certify neural feasibility. The checkpoint's L_mu is an upper bound on its possible improvement, not its "achievable gain."
- D-5 changes the loss estimand: predictions are assessed conditional on a common valid-edit subset. That is defensible, but the subset and retained probability mass must be explicit. Invalid edits cannot silently disappear from coverage accounting.
- Section 6 contains commitments for a later study. Its presence does not authorize that study. In particular, neural effect magnitude, numerical boundaries and complete outcome accounting still need to be settled before checkpoint inspection.

There is also a notation correction. Phi is a ratio of linear functions of the four joint-state probabilities. It is not generally a ratio of linear functions of the covariance chart jointly, because the inverse chart contains mu_x*mu_y terms.

2. Q2: H* and the oracle thresholds

Yes. H* is the appropriate comparator loss for this synthetic-control exercise: it uses the separately fitted fixed-kappa comparator and held-out responses. Because the exact full control has zero prediction error mathematically, H* equals its ideal gain; the implementation should report the measured gain H* minus measured full-model loss as well.

Accept the S5 thresholds as declared conventions:

```text
ALL:  sqrt(H*) >= 1e-3 and R* >= 0.05
MARG: sqrt(H*) >= 1e-4 and R* >= 0.02
```

They describe resolvability under the specified synthetic embedding, directions, scale and comparator. They are not intrinsic thresholds of W2, neural sensitivity guarantees, or evidence that a later study will pass.

Keep eps_syn = 0.1 as the stated oracle reference. Report the other sizes without allowing them to replace a failed reference.

The validity criterion must apply to both predictions and the control's actual perturbed belief. Report coverage by fold, including law-mass coverage. A large count of valid low-probability edits must not conceal loss of most of the evaluated probability mass.

3. Q3: Accuracy floors, quota, precedence and edit size

Keep L_full <= L_mu/2. Do not tighten it to one quarter on the supplied argument.

The claimed decomposition into shared error E_s and separate kappa errors K and e has not been established. With separately fitted maps and nonlinear finite predictions, residual components can have cross terms. If

```text
r_mu = actual_response - Pred_mu
d    = Pred_full - Pred_mu
```

then the exact identity is

```text
L_mu - L_full = 2*<r_mu,d> - ||d||^2
```

under the scoring weights. There is no automatic nonnegative "shared error plus kappa error" decomposition.

Therefore, the half-loss rule means halving the named comparator's total prediction error. It does not mean that at most 71% of a separately identified kappa-component RMS error remains.

Together with L_full/S <= 0.25, it is an acceptable, deliberately modest relative-prediction criterion, subject to substantive headroom and response-magnitude requirements. The latter condition permits relative RMS error up to one half of the response RMS. Do not describe that as precise recovery of covariance.

The 8/10 seed quota is acceptable as a descriptive replication requirement. Folds are repeated evaluations of overlapping prefixes, not independent replications. No binomial significance interpretation follows.

Revise the package precedence. Three headroom-limited seeds must not conceal several integrity-unresolved seeds, and unresolved comparisons must not silently become failures. Report the disjoint counts explicitly and use outcome bounds as specified below.

Finally, eps = 0.1 and 0.25 do not establish departure from the linear regime. A network may be nearly affine over both, or strongly nonlinear below either. The finite-response test is still useful, but its relation to the Jacobian approximation is an empirical diagnostic.

4. Q4: Add the substantive neural headroom ratio

Yes: use L_mu/S >= 0.02 for both ALL and MARG, assessed at the same pooled level as the primary loss comparison.

The rationale is transparent: combined with removal of at least half the comparator loss, it requires a gain of at least 0.01*S. This prevents a pass based solely on halving an arbitrarily small fraction of response energy. It is a convention, not a consequence of the oracle R* threshold; the numerical match to R*_MARG supplies no theorem.

This still does not prevent success on arbitrarily tiny absolute responses. The later inference specification must also declare an absolute response-magnitude floor in forecast units. Do not borrow the oracle H* threshold as though it already supplied that neural floor.

Zero response energy makes the normalized ratios undefined. It is a low-sensitivity outcome, not a pass, a division-by-zero workaround, or an implementation failure.

Apply the substantive headroom condition to the pooled seed-by-size statistic. Report foldwise headroom descriptively. Requiring every fold separately to clear it would introduce an additional worst-fold criterion that is not the pooled primary question.
