Q2. Accept the protective-rejection event and the conditional Route B derivation.

The protective-rejection definition now measures the intended operational event: an eligible, finite assessment withholds the rbar fidelity certificate. Excluding unavailable assessments, non-finite computations, constant-readout flags and failures on other nuisances prevents those cases from manufacturing successful protection.

The all-200-replicate denominator is correct. Keep the reported stronger event, lower bound above the tolerance, separate from protective rejection. The CP requirement and complete tier-3 false-licence requirement retain their previously declared, bounded operating-characteristic interpretation.

For Route B, the coupling argument works as follows.

While the two trajectories have identical histories, suppose their step-j action distributions differ in total variation by at most:

    c_pi * kappa_j * a

A coupling then bounds the probability of divergence by step k by:

    c_pi * a * sum_(j <= k) kappa_j

Because each scored indicator lies in [0,1], and G1 makes the score identical while the relevant histories remain identical, averaging the ten indicator differences gives:

    |E B(PL) - E B(d)|
        <= (c_pi / H) * sum_(k=1..H) sum_(j<=k) kappa_j * a

For H = 10, c_pi = 0.3 and every kappa_j = 1:

    C_H = (0.3 / 10) * 55 = 1.65

Applying the triangle inequality to the low- and high-donor contrasts gives the stated bound:

    |E Delta_ATTR,i| <= 1.65 * (a_L,i + a_H,i)

This proof does not require the evaluation's particular CRN implementation to be a maximal coupling. The coupling establishes an expectation bound; the observed CRN divergence frequency should not be substituted for the total-variation bound.

One premise must be verified explicitly in KR-ID/P1: beta/4 bounds the full action law only for the appropriate action construction. It directly bounds a Bernoulli sigmoid decision. It also suffices for a policy of the form:

    pi_g = p(g) * K_approach + (1 - p(g)) * K_other

when both conditional action kernels are independent of g given the common history.

It does not follow from the approach probability alone if g also changes which particular approach or non-approach action is selected. That would require the full kernel's bound. Likewise, G3 must hold conditional on the common history, rather than only on average across trajectories.

These are checks of the declared premises, not requests for another plant or study arm.

The gate should retain its unrounded form:

    1.65 * U_A < 0.05

The equivalent threshold is 1/33. U_A must concern the same weighting and conditioning used for the scored endpoint.

Route B establishes qualification relative to the margin, not exact zero. Its gate is a statistical qualification using the declared upper-bound procedure; it does not turn that procedure into a deterministic guarantee or certify its nominal coverage. Route I supplies the exact-zero statement when its analytic premises hold.

Blocking REW-CORR until its full action law and update behavior are confirmed is correct. A name or similarity to the pure plant is not sufficient to inherit its constant.

Q3. Accept the numerical edge-case rules.

The rules now distinguish:

- A defined positive-scale ratio.
- A genuinely unchanged constant readout.
- A nonzero change against a zero scale.
- An undefined or non-finite construction.

The constant-readout flag cannot grant a fidelity certificate or contribute a protective-rejection success. That is conservative and closes the vacuous-pass case.

Defining s_(r+1) = 0 when the retained rank exhausts the available singular directions is correct. The rank-zero wording also correctly distinguishes an empty fitted nuisance span from demonstrated nuisance removal.

The explicit exceptions take precedence over the generic 0/0 convention: an undefined controller normalization is a plant construction failure, and a required A-EXT unit with no invocations fails its precision requirement.

Stopping the affected A-EXT evaluation on non-finite values, preserving its incomplete accounting and returning UNRESOLVED is appropriate. Such failures must not disappear through clipping, imputation or a reduced denominator.

Q4. Accept the L8 separation.

The sequence is now appropriate:

    Frozen states -> qualification Q -> frozen qualifying list
    -> independent scoring S -> return checks

Qualification may use termination outcomes because the subsequent return evaluation uses independent randomness. Q returns are not used for selection or scoring.

CRN pairing within Q and within S remains useful; the two allocations themselves must remain independent. The conclusion is conditional on the declared qualification procedure and resulting state set. It does not become a claim about all 200 states or every possible failure strategy.

The rollout arithmetic is correct:

- At most 200 states x 50 pairs x 2 trajectories = 20,000 qualification rollouts.
- At most another 20,000 scoring rollouts.
- At most 40,000 rollouts for one complete 200-state L8 audit.

The ledger must multiply that amount if the authorized package contains multiple such audits. It is not automatically a package-wide total.

Coverage failure remains INCONCLUSIVE and blocks audit approval. It is not evidence that a premature-failure incentive exists. The per-state estimate screen remains correctly distinguished from the mean statistical check.

The classification now follows causal structure rather than code availability:

- A causal alias has no distinct variable to intervene on.
- A distinct non-routing node is not relabelled as an alias.
- A distinct router remains a comparator, including a stateless or deterministic-copy router.
- An unavailable intervention remains a blocking status on the required comparator.

For this classification, an �own consumer path� means a consumer path that does not pass through W. This is the counterpart of the stated non-routing definition.

The R_core/R_T3 selection rule also preserves the intended dependency:

- R_core determines the common sample size.
- R_T3 is assessed at that fixed size.
- A calibration-blocked tier cannot be restored by a later diagnostic.
- Calibration success still requires fresh confirmation success.
- Lower-tier reporting does not imply full-battery success.

Deriving Final from the separate calibration and confirmation fields is preferable to allowing a manually entered final status.
