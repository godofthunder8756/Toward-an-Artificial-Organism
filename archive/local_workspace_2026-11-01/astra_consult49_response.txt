Q1. GA-1 is technically defensible as a conditional control, with explicit limits.

The pinned construction addresses the earlier objection. Independent, controller-unused eta coordinates do not deliberately introduce a nonlinear reward feature into the block. With centered population regressions, independence from the sampled targets and a nonzero E-probe coefficient on zeta, the E probe lies in the zeta direction, which the nuisance readout spans. This supports the population statement. It does not imply finite-sample degeneracy. If the population E probe itself is zero, the appropriate statement is an undefined residual ratio, rather than rho_E = 0.

The corrected distinctions are appropriate:

- A degenerate cell has no attribution statistic: UNDEFINED.
- "Did not issue MET" records an output event, not a measured negative effect.
- A qualified replicate computes the declared statistic from its qualified cells.
- Insufficient qualified support cannot certify the nondegenerate branch.

The 36-replicate arithmetic is correct:

    CP upper(0/36) = 1 - 0.05^(1/36) = 0.0798465
    CP upper(0/35) = 0.0820316

However, 36 is only the smallest denominator that can meet the 0.08 upper-bound criterion with zero false licences. It is not an automatic pass at 36; nonzero counts must still satisfy both frozen criteria.

The conditional assessment must use all qualified replicates from the prespecified 200, not the first or best 36. Qualification must not depend on whether the attribution label is MET. Route and geometry checks may determine qualification, but the resulting operating characteristic concerns that qualified branch.

Q4. Confirm the generator and transform design; accept the KAT status as reported verification.

Philox4x64-10 is a specific counter-based generator, resolving the earlier family-level ambiguity. Random123 provides deterministic outputs indexed by keys and counters. [GitHub](https://github.com/DEShawResearch/random123/blob/main/README.md?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The specified transform gives:

    u1 in (0, 1]
    u2 in [0, 1)

    normal = sqrt(-2 log(u1)) * cos(2 pi u2)

Thus log(0) is excluded. Keeping the cosine output only is acceptable; there must be no hidden cached sine output that makes later draws depend on call order.

The remaining address detail is s in counter = (t, s, 0, 0). Define it. For the single REW-CORR perturbation per step, s = 0 is sufficient. If it indexes additional draws, fix its meaning, range and word allocation. It must never become a branch ID, donor ID or call-order counter. Preserve the inherited recipient root and absolute-step rule.

Integer Philox known-answer vectors require exact word equality. The floating normal transform has a separate libm/tolerance check. The three reported matches support the core implementation; they do not by themselves validate encoding, stream separation or the transformed outputs. Your additional encoding checks and planned audits address those separate obligations.

The master-seed rule is acceptable:

- Hash the exact registered calibration-spec bytes.
- Derive and record the seed outside those bytes, avoiding circularity.
- Register one specification and preserve its provenance.
- Do not run candidate study seeds before registration or silently replace the registered seed later.

This provides reproducibility. It does not cryptographically prevent someone from searching specification variants before registration.

The public beacon is optional. Use it if independent unpredictability is a governance objective; it is not required to repair this design, and I would not add it as another freeze blocker.
