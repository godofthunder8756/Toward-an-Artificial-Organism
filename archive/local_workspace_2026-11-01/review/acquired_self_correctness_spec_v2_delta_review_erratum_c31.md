# Erratum to the CNA delta review of spec v2 (after Astra #31)

2026-10-21, ~6:20 PM ET. This is a new file. The delta review (sha 488f70254db2d5cc10b70d2214e775557c2b89cf801b3dafdfef37cbcb40728c) is unchanged.

2. Withdrawn: in §4 point 4, the suggestion to choose fault strength from a centered-variance target on Δ^F declared in advance. With D^F already observed, that is still selection on a closely related tested quantity (Astra #31 L55).
3. Withdrawn: §1's statement that M1–M5 and m1–m7 are all adequately resolved. Astra #31 found independent blockers that I did not identify: the K3 rescue sign, CF2 double counting, acquisition credit, and the causal-interpretation gap.
4. Unaffected: the reproduced numbers (D^F, the centered RMS, the offset share, the per-pair LRs and the CON n_eff) and the recommendation not to demote CF1a.
