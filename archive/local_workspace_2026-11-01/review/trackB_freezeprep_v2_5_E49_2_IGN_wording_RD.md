## 5. Replacement text, item 4: the pooled test (RD; justification stated carefully)

> **E49-2 (4) Pooled floor (replaces CE F-4 (a) "The CP floor stays valid …" as the stated justification, and the "exact" / "binomial" description of the pooled count wherever it appears).** The per-(replicate, required bin) rule x ≥ x_min(N) is a **conservative fixed-threshold eligibility test for the average conditional eligibility probability p̄ across the N scheduled final-partition trials** (A49 L75). It is **not** an exact binomial test: the pooled count is a sum of independent Bernoulli trials with possibly different conditional probabilities (Poisson-binomial), not a binomial count, and the rule does **not** establish exact Clopper–Pearson coverage at every parameter value (A49 L75). Its claim is one-sided and conditional: **if p̄ ≤ 0.90, then P(x ≥ x_min(N)) ≤ 0.05**, conditional on the frozen setup (§3).
>
> **Justification.** Hoeffding (1956), "On the distribution of the number of successes in independent trials", Ann. Math. Statist. 27(4):713–721, doi:10.1214/aoms/1177728178, Theorem 4 (as cited by CE F-4 and EK): for independent trials S with E S = N·p̄ fixed, if c ≥ N·p̄ then P(S ≤ c) ≥ P(Bin(N, p̄) ≤ c). Taking c = x_min − 1, the condition is x_min ≥ N·p̄ + 1, and then P(S ≥ x_min) ≤ P(Bin(N, p̄) ≥ x_min). Every frozen pooled schedule satisfies **x_min ≥ 0.9·N + 1** (CE §3.1; V2.3 §3; re-checked here against CE §3.1's x_min column for all nine GRID values), so for any p̄ ≤ 0.90 the condition holds, and because the binomial upper tail is nondecreasing in p, P(Bin(N, p̄) ≥ x_min) ≤ P(Bin(N, 0.90) ≥ x_min) ≤ 0.05. This is the sense of A49 L75: "supports the fixed upper-tail test at 0.90 when its rejection threshold is sufficiently above the mean. The stated x_min >= 0.9N + 1 is the relevant condition." Two limits stay explicit. (a) The comparison needs independence across all N pooled trials conditional on the frozen setup; it gives nothing for dependence created by a joint gate (§4 (3b)(iii)). (b) The tabulated floor-pass probabilities at e ≥ 0.93 (CE §3.1) are planning figures; at true eligibility 0.90 the floor is expected to withhold the certificate most of the time (A49 L62).
>
> **Scope of the certificate.** A pass certifies eligibility for the **scheduled, conditional trial population** of that replicate and bin only, not for new seeds or trained agents; the existing claim ceiling governs that distinction (A49 L137).

Source note: the theorem's content (Theorem 4 gives the maximum and minimum of P(S ≤ c) for fixed E S; Theorem 5 is its corollary) is confirmed from the paper's published abstract (Project Euclid / doi above). The regime condition "c ≥ N·p̄" was taken from a secondary source (a public formalization of the theorem's regimes), because RD did not read the full paper text this pass. **PENDING CNA:** confirm the regime statement against the paper's p. 718 before freeze.
