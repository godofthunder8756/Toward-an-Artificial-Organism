The amended row therefore needs separate conditions for:

- Plant realization.
- Qualified support and assessment availability.
- False-licence performance.

A high realization fraction cannot substitute for the latter two.

(b) Split BOUNDARY from UNRESOLVED.

A valid BOUNDARY result is a computed non-licence. For the literal event "the instrument issues MET", it belongs in the denominator and contributes zero to the numerator.

An UNRESOLVED result is an unavailable assessment. Report it separately. Excluding it from a conditional rate is permissible only alongside an explicit availability condition or conservative assessment.

The decisive-only rate n5/(n4+n5) may remain a diagnostic, but it estimates false licensing conditional on obtaining a decisive result. It is not the overall false-licence rate.

Counting valid BOUNDARY results as adverse events is deliberately conservative. Describe that check as a combined specificity/informativeness screen, not as the observed false-licence rate.

That binding screen already limits the row-4b fraction: row-4b cases are a subset of its adverse events. A separate arbitrary cap is unnecessary. Its permitted count follows from the actual denominator and the two acceptance conditions.

(d) Yes: the conservative denominator is n4+n4b+n5. The >=36 rule concerns that assessment's denominator; 36 permits acceptance only with a sufficiently small adverse count. Row-0 licences remain immediate failures.

(e) Reject E2-4 as written. "NOT ASSESSED passes only when n4b = 0" still allows no assessment at all to pass.

Use:

Q4. Accept the reset construction; the scalar noise and numerical definitions need correction.

For scalar plants, resetting each trial to the declared deterministic x_lo and setting x_ref = x_lo is coherent. Removing a stochastic-settling exclusion from these plants is appropriate. This defines a reset experiment, not a stationary-state experiment.

The half-normal calculations require Gaussian displacement assumptions. A nonlinear noisy system does not generally have an exactly Gaussian stationary distribution. Do not present the quoted exclusion fractions as universal consequences of the nonlinear plant.

The proposed network quantile gate is defensible, with a finite-sample specification. An empirical 0.99 quantile does not guarantee exactly 1% exclusion on new runs.

A concrete prospective choice is:

- Fit and freeze the center using a separate portion of fitting data.
- Generate 1,000 independent null runs per applicable seed/network setting for threshold estimation.
- Use the 990th ordered distance as the threshold, with a fixed tie rule.
- Do not use these runs again as final eligibility observations.

Conditional on the fixed center and a continuous distance distribution, the probability that this threshold covers less than 97% of that same null distribution is:

    P[Binomial(1000, 0.97) >= 990] = approximately 0.00001813.

This is a per-threshold statement, not a simultaneous guarantee over the battery. Order-statistic tolerance calculations distinguish estimated quantiles from coverage guarantees. [search.r-project.org](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/EnvStats/html/tolIntNparCoverage.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The number of center-fitting runs must also be declared and costed. The final eligibility gates remain operative; a broad tolerance cannot rescue a collapsed workspace or an absent response.

The main scalar defect is:

    sigma = 0.05 * |x_hi - x_lo|

IGN-N1 is monostable. It has no distinct high and low attractors from which to obtain this scale. Setting their difference to zero would silently remove its noise.

Pin a positive reference separation from a declared bistable reference condition and use that frozen reference scale for the matched negative and diagnostic conditions. State the reference input and parameters. Do not recompute the noise scale from each probed input where attractors may merge or disappear.

The SDE and Euler-Maruyama update are mutually consistent:

    tau dx = f(x) dt + sigma sqrt(tau) dW

    x_next = x + (dt/tau) f(x)
               + sigma sqrt(dt/tau) xi.

The proposed step rule gives integer readout indices for 2tau, 3tau and 5tau. Accept it as a prospective discretization convention. It does not establish numerical accuracy or prove bimodality. Either define the discrete generator as the tested plant, or separately justify any claim about its continuous-time counterpart.

For IGN-N1, the unique fixed-point definition is correct for g < 1, but direct fixed-point iteration is not guaranteed to converge for every negative g. Restrict the intended range, for example 0 <= g < 1, or specify a suitable bracketed root solver. Include a termination bound and failure status. Excluding g = 1 alone does not resolve arbitrarily slow convergence near it.

The readout-time grids are acceptable. Selection among them must use selection data; final outcomes cannot choose the winning time.

Q5. Confirm the RNG design and modulo arithmetic.

For a uniform 64-bit word reduced modulo m, the bucket probabilities are slightly unequal unless m divides 2^64. Your bound is correct:

    Relative deviation <= 508 / 2^64
                       = 2.7538735e-17.

I independently found the maximum over m = 2,...,508:

    488 / 2^64 = 2.6454533e-17, at m = 504.

Thus the fixed-draw permutation is acceptable as a negligibly biased assignment, not an exactly uniform Fisher-Yates permutation. Exact partition sizes are unaffected.

Confirm:

- 507 fixed draws.
- No outcome-dependent reassignment.
- Unsigned big-endian DOSE0 = 2^64-1, reserved from ordinary dose indices.
- A DOSE0 address KAT.
- Separate roles and fixed counter-word allocations.
- Exact integer KAT equality, with floating-transform checks handled separately.

Philox4x64 has a four-word counter and two-word 64-bit key, consistent with this layout. [random123.com](https://random123.com/releases/1.09/docs/philox_8h.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The partition-generating key must be formed before partition membership exists. Give that role a fixed partition-field sentinel if the common encoding requires such a field. This avoids a circular address definition.
