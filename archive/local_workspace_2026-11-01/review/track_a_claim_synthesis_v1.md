# Track A claim synthesis v1 (one page)

Paper only, 2026-10-21. Sources: triage v1.2; the U1 audit (sha256 `48c174f5…f13a`); Astra #25 and #26. C1–C5 are Definitions Charter v2 criteria, not C-axis levels.

**Closure sentence (verbatim from the U1 audit §5)**

> Within the declared model and operating range, and with interpretation, decoding, copy, comparison, write, erase, majority-restore, the W-catalysed affordability gate, action execution, observation and conservation as substrate, the production of W, C and B by program-selected actions executes declared, inherited content and meets C1–C5. The replacement of the description (succession) and the reconstruction and repair of the program and the maintained registers do not meet the rule. They are sequenced, triggered and scheduled by supplied, organization-specific coordination: `maintain()`'s schedule and triggers; `advance()`'s fixed copy→verify→switch→remove order, start condition and timer coupling; the regeneration trigger, budget and exclusion policy; and the relinquishment policy. The organism's maintained state holds only the current phase, pointer, counter and streak values that this coordination reads. Production closure of the full network {W, C, B, description, derived program} therefore holds only conditional on that external coordination, and is not established under this rule.

**What Track A can claim, by Q-tag**
- **Q-EXIST:** the bounded, conditional closure statement above, with reconstruction of inherited content through a supplied decoder (F5).
- **Q-CAUSE:**
  - within-model ablation dependence, including interruption while alive with machinery-only rescue (F2);
  - W-gated coordinator writes, where refusal at W=0 shows the gate matters (F3);
  - endpoint agreement under the audited observer-discard procedure (F4);
  - bounded neural causal contribution at exactly 8/10 (S15).
- **Q-LEARN:** two-stream acquisition and recurrent-core transfer (S12, S13).
- **Q-NECESSITY:** nothing. R-FIX reached no target "within declared search" (U4).

**What it cannot claim**
- Production closure of the full network under the consult #25 rule.
- That the sequence is internally constituted.
- Trajectory-level state sufficiency.
- Exclusion of common-resource explanations in general.
- Adaptation of its own organization (U2, NOT ESTABLISHED).
- Minimality.
- Whole-organism unity (U6).
- Any W-REL separation: formal A-SEP is UNRESOLVED-COMPUTE.
- No C4, C5 or consciousness claim, and no "autopoietic" or "alive" claim.

**Would U3 change this synthesis?**
- **For the closure sentence: no.** Under the current code, every repair and regeneration path runs through the supplied maintenance schedule and regeneration policy.
- **For a separate Q-CAUSE claim (ledger O3, the content→machinery→content loop): yes.**
- **Exception:** U3 would bear on closure only after the coordination had been moved into declared state.
- Per consult #25, U3 is held, not proposed. Proposing it for the Q-CAUSE claim is Aidan's decision.
