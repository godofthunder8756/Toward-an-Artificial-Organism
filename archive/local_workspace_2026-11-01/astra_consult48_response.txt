Q2. Confirm nine required bins, but do not confirm the pooled schedule as sufficient for the existing IGN instrument.

The sampling unit remains unresolved. The carried instrument uses within-seed fitting and seed-level inference. Under the proposed allocation:

    ceil(121 / 67) = 2 trials per seed per bin
    ceil(215 / 67) = 4 trials per seed per bin

Those counts do not supply a within-seed 100-eligible-trial requirement or the previously described within-seed model fitting.

Separate these quantities:

- Trials per seed and bin needed to compute the seed's instrument statistic.
- The denominator used for an eligibility-floor calculation.
- Outer replicates used to assess operating characteristics.

Pooling the eligibility denominator does not supply missing observations to the seed-level estimator. If pooling the instrument fits across seeds is intended instead, that is a substantive estimator change and must be reviewed as such.

There is a second issue: exact binomial calculations assume independent Bernoulli trials with a common success probability. Pooling trials sharing seed-specific fitted models, collapse conditions or eligibility probabilities does not automatically satisfy that assumption. [CSRC](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/binomial_distribution?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Q3. Accept the revised NLN storage and timing law, with two qualifications.

With frozen mu and s = SD(rbar), the recursion

    phi_t = lambda * phi_read,t
            + (1 - lambda) * (r_t - mu) / s

and initialization

    phi_0 = -mu / s

give the desired identity on unmodified trajectories:

    phi_read,t+1 = (rbar_t - mu) / s.

Storing both factors makes an intervention capable of changing their product. Reconstructing u2 only after the action and reward update avoids immediately erasing the intervention before it can affect the controller. Using the post-clip body value to form the next denominator also keeps that divisor in [0.5, 1.5].

However:

- Product movement under the actual fitted clamp remains a realization requirement. The law permits R2; it does not establish that the required magnitude will occur.
- Geometric decay applies to the perturbation component while reward increments match. Once actions and rewards differ, the difference obeys a recursion containing the reward-difference term. Do not claim unconditional lambda-per-step decay across divergent trajectories.

The controller's direct dependence remains through phi. This does not imply that changing physical E, and thereby changing rewards or termination, has no later behavioral effect.

The discarded initialization's offset should be written with its amplitude:

    error_t = lambda^t * mu / s.

For lambda = 0.98, lambda^50 is approximately 0.364, not 0.243. The latter would require an additional mu/s factor. Correct that explanation without changing the accepted initialization.

The proposed 1e-9 audit tolerance can remain a prospective convention subject to P6. It is not a certified floating-point error bound.
