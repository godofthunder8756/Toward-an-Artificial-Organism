**B. Rulings on choices 1-7**

1. **Thresholds: accept the values; correct the rationale.** Exact invariants, 0.01 effect floors, 50% C-T2 error reduction and 0.001 reproduction tolerances are defensible prospective conventions.

   However, 0.01 is not one-sixth of 0.006, and the quantities have different units. Also, a 0.001 tolerance on squared correctness error can be permissive relative to that error's entire range. Call reproduction agreement within declared tolerances, not "near-identity." It need not imply the same criterion flags.

2. **I-SUSPEND: accept.** Together with intact, SWAP and CORRUPT, it provides the appropriate content-by-update comparison. Keep SWAP primary and CORRUPT/SUSPEND descriptive. Specify that suspension suppresses all updates belonging to the declared content, including MF's agreement-memory update, while time and world evolution continue.

3. **Rescue: accept the shared-history design; strengthen the credit rule.** The signed A-minus-B differences alone can pass when B moves farther from actual correctness. Require improvement in absolute assessment error as well. Specify that the core rescue criterion concerns SWAP in both directions; the other rescue results are descriptive.

4. **lambda_a population: accept.** A common threshold derived by a frozen formula from the reference's intact population is acceptable for descriptive frequencies. It is not outcome-dependent tuning. Keep it fixed across agents and interventions within a setting.

   These frequencies describe incorrect outcomes with high estimates and correct outcomes with low estimates. They are not themselves calibration-error measurements.

5. **Equal R/M weighting in C-T2: accept.** This measures assessment across the two checking options without letting each policy's action frequencies determine the evaluation weighting. Retain action-specific errors alongside the combined statistic, and explicitly normalize the weighting across trial decision points.

6. **PM-ACT repair closure: accept the approach, but complete the state specification.** The stated state tuple lacks some information needed between phases: the original read, actual action and check observation. Repair also requires retaining the shadow update through the repair boundary, not merely the pre-perturbation state.

   These caches and the shadow must be accounted for. The current cardinality figures are planning estimates, not established upper bounds.

7. **UNRESOLVED-DESIGN for an unavailable essential comparison: accept.** Apply it to the comparison, with the reason shown. An unavailable criterion is neither a failure nor a pass. A control with another demonstrably failed criterion can still be ruled out as attaining the conjunction.

   Reproduction also needs an unresolved value when required statistics are unavailable; a mandatory YES/NO column is insufficient.
