- Floor F_A. F_A is the pooled class-conditioned measured-gain ratio (L_mu - L_full)/S for SYS-NC1 (O7), per counted size and group. It is declared as the reference that a *later* neural spec's class gain would have to exceed before the spec could word a result as more than the O7-type confound would produce.
- Limits.
- F_A is measured on one kappa law. A trained GRU's mu-only nonlinearity need not match it, so F_A may not transfer.
- Clearing F_A says only that the gain exceeds what that one declared confound produced. It does not exclude two-coordinate mediation (E37-7).
- F_A is not an outcome for SYS-NC1. SYS-NC1 neither passes nor fails.
