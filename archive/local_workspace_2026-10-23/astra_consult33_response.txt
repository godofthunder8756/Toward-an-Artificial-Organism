The rank finding is consequential, but the review overstates it slightly. The unidentified coefficient directions are not recoverable from fit-set observations alone. The 0.632 and 0.408 figures describe feature-space geometry. They are not estimates of the fraction of C2 gain, prediction error or oracle information attributable to that geometry. Their calculation should specify centering, scaling and weighting.
