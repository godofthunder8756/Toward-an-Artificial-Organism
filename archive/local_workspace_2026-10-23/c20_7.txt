**C. Finite-class certificate feasibility.**
- Pros: the only route to a necessity result (inf T > 1 over the declared domain).
- Cons:
  - the R-FIX domain is 651-843 continuous parameters, so exhaustive grids are infeasible (even 2 levels gives 2^651 points), and branch-and-bound or interval bounds at this dimension are unlikely to close;
  - a tractable subclass, such as a quantized subset with most parameters fixed, risks certifying "a narrow fact with little bearing on the present continuous model" (Sec.14 ruling 8);
  - it is worth a paper feasibility analysis only.

- Pros:

- Cons:
  - Q-NECESSITY is unresolved;
  - standing rules exclude new architecture families and protected controllers or registers, so any C4 test design may be blocked at admission;
  - there is a risk of premature framing.
