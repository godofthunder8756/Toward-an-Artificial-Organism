A. Verdict

Accept UNRESOLVED-DESIGN as the reported frozen decision-rule outcome at both A3 settings. Do not accept the stronger interpretation that W-REL has demonstrated a fundamental inability to distinguish assessment from compiled policy.

D1 appears to be an implementation repair. D2 is defensible as restoration of the frozen verification contract, provided no required comparison was removed by the additional "stored primary emits" restriction. That needs a short coverage record.

End the analytic tournament. Complete the documentary close-out, then pursue option (a): a learned-system study of learner-specific correctness. Do not make another PM-ACT separation attempt the next scientific study.

This review interprets the supplied records; it does not independently certify the repository implementation.

B. Q1: Label and interpretation

The label follows the stated rule:

- The reference attains the conjunction.
- Every non-PM named control fails applicable core criteria.
- PM-ACT has no demonstrated core failure but lacks an essential rescue comparison.
- Therefore the procedure cannot issue DISTINGUISHES NAMED CONTROLS.

However, three parts of your interpretation need correction.

First, say "every non-PM implementation in the frozen roster," not "every ordinary rival." PM-ACT and the Bayes reference are themselves ordinary implementations. The roster does not exhaust conventional adaptive control.

Second, criterion attainment and full-pattern reproduction differ. The primary tables report:

| Setting | PM-ACT full-pattern reproduction | Core conjunction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T=3 | NO; `e_A_S` exceeds reproduction tolerance | UNRESOLVED |
| T=4 | UNRESOLVED | UNRESOLVED |

Thus, PM-ACT does not demonstrably reproduce the full pattern at either setting. Passing all evaluable criteria does not mean matching every statistic.

Third, the zero-probability statement needs its conditioning event identified. There are materially different possibilities:

- A recipient-world event has zero probability.
- A repaired PM state has zero occupancy under the intact distribution used to define its decoder.
- An intervention or apparatus-state correspondence leaves the required quantity undefined.
- An implementation incorrectly assigns zero weight to an otherwise reachable event.

The report does not distinguish these.

For a compilation that preserves actions under the same observations and mapped interventions, corresponding observable event probabilities should agree. The reference has a positive-probability rescue procedure. If PM-ACT's *corresponding observable event* instead has probability zero, some part of the procedure or mapping differs.

But PM-ACT does not preserve every assessment readout or probability output. Its frozen state-conditioned decoder may be undefined on a repair state even when the world trajectory has positive probability. That would be a support limitation of this evaluation procedure.

My ruling is therefore:

"This is an unresolved comparison in the specified assessment/intervention procedure. It is not yet a demonstrated fundamental identifiability limit, and the record does not establish whether a criterion revision or an implementation correction would resolve it."

There is also a general limit independent of this result: behavior alone cannot distinguish implementations constructed to be behaviorally equivalent on the tested domain. That does not establish that the compiled implementation lacks the functional role. Compilation may preserve the role in another representation.

We should have checked the decoder and repair-state support under compilation before freezing this comparison. That was a weakness in admission design, not evidence against assessment.

E. Q4: Choose one next study

Choose (a): acquired assessment of the learner's own correctness.

The goal should be to establish an acquired causal role, not to defeat compilation. A perfectly faithful compiled implementation cannot be assumed to be a role-absent control.

Minimal design sketch:

**System.** One small GRU, provisionally 16 hidden units, with ordinary prediction and checking-action readouts. No supplied assessment register, fault labels or oracle-correctness training target.

Train first-order prediction from revealed outcomes and action selection from correctness minus checking cost. The analyst's assessment decoder is fitted afterward on a separate identification split.

**Task.** A short finite episodic binary-evidence task with PREDICT, RECHECK and RESAMPLE. Retain the useful same-evidence versus fresh-evidence distinction.

Introduce prespecified stochastic perturbations in the learner's read-processing computation, alongside evidence-noise conditions. The intervention location, persistence and action-specific effects must be explicit. The purpose is to create cases in which the learner's actual conditional correctness differs from the world-model oracle's.

Do not assume that the old W-REL symmetry survives this change. Matching and first-order controls must be established for the new task and learner.

**Target.** At the action decision, predict:

```text
m_a(H) = P(the frozen learner's own post-action decision is correct
           | observable history H, do(action=a))
```

Evaluate this using the frozen learner and the declared counterfactual observation distribution. The target must include that learner's errors, not substitute the oracle's optimal decisions.

**Controls.**

- Current-output confidence and a cost-general history-dependent controller, with disclosed information and resources.
- The same network at initialization, to distinguish acquired performance from supplied structure.
- Random-donor, same-content and first-order-matched intervention controls.
- A compiled implementation of the frozen learner over the declared finite audit domain.

The compiled control should preserve the tested predictions, actions and mapped interventions. It is an equivalence control, not a presumed negative example of assessment. If it reproduces the role, record an ordinary alternative implementation. If an assessment readout is unavailable in one representation, report that limitation without treating it as role absence.

**Primary criterion.** A preregistered acquired-role conjunction:

1. Held-out state content predicts actual learner-specific correctness beyond the frozen confidence baseline, with both an absolute improvement floor and a relative criterion.
2. Content intervention changes assessment error and checking in the predicted directions while the declared first-order battery is preserved.
3. Repair reduces the assessment error and reverses the checking consequence on a shared received history.
4. The conjunction is not met at initialization and is met after the declared training budget under the frozen seed rule.

Post-check probability effects remain separately reported and constrain the dissociation claim. Passing a pre-check battery is not preservation of the full first-order computation.

**Cheapest falsifier.** Before expensive intervention analysis, check whether actual learner correctness differs meaningfully from oracle correctness and whether the candidate state predicts that difference beyond current confidence.

If that distinction is absent, the study has not exercised its motivating question. Stop under the declared rule. Do not search for convenient faults or checkpoints afterward.

A second cheap falsifier is that every intervention producing a checking effect also damages the first-order probe battery beyond tolerance.

**Compute ballpark.** For planning: ten seeds, one fixed training run per seed, approximately 10,000 updates each, a small batch and short episodes. Expect a few CPU-hours including counterfactual evaluation, rather than the seconds required by the analytic reference. This is an estimate, not an execution budget; bounded throughput and evaluation-cost estimates must appear in the spec.

**C-level.** This would be the first empirical C4-candidate study in this line. The completed W-REL work was admission theory and analytic comparison. Neither the proposed study nor a positive result automatically establishes C4 status or consciousness.

Before review, freeze:

- The exact scientific claim and claim ceiling.
- Network, inputs, readouts and learning rules.
- World law, internal perturbations, costs and event order.
- The assessed prediction stage and learner-specific target.
- Training, identification and evaluation splits.
- Seeds, training budget, checkpoints and stopping rules.
- Baselines, information access and resource accounting.
- Decoder selection and intervention construction.
- First-order controls, all margins and acquisition criterion.
- Rescue conditioning events and support requirements.
- Compiled-control domain and intervention/readout correspondence.
- Outcome labels for failure, undefined support and incomplete computation.
- Verification coverage, smoke tests, retries and hard caps.

The new spec must contain a support audit before execution. It must not inherit PM-ACT's undefined-rescue problem or use failure of a compiled control as a necessary success condition.
