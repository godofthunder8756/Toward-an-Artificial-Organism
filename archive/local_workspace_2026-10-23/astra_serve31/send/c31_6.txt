[c31 part 16/25]
- (c) Decoder features are h only.
- (d) **Permuted-target control (m3):** m_a is permuted across received states **within strata** (stage, cue, read-type pattern) in S_I, using generator 9000+s. If it meets K1, the label is `UNRESOLVED-DESIGN [DECODER LEAKAGE OR DEGENERACY]`.

- (f) Training never reads m, o or o^R.

### 17.4 R-VOI, a first-order value-of-information rival (CNA M4: ADOPTED, with one sub-part REBUTTED)

- **Architecture:** R-VOI's action head reads [p, the arm's own predicted post-read p for each of the 12 possible next reads (6 RECHECK, 6 RESAMPLE), N, read type, stage], all detached. It is trained with the identical recipe and seeds, and the full K1-K3 pipeline is run on it.
- **Rebutted sub-part:** CNA suggested weighting the 12 values "by the law". v2 gives them unweighted. Law weights would hand R-VOI world-law knowledge that CAND does not have (ledger Sec.9.4). That would make the rival stronger than any learner without the law, so it would no longer be an information-matched rival. The action head can learn its own weighting.
- **Outcome rule:** if R-VOI meets J in at least 8 of 10 seeds, the Sec.2.3 label is attached.
