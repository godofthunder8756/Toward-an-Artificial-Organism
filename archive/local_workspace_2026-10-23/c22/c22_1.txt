## 1. World W-REL-B: binary persistent regime (illustrative; nothing frozen) [replaces DRAFT Sec.1]

**Astra Q8 (verbatim world block):** `Z_t in {S, E}, with a specified persistent transition law. S uses (w,e) = (1/20, 1/5). E uses (w,e) = (1/5, 1/20).`

**Per trial t** (the DRAFT Sec.1 steps, with the regime Z_t replacing (I_t, E_t)):
1. X_t is uniform.
2. Evidence bits are b_{t,i} = X_t  XOR  W_{t,i}, with W ~ Bern(w_{Z_t}) and n = 3.
3. **Designated processing channel** F reads y_{t,i} = b_{t,i}  XOR  N_{t,i}, with N ~ Bern(e_{Z_t}).
4. Action:
   - PREDICT;
   - RECHECK (cost c_r): the same b_t, re-read with fresh internal noise, giving y'_t;
   - RESAMPLE (cost c_s): fresh world bits, read once, giving y''_t.
5. The learner outputs its first-order prediction p_t.
6. Feedback: the outcome and the realized cost. Reward = 1[correct] - cost.

**Regime.**
- S, "system-dominant": internal noise dominates.
- E, "evidence-dominant": world noise dominates.
- In both, eps = w + e - 2we = 23/100.
- **Transition law (illustrative; to be fixed before any execution):**
  - H0: static within an episode, h = 0.
  - H1: symmetric persistent switching with hazard h = 1/20.
  - In both, P(Z_0 = S) = 1/2.

**Costs** are visible inputs to the learner and to **every rival** (Sec.4). There are no categorical cost bins.

**What the learner does not receive:**
- Z_t;
- fault labels;
- oracle confidence;
- the Bayes posterior;
