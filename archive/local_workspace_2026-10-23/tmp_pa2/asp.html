WHAT IS ASPREDICTED?
A platform that makes pre-registrations easy to make and evaluate. All pre-registrations can be downloaded as single page PDFs that are time-stamped and include a unique URL for verification.


Example: https://aspredicted.org/kv692.pdf

HOW DOES IT WORK?
  • One author creates the pre-registration.
  • Coauthors get an email asking for approval.
  • Pre-registration remains private until an author makes it public (why?)
  • Public pre-registrations cannot be modified and are automatically backed up in the Web Archive.
IF NOT 'AS PREDICTED'?
  • 'Contrary to expectations, we found that...'
  • 'Unexpectedly, we also found that...'
  • 'In addition to the analyses we pre-registered we also ran...'
