This dataset release contains the confirmatory evidence and decision record for Experimental Block V.

The original preregistration was accepted publicly before any confirmatory data were generated. A pre-execution protocol clarification was then accepted publicly by OSF before any confirmatory seed was run. The clarification resolved two execution-level ambiguities without changing hypotheses, seeds, held-out variants, architecture classes, misinformation/correction procedures, impairment ranges or other frozen scientific criteria.

Final decisions:

- H5.1 Active self-monitoring — RETAIN
- H5.2 Recovery after internal impairment — RETAIN
- H5.3 Misinformation and correction/rescue — RETAIN
- H5.4 Episode-specific monitoring — RESTRICT
- H5.5 Self-specific versus environmental uncertainty — RETAIN
- H5.6 Bypass resistance / endogenous necessity — REDUCE

The dataset preserves the twenty-seed confirmatory outputs, analysis record, decision table, unlock and clarification provenance, and independent reproduction record.

The study supports a causal role for self-related estimates in the registered artificial tasks, while the H_EXTERNAL bypass reduces the stronger claim that endogenous reflective self-modelling is necessary for the downstream adaptive-control function.

No result establishes phenomenal consciousness, sentience or subjective experience.
