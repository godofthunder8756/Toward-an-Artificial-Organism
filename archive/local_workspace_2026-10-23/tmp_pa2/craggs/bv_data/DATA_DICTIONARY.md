# Data Dictionary

## block5_confirmatory_per_seed.json
Per-seed confirmatory outputs for all six preregistered hypotheses.

## block5_confirmatory_analysis_record.json
Machine-readable hypothesis-level analysis and decision record.

## block5_decisions.csv
Compact H5.1-H5.6 decision table.

## BLOCK_V_ANALYSIS_REPRODUCTION_CHECK.json
Independent regeneration check showing byte-for-byte identity of the substantive analysis artefacts.

## frozen_protocol/
Snapshot of the preregistered manifests, locks, seeds, held-out variants and audits used to interpret the dataset.

## provenance records
The unlock, public clarification and execution records document the evidential sequence from preregistration to confirmatory execution.
