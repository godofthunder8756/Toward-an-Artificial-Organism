# Evidence-Driven ToE Dependency Map After Block V

## Governing distinction

The completed experiments support several mechanisms as causally useful within registered artificial systems. They do **not** establish that those mechanisms form one necessary developmental or ontological chain.

| reflective self-modelling -> phenomenal consciousness | OPEN / SUSPEND | No validated bridge and no phenomenality test. |

## Revised topology

The evidence supports:

`framework conditions + enabling conditions`

feeding into a set of **scoped causal mechanisms** and **open transition problems**, with multiple bypass routes.

It does not support:

`physics -> maintenance -> reproduction -> learning -> agency -> self-model -> consciousness`

as one experimentally validated compulsory sequence.
