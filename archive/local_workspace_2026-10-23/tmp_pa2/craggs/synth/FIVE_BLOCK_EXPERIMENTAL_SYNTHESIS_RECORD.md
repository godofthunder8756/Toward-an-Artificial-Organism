## 1. Purpose

This record consolidates the first completed preregistered experimental cycle of the Theory of Everything Research Programme. Its purpose is not to count positive hypothesis decisions as validation of a single hierarchy. Instead, it separates:

1. what each preregistered experiment established within its registered artificial system;
2. which stronger necessity claims were defeated by successful bypasses;
3. which cross-block dependencies were never directly tested;
4. which transition problems remain open after the first experimental cycle.

A `RETAIN` decision therefore means that a frozen criterion was satisfied within its registered scope. It does not imply universal necessity, natural historical identity or fundamentality.

The study retained parent-offspring hereditary dependence, a causal inherited-trait effect on reproductive success, selection-driven cumulative population change, fidelity-variation dependence and historically structured re-adaptation after environmental switching.

`Block I self-maintenance -> reproduction`

because that transition was not experimentally constructed or tested.

## 5. Block III — Retention, Feedback and Learning

- OSF: 10.17605/OSF.IO/8ZWM7
- Software: 10.5281/zenodo.23244848
- Dataset: 10.5281/zenodo.23244857

Decisions:
- H3.1-H3.7 RETAIN.

The study separated policy-accessible retained state, within-episode closed-loop feedback and between-episode learning. Targeted interventions and factorial contrasts showed distinct causal roles.

Serious bypasses remained successful:
- direct present-state augmentation bypassed retained state;
- open-loop control was sufficient under predictable disturbance;
- explicit contextual policy selection bypassed parameter learning when mapping identity was supplied.

### Block III synthesis
Retain causal usefulness and separability. Reject any interpretation that retention, feedback and learning form a universally mandatory adaptive hierarchy.

## 6. Block IV — Adaptive Agency and Structural Generalisation

- OSF: 10.17605/OSF.IO/REHW5
- Software: 10.5281/zenodo.23263492
- Dataset: 10.5281/zenodo.23263512

Decisions:
- H4.1-H4.7 RETAIN.

A3 showed rapid causal-reversal adaptation, horizon-sensitive counterfactual rollout, strong held-out composition and problem-family performance, selective failure under targeted world-model corruption, and complete correction rescue. A4PLUS and preregistered heuristics provided serious alternatives but did not reproduce the decisive cross-family profile.

### Block IV synthesis
Retain a scoped causal role for world-model content and counterfactual rollout in the registered structural-generalisation tasks.

Do not infer universal necessity of explicit world models or unrestricted general intelligence.

### Block V synthesis

`accurate self-relevant information -> causal adaptive control`

within the registered tasks.

Reduce:

`endogenous reflective self-modelling is necessary for that control function`.

## 8. Cross-block experimental lesson

The first five-block cycle supports a network of conditional mechanisms, not a compulsory ladder.

Three classes of result recur:

1. **Selective causal usefulness:** maintenance, retention, feedback, learning, model use, rollout and self-related estimates can each make measurable causal contributions when the corresponding function is required.
2. **Successful bypass:** several target capacities can be reproduced by alternative architectures when equivalent task-relevant information or control opportunities are supplied.
3. **Untested bridge:** experiments on capacities located on either side of a proposed transition do not by themselves establish the transition between them.

The programme therefore gains explanatory discipline by distinguishing *mechanism efficacy* from *dependency necessity*.

## 9. Main theoretical consequences

- Persistent dissipation and self-maintenance remain distinct.
- Self-maintenance and reproduction remain separated by an unresolved bridge.
- Selection operates once a heritable reproducing population exists; it does not explain the origin of that population.
- Retention, feedback and learning are separable mechanisms rather than one adaptive principle.
- Structural generalisation can depend causally on model content and rollout in the tested architecture, but world models are not promoted to a universal law of intelligence.
- Causal self-related control is experimentally supportable without establishing endogenous self-modelling as universally necessary.
- Phenomenal consciousness remains independent Open Problem 1.

## 10. Overall conclusion

The five-block programme does not validate the original hierarchy as a single sequence. It replaces it with a more defensible architecture containing:

- framework conditions;
- enabling conditions;
- scoped effective mechanisms;
- multiple still-open transition problems;
- successful bypass routes;
- one independent open problem concerning phenomenal consciousness.

That is a stronger result than simply confirming the original architecture because the programme has identified where necessity claims fail, where causal roles survive, and where the evidence still does not reach.
