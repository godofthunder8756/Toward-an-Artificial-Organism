Abstract
This paper validates an engineered reference assay, not an autonomous self-model. The transparent
deterministic construction stores event content E and an engineered actor-local relational tag R separately,
maps them to an explicit integer history carrier and present mediator P, and applies a deliberately constructed
prospective evaluator before deterministic option selection. A prior M1 campaign served only as assay
calibration. The SITC campaign preregistered S1-S6, P and Q clamps, exact integer thresholds, and fail-closed
decision rules. Six process-isolated deterministic re-executions in alternating forward and reversed order
produced identical normalized projections. They comprised 88 registered cases per process, 528 case records
total, 292 trace endpoints per process, and 1,752 endpoints total. Each endpoint was read 256 times without
mutation, producing 448,512 probe rows that are persistence checks rather than independent observations. Full
S5 restoration was byte-identical to the checkpoint and reproduced the direct continuation endpoint and its
repeated read-only projection in 96 of 96 expanded cases. The demonstrated dependencies are consequences of
interventions on the registered engineered causal graph and validate the assay's conformance and evidence
discipline. In the registered finite construction, a relationally self-indexed temporal state causally influenced
future-directed choice through the separately intervened prospective-evaluation path. The construct remains
extensionally reproducible by ordinary conditional computation. NOVELTY = UNADJUDICATED.

3.1 Algebraic reduction
 - Let r=1 when attributed-owner and controlled-entity tokens are equal (SELF), and r=0 for OTHER or UNBOUND.
   Then p=r e.

 - For option i, F_i=p_eff+d_i, Q_i=-(F_i-g)^2, and A is the unique argmax of Q. A tie invalidates the case.

 - Target options are d_C=g-e and d_B=g. Therefore Q_C=-(p_eff-e)^2, Q_B=-p_eff^2, and Q_C-Q_B=2 e p_eff-e^2.

 - SELF gives p_eff=e, so COMPENSATE wins by e^2. OTHER, UNBOUND, and the S4 coupling ablation use p_eff=0,
   so BASELINE wins by e^2.

 - S2 uses p=+/-m and d=+/-m at g=0, making the cancelling option win by 4m^2. P clamp fixes p=4 and yields
   margin 64. Q clamp directly fixes LEFT=16, RIGHT=0.

effects are guaranteed when the equations and interventions are implemented as specified. This is useful for
assay validation and fault detection, but it is not empirical discovery of an independently arising mechanism.

6. Run design and independent units
 - Six process-isolated deterministic re-executions used the registered F/R/F/R/F/R order and six opaque-token
   bijections. This establishes process separation plus order- and token-invariance, not six independent scientific
   replications.

 - Each process contained 88 ordered case records: S1 16, S2 4, S3 16, S4 16, S5 16, S6 16, P clamp 2, Q clamp 2.
   Across six processes these become 528 case records.

 - Collapsing NORMAL/REVERSED pairs leaves 44 underlying registered parameter settings per process. Trace
   arms further reduce to 30 distinct evaluator-input tuples; Q clamp bypasses the evaluator and adds one distinct
   selector vector.

 - Each process produced 292 read-only trace endpoints: 32 S1, 16 S2, 48 S3, 64 S4, 64 S5, 64 S6, 4 P-clamp, and
   zero Q-clamp traces. Across six processes there were 1,752 endpoints.

 - Each trace endpoint was queried 256 times without state mutation. Thus 292 x 256 x 6 = 448,512 probe rows.
   They are repeated persistence/non-mutation checks, not statistically independent observations or evolving
   time steps.

 - Q clamp instead used four direct selector invocations per process, 24 total, with no probe series.

7.2 Restoration
S5 captured a checkpoint after acquisition and 256 neutral ticks. Divergence replaced SELF-history content with
-E, recomputed P, and added 17 neutral ticks. Full restoration recovered the complete declared checkpoint
bytes. DIRECT and FULL_RESTORE then advanced to the same deterministic endpoint and yielded identical
read-only probe projections in all 96 expanded cases. The implementation performs 256 neutral ticks
arithmetically and then reads the resulting endpoint 256 times; it does not store 256 independently evolving
post-restore states. The finding is deterministic declared-state restoration, not restoration of a self or identity.

9. Prior art and novelty posture
Component-level prior art is extensive. Cognitive and robotic work already separates agent/owner slots from
event content, implements autobiographical or episodic records, distinguishes self from other, and connects
memory with prospective action evaluation. Causal mediation, causal tracing, activation patching, and
interchange interventions already provide mediator clamps, corrupt-and-restore logic, and representation
swaps. In particular, Vig et al. (2020) formalized causal mediation interventions in language models; Geiger et al.
(2021) used interchange interventions to test causal abstraction; Meng et al. (2022) used causal tracing with
corrupt-and-restore logic; and activation-patching methods papers document both the technique and its
interpretive limits.

The focused review did not locate the exact packaged S1-S6 plus dual-clamp protocol in one source, but this
absence does not establish novelty. The architecture is an ordinary finite conditional computation, and every
individual intervention family has close methodological precedent. Any possible contribution is limited to the
protocol combination and evidence discipline. NOVELTY = UNADJUDICATED.


10. Limitations and interpretation
 - SELF, self-history, future-directed, and prospective are operational engineering labels. The system neither
   identifies itself nor represents lived autobiography.

 - The mechanism is transparent, deterministic, hand-built, and extensionally implementable as a small finite-state
   or lookup system.

 - The observed effects mostly follow from the registered equations and direct interventions; they validate
   implementation conformance and named controls.

 - The fixed quadratic objective was not varied and supplies no independent evidence. Several crossed factors are
   invariance controls by construction.

 - The finite panel does not establish generalization to learned, opaque, stochastic, embodied, open-ended, or
   independently evolved systems.

 - The 256 repeated probes test non-mutation/persistence of one endpoint. They do not increase the number of
   independent scientific units.

 - Six fresh processes test deterministic re-execution, order, and token invariance. They are not six independent
   scientific replications.

 - Public verification re-scores one normalized projection and receipt assertions; the historical raw campaign is not
   reconstructed by the compact package.

 - S6 establishes only that an omitted harness field cannot affect the core. It does not test truth-sensitive
   self-knowledge.

 - No finite panel rules out every extensionally equivalent lookup implementation.

11. Conclusion
The RC2 interpretation is deliberately modest. A programmer specified a finite causal graph, froze a set of
interventions and decision rules, executed the transparent reference construction, and used a separate
fail-closed scorer to verify exact conformance. Swap, ablation, clamp, origin-isolation, and restoration results are
reported by their actual evidentiary roles rather than as equally independent discoveries. In the registered finite
construction, a relationally self-indexed temporal state causally influenced future-directed choice through the
separately intervened prospective-evaluation path. This result does not establish phenomenal consciousness,
sentience, subjective experience, personhood, life, AGI, human-equivalent selfhood, metaphysical identity,
accurate factual self-knowledge, autobiographical subjectivity, spontaneous emergence of a self-model, identity
persistence, or architectural novelty. The work is an engineered reference-assay validation. NOVELTY =
UNADJUDICATED.
