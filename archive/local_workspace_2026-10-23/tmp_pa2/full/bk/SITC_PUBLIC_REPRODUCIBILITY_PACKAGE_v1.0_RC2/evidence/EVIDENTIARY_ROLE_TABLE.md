# Evidentiary roles of the registered SITC components

## Scope

This table describes what each registered component established in the engineered, transparent, deterministic reference construction. The component outcomes are parts of one preregistered assay. They are not eight statistically independent discoveries.

`SELF` is an engineered actor-local relational tag produced by equality of two opaque tokens. The history carrier, present-state mediator, prospective evaluator, and selector are explicit variables in the construction. Several registered outcomes therefore follow directly from the equations when the implementation conforms to the specification. Their evidentiary value lies in validating the intervention protocol, interface isolation, and complete causal chain over the registered finite panel.

| Component | Registered manipulation or comparison | Frozen result across six processes | Evidentiary role | Construction-aware interpretation |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| **S1 - Self vs Other Attribution** | Hold event content, goal, options, initial conditions, and schedule fixed; change only the attributed-owner token so that the resolved relation is `SELF` or `OTHER`. | 96/96 expanded cases passed. `SELF` produced `p = content` and `COMPENSATE`; `OTHER` produced `p = 0` and `BASELINE`. | **Primary target causal contrast; content-only rival control.** | The result verifies that the implemented path is gated by the engineered relational tag rather than factual content alone. Given `p = content` for `SELF`, `p = 0` otherwise, and the registered target options, the choice difference is algebraically entailed by a conforming implementation. It is not evidence that self-attribution arose spontaneously. |
| **S2 - Self-History Swap** | Exchange the complete actor-local self-history representations between matched recipients before recomputing present state. | 24/24 expanded cases passed. Each recipient's `p` and semantic choice followed the donor history in both swap directions. | **Substitution/interchange check.** | The swap explicitly substitutes the donor history and recomputes `p` from it. Donor-following therefore validates the registered interchange operation and downstream data flow; it is not an independent discovery of identity transfer or selfhood. Recipient event memory remains a control. |
| **S3 - Attribution Ablation** | Change the relation from `SELF` to `UNBOUND` while preserving factual event memory; then clamp `p` back to the original content. | 96/96 expanded cases passed. | **S4 - Prospective-Coupling Ablation** | Preserve stored event memory, relation, history, and `p`; replace only the evaluator's effective `p` with the registered reference value `0`. | 96/96 expanded cases passed. | **S5 - Complete-State Restoration** | From a checkpoint, compare a direct continuation with a divergent history substitution followed by full, sham, or event-memory-only restoration. | 96/96 expanded cases passed. ## Joint interpretation

S1 and S4 are the central registered contrasts for the relational-attribution and prospective-evaluation edges. S2 and S3 test substitution and ablation of declared mediators. S5 tests complete declared-state restoration and deterministic continuation. S6, the P clamp, the Q clamp, presentation reversal, token permutations, and process replication are controls for leakage, interface isolation, order, and execution integrity.

The maximum supported claim remains exactly:

> In the registered finite construction, a relationally self-indexed temporal state causally influenced future-directed choice through the separately intervened prospective-evaluation path.

This is a claim about the registered causal organization of an engineered reference assay. It does not establish phenomenal consciousness, sentience, subjective experience, personhood, life, AGI, human-equivalent selfhood, metaphysical identity, accurate factual self-knowledge, spontaneous emergence of a self-model, or architectural novelty. `NOVELTY = UNADJUDICATED`.
