# Registration entry

Title: When Internal Signals Mislead: Sensing and Preference in Artificial Homeostasis

Author: Jonathan D Faeth

Affiliation: Independent Researcher

Study type: Computational simulation; prospective main study following disclosed exploratory development.

Keywords: artificial homeostasis; interoception; active inference; embodied agents; simulation; robustness

## Description

This simulation study tests whether coupled selective sensing and assumed likelihood precision differ from preference reweighting in robustness to misleading internal signals. In the AffectWorld platform, three agent configurations will be evaluated under clean, positively biased, and symmetric-perturbation conditions across 12 fixed layouts and 64 new seeds (6,912 episodes). The primary outcome is a 40-step resource-regulation loss including a terminal-failure penalty. The primary estimand is the seed-paired difference between coupled and preference agents in bias-minus-clean loss, with a two-sided 95% Student-t interval. Earlier exploratory simulations informed the design and are excluded from confirmation. This is an empirical extension of prior artificial-homeostasis research; subjective experience and consciousness are not measured.

## Data collection status

Exploratory and software-validation data already exist. No confirmatory observations using seeds 10000–10063 have been collected. See protocol.md for full disclosure.
