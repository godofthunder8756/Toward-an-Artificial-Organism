[344-40] The study is a controlled simulation experiment organised into six linked confirmatory manifests covering H5.1-H5.6.  The core architecture comparison uses S0-S4 plus the H_ERROR and H_EXTERNAL bypasses. Two decisive task families are used: a decision family where low estimated competence can justify costed checking, and a verification family where low estimated competence can justify purchasing additional computation. Matched self-irrelevant controls remove the usefulness of self-related information.  H5.2 and H5.4 use unannounced hidden internal impairment. H5.3 directly manipulates the nominated self-estimate while ordinary task information is held fixed, then restores the accurate estimate. H5.5 holds external difficulty fixed while internal reliability changes. H5.6 compares S2 against serious non-self and externally supplied-information bypasses.  Conditions are paired within seed wherever appropriate. The independent simulation seed is the inferential replicate.
