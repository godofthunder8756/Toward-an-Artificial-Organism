B. Answers to Q1-Q7

1. Track A triage and the substrate rule

The triage is useful for allocating effort, but some classifications overstate what the comparisons exclude.

- F1 is foundational only to a conditional closure claim. A strongly connected dependency graph does not by itself demonstrate production closure: the edges must distinguish production, enabling conditions, resource dependence and supplied sequencing.
- F2 supports within-model ablation dependence. The digest does not justify treating common-resource explanations as generally excluded.
- F3 does not exclude an externally specified coordinator with a consequential resource gate. Refusal at W=0 establishes that the gate matters. It does not establish that the sequence of operations is internally constituted.
- F4 should say "endpoint agreement under the audited observer-discard procedure." That is narrower than trajectory-level state sufficiency or absence of every host-side influence.
- F5 supports reconstruction of inherited content through a supplied decoder. That remains useful even though template copying explains it.
- S8 and S10 are integrity achievements, not additional functional capabilities.
- S12 is a documentation gap to resolve by reading the existing comparison, not a reason for another experiment.

I would rename REDUNDANT to "REDUCED OR NO LONGER PRIORITY," with separate fields for retained evidence and future effort. A mechanism can instantiate a real role while being conventional.

U10 particularly needs correction: PATTERN REPRODUCED BY NAMED CONTROL does not automatically create a REDUNDANT entry. That directly contradicts the accepted W-REL interpretation rules.

U1 belongs first because it changes the flagship sentence without another run. Freeze a finite rule:

> "A supplied substrate operation may implement generic state transformation, transport, execution or numerical integration. Organization-specific instructions, maintenance sequences, switching conditions and repair policies must be represented in declared system state rather than hidden in the supplied operation. The substrate must execute alternative admissible instruction contents, including unsuccessful ones, without substituting the intended organization."

Under that rule, a hard-coded copy->verify->switch->remove routine is supplied organizational coordination unless that sequence is represented in maintained state. A generic interpreter can qualify as substrate; calling a function an interpreter is insufficient.

This does not settle an observer-independent definition of autopoiesis. It makes the model's conditional claim inspectable.

3. Track B novelty and first prior-art checks

The novelty risk is substantial. I would check these in this order:

- **Elliott and Shadbolt, 1998; van Ooyen and Willshaw, 1999.** Retrograde trophic competition, activity-dependent uptake and structural consequences of limited support are already explicit mechanisms. This is the closest match to UDTN's resource-routing machinery. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6793069/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Egbert and Barandiaran, 2014.** Their sensorimotor model develops self-maintaining behavioral patterns shaped by prior activity. It directly challenges the novelty of persistent, history-dependent behavior that sustains its own enabling organization. [frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00590/full?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Di Paolo, 2000.** Homeostatic plasticity and recovery after sensorimotor disruption are an essential comparison for the adaptation claim. [ezequieldipaolo.net](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/homeostatic_adaptation_2000.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Energy-constrained plasticity work.** Add *Energy efficient synaptic plasticity* and *Competitive plasticity to reduce the energetic costs of learning*. Charging plasticity and introducing resource competition are not, individually, new contributions. [eLife](https://elifesciences.org/articles/50804?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Keramati and Gutkin, 2014.** Their model explicitly connects internal-state regulation, reward construction and behavior. It is a strong mechanistic comparator, not merely a reward-based straw man. [eLife](https://elifesciences.org/articles/04811?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Also distinguish fixed-total-weight normalization from biological synaptic scaling. Turrigiano's scaling work concerns activity-dependent adjustment of synaptic strengths; those terms should not be used interchangeably. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/36103?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

My inference: UDTN presently looks like a potentially useful combination of established mechanisms. No demonstrated novelty follows yet.

The decisive weakness is that "local co-activity x weight" is not a measure of functional necessity. Redundant, saturated or mutually cancelling pathways can be highly active without being important to the declared function. Calling that signal "functional use" does not establish the distinction on which the novelty claim depends.

Finally, replace "show there is no structure-preserving mapping to Track A." An unrestricted simulator can encode many architectures. The relevant question is whether a declared mapping preserves the causal dependencies, interventions, information access and resource accounting relevant to the claim.

4. Adequacy of the falsification program

The proposed order is sensible, but the tests are not yet adequate.

First, define the mechanism completely. Missing pieces include the action rule, plasticity rule, support-allocation rule and resource balance. "Conserved and replenished" needs a precise interpretation:

```text
change in accounted resource =
    inflow - computation cost - maintenance cost
           - plasticity cost - outflow
```

Internal transfers cancel. A fixed inflow with activity-dependent redistribution may support use-dependent retention without demonstrating autonomous production of maintenance capacity.

For the 2x2:

- Keep total available resource comparable. Separate budgets must not simply grant extra resources.
- Specify what distinguishes functional-use routing from activity routing independently of the desired outcome.
- Match initial competence, exposure and activity where the comparison requires it.
- Report main effects and the interaction. A positive interaction is neither sufficient evidence of constitutive organization nor necessary for two mechanisms to contribute.

The most important missing control is a **dependency change that preserves immediate computational function**. Swapping learned circuits can directly swap a policy or disrupt compatibility. That does not show that behavior tracks maintenance needs.

Prefer two initially function-matched organizations with different maintenance dependencies. Change which input or action sustains the vulnerable organization while preserving immediate task outputs. Test whether the subsequent behavioral change tracks that altered dependency.

Also:

- Add a yoked sensory-history control: repeated input alone may preserve the circuit.
- Check whether raw stimulation can sustain behaviorally useless structure.
- Measure retention with plasticity disabled where appropriate, separately from reconstruction or relearning.
- Give decoupling a prespecified observation window. A learned bias need not disappear immediately when its maintenance coupling is removed.
- Do not import FO+ISO reversion mechanically. The new study needs its own scalar outcome, differentiability assumptions and interpretation.

Run R-HEBB-NORM early, but also include an actual trophic-competition baseline. HRL needs matched observables, intervention access and an explicit resource ledger; "same internal state" alone does not ensure comparability.

5. Substrate, self-maintenance and endogenous priorities

The proposed locality rule is insufficient. A local rule can encode a preferred organization; a non-local numerical implementation can faithfully implement generic dynamics.

Separate three things:

- Supplied laws and learning rules.
- Acquired organization.
- External interventions or control during measurement.

Turning off backprop removes that external optimizer from the measurement phase. It does not make the trained content self-originating, and it does not establish ongoing endogenous learning.

Likewise, absence of a scalar reward establishes an implementation fact. It does not establish absence of designer-specified preferences. Decay laws, support allocation, initial training and the sensorimotor environment can all determine which states persist.

An admissible claim would be:

> "Under fixed supplied dynamics, acquired differences in organization caused different maintenance dependencies, and interventions on those dependencies redirected behavior."

That is meaningful without claiming that values arose independently of supplied constraints.

6. A stronger candidate?

I would not replace UDTN with another speculative architecture now. The stronger next candidate is an experiment:

**Function-matched dependency reassignment.**

Construct or train two organizations with matched immediate performance but different maintenance dependencies. Hold the task and reward conditions fixed. Alter the dependency while controlling direct changes to the policy. Ask whether behavior follows the newly relevant maintenance requirement.

UDTN may be a vehicle for that test. If "functional use" remains co-activity under another name, then study it as a trophic or habit model rather than claiming a new architectural principle.

The result need not defeat every HRL implementation to matter. Reproduction can identify an alternative implementation or show that the behavioral evidence is non-diagnostic. It does not establish that the candidate's coupling is absent.

A defensible novelty claim requires a specific new mechanism, prediction, limitation or demonstrated advantage. "Cannot be explained by conventional computation" is not an attainable research target.

D. Strongest objection to the whole program

The program may be repeatedly building systems whose persistence conditions are supplied by the designer, then interpreting the behavior induced by those conditions as increasingly endogenous organization.

Track A can place the crucial coordination inside its substrate convention. Track B can place the preferred outcomes inside its decay, transport and plasticity laws. Both can then produce successful interventions largely because the relevant dependencies were engineered into the model.

The risk is not that the mechanisms are conventional. The risk is that the experiments never distinguish the intended claim from the designer's operational definition.

Even successful dependency-specific regulation would establish an autonomy or adaptive-control result, not a connection to consciousness by itself. The strongest path forward is therefore to earn one narrow causal result that survives the closest mechanistic comparison, and state exactly what it contributes without requiring it to validate the program's ultimate ambition.
