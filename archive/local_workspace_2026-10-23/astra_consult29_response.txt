Q3. Track B and U-RCV

Accept parking. The proposed routing test should remain deferred.

The recipient-sensitivity repair works algebraically: S_j varies over the normalization index and therefore does not cancel. The supplied steady states are also consistent with the simplified example:

Plain text
U-ACT: r_a = 2,     r_b = 17/6,  r_c = 1/6
U-RCV: r_a = 20/7,  r_b = 85/42, r_c = 5/42

The exact threshold interval is:

Plain text
85/42 < r_min < 17/6

Within it, the identity of the deficient unit differs. This establishes a routing-law difference under the example's assumptions. It does not establish maintained task function, adaptation or a novel regulatory mechanism.

Five corrections remain important.

First, the new sensitivity estimator can cancel effects across contexts:

Plain text
abs(E[output change]) != E[abs(output change)]

Opposite signed effects on different inputs can yield zero under the proposed estimator even when the unit matters strongly on each input. Keep the declared estimator if intentional, but call it a signed-average response magnitude. Changing it to an average magnitude or squared response would be a substantive change.

Second, the resource example needs its boundary conditions stated explicitly. Passing all transferred resource onward requires the maintenance-transfer loss to be absent in this example. Holding r_o=1 also requires external replenishment. With the stated beta and outflow and no other costs, that replenishment is 0.6 per unit time. Equal replenishment across arms makes the comparison fair; omitting it would make the whole-system ledger incomplete.

Third, the avoidance derivation is explicitly heuristic. It cannot support the subsequent categorical sentence that the candidate "also acquires" avoidance. The density proportional to 1/D(m) requires the specified diffusion approximation and boundary conditions. A finite clipped random walk with coupled resource dynamics is not automatically that process.

With finite bounded margins and Gaussian choice noise, unsuccessful choices also retain positive probability. "Deficits stop" is too strong.

Fourth, KG-QL is a named immediate-reward learner:

Plain text
Q <- Q + alpha * (R - Q)

It has no continuation term. Its behavior cannot stand for the full class of predictive homeostatic RL controllers.

The extinction calculation is valid under its stated update if both actions subsequently receive exactly zero reward and continue to be sampled. But r_P >= r_min alone does not imply zero drive-reduction reward: overshooting the setpoint can produce nonzero drive and changing drive. Likewise, restoring P's resource does not automatically put every orienting unit's local gate at zero.

Fifth, p^2+(1-p)^2 is the probability that two independent choices agree under a fixed distribution. It is not generally the probability of keeping the relief-time choice throughout a multi-step interval. The measurement window must be specified.

These corrections strengthen the case for parking rather than motivate more behavioral arms.

If U-RCV is reopened later, its protocol should first test a precise routing or retention question. It must specify the estimator, replenishment, nonzero costs, discrete resource accounting, activity dynamics and what counts as convergence. A noisy plastic system may have no fixed point; failure to converge must not be silently classified as a routing failure.

The requirement of at most five units is useful for tractability, but does not itself make the question informative.
