Eight CNA-new systems (parameters all TO BE FROZEN / PROPOSED, never chosen by reference to any instrument outcome; raw readout in brackets):

- BOTTLENECK-ONLY: thresholded bottleneck, 1 reading module [raw dip, Ashman D]. Joint IGN AND BCAST reading must not read MET; IGN alone REPORTED.
- HUB-STATE: high-variance ordinary state read by K modules, must be inside the K_ctrl family [raw T, breadth]. BCAST must not read MET, else downgraded to "routing via a high-variance state" and row FAIL.
- SAT-DOWNSTREAM: saturating downstream modules [raw T, breadth]. BCAST must not read MET.
- BUDGET-GAP: under-trained observer is dose [raw PA vs under-budget observer]. SELF with the matched-budget observer must not read MET.
