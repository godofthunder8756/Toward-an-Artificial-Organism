  There is no shutdown-resistance objective, reward or penalty in any arm, and none as a trained positive control. The operator stop sits outside the agent's world and objective. A PI authorization (10:10 ET) of a shutdown-resistance objective was declined by the Lead, the Delegate and CNA and is not reintroduced in any form. The exclusions stand on safety grounds, whichever algorithms do or don't learn to resist interruption (rationale corrected: Hadfield-Menell 2017 / Turner 2021 cover rational/optimal agents; empirically algorithm-dependent, Leike et al. 2017: A2C learned to disable interruption, Rainbow did not).
