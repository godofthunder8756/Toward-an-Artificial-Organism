== (B) NOVELTY SHRINKAGE AND WHAT SURVIVES (cover note S1) ==
Every round of prior-art verification has shrunk what we can claim as novel:
- v1 (f2fd0ae6) claimed three novel items: Q-NECESSITY of a learned interoceptive self-model vs matched ablations; SELF for agents; pre-registration plus the Stage-0 known-answer gate.
- Pre-freeze note 29068cce downgraded pre-registration to PARTIAL OVERLAP (Craggs 2026 Blocks I-V; Faeth 2026).
- Open-items note 16646915 narrowed Q-NECESSITY, after Craggs Block V turned out to have been run already, with H5.6 REDUCE.
- Full reads badf7258 made Q-NECESSITY an EXTENSION of Asan 2026 and narrowed the Stage-0 gate to its confound-realization element.
The cover note states: "The study is now an EXTENSION or PARTIAL OVERLAP on every claim." (SELF itself remains NOVEL AS FAR AS FOUND; see item 1.) What survives, as far as found:
1. SELF for agents (adapted from Binder et al. 2024): NOVEL AS FAR AS FOUND.
2. The confound-realization gate. Stage-0 rows use confounds built to fake each instrument's signature; each is checked for realization; a confound that is not realized leaves specificity UNRESOLVED and blocks Stage 0. Known-answer validation and fail-closed rules themselves follow prior practice (Akiyama 2026, Bankuti 2026, Kulma 2026). Label: PARTIAL OVERLAP; Akiyama S2.5 nearest precedent.
3. The D3/D4 design for Q-NECESSITY in a recurrent agent with a learned interoceptive self-model, including the required A-EXT arm that bypasses the self-model with an external estimate. Label: EXTENSION of Wilterson & Graziano 2021 and Asan 2026 (A6-2).
Novelty table (A5-4): Q-NECESSITY NOVEL -> EXTENSION (Asan 2026, Wilterson & Graziano 2021, Craggs 2026 Block V, Phua 2025). SELF NOVEL -> NOVEL AS FAR AS FOUND. Stage-0 gate NOVEL -> PARTIAL OVERLAP (only the confound-realization gate survives). Pre-registration -> PARTIAL OVERLAP (Craggs, Faeth).
Other labels unchanged from base v1 S10 / A1-1: homeostatic depleting-drift setup = SETUP, CITED NOT CLAIMED (Keramati & Gutkin 2014; Yoshida et al. 2024; EVAAA 2025); fate-sharing = PARTIAL OVERLAP (Man, Damasio & Neven 2022); workspace necessity and IGN/BCAST markers = REPLICATION/EXTENSION of Phua 2025; Q-EXIST / Q-CAUSE = EXTENSION of Asan 2026 (A5-2; earlier PARTIAL OVERLAP with Hayes 2026); S-INT = NOT A NOVELTY CLAIM (Orseau & Armstrong 2016; Leike et al. 2017).
