**The construct, stated precisely.** The candidate assessment target is the **locus** of current unreliability: whether errors arise in the learner's own processing channel or in the world's evidence. Its **magnitude** is not the target. The world in Sec.1 is built so that the locus carries **zero** information for default first-order prediction, but determines which costly check is worth buying. That is the identifiability lever (Sec.2).

## 1. World sketch W-REL (illustrative; nothing frozen)

**Per trial t:**
1. A hidden binary target X_t is drawn, uniform. (A variant with a skewed prior is noted in Sec.10, A4.)
2. The world emits n = 3 evidence bits b_{t,i} = X_t  XOR  W_{t,i}, where W_{t,i} ~ Bern(w_{E_t}) is world noise.
