### 3.3 R-BLIND / R-CONF-S (one control, both names; consult #23, Q8)

- **R-BLIND:** stateless. a_t = argmax_a [g0_a(r_t) - c_a] (ties P ? R ? M). Prediction F0. No update.
- **R-CONF-S (formula).**
  - a_t = argmax_a [g0_a(s_t) - c_a], with s_t = |p0_t - 1/2|.
  - At these rates s takes two values in one-to-one correspondence with r. So **R-CONF-S coincides with R-BLIND by definition**. It is recorded as **one control, "R-BLIND / R-CONF-S"**, with both names and the identity disclosed.
- **R-CONF-ENUM (descriptive only).** The 3^2 = 9 deterministic maps r -> action, applied at every cost. Consult #22: "the nine deterministic tables are an enumeration of mappings, not automatically nine versions of that formula." They are not eligible A-SEP controls.

### 3.4 R-CONF-A (complete cost-dependent threshold rule)

**State.** theta_t  in  [0, 1/2], with theta_1 = (s_U + s_Sp)/2.
- s_U = 456533/468700 - 1/2.
- s_Sp = 27/100.

**Action.**
- If s_t > theta_t: a_t = P.
- Otherwise: a_t = argmax_{a in {P,R,M}} [g0_a(r_t) - c_a] (ties P ? R ? M).

**Prediction.** F0.

**Update after X.**
- err_t = 1[d0_t != X_t], where d0_t is F0's pre-check decision on y_t, defined whatever the action.
- theta_{t+1} = min(1/2, max(0, theta_t + eta*(err_t - alpha\*))).

**Grid.** eta  in  {1/20, 1/10, 1/5} x alpha\*  in  {1/20, 1/10, 1/5}, giving 9 named controls.

**Terminal.** None.

**Analytic bound (restricted to this information set).** By L1, err histories are independent of the Z path, so V <= Sum_t v_blind(c_t).

### 3.5 R-HAC-MF (secondary; complete)

**State.**
- A q-table q(?, a) over ? = (r, g), where g  in  {none, 0, 1, 2, 3} is the y/y' agreement count (3 - n_d) at the most recent RECHECK in the episode.
- 10 ? x 3 actions.
- **Initialization** q = 1 everywhere (optimistic gross correctness). g_1 = none.

**Action.**
- v(?, a) = q(?, a) - c_a (costs visible).
- **eps-greedy distribution:** with probability 1 - eps, the argmax of v (ties P ? R ? M); with probability eps, uniform over {P, R, M}, each eps/3.
- So the greedy action has total probability 1 - 2eps/3, and each other action eps/3.
- Variants: eps  in  {0, 1/10}.

**Prediction.** F0.

**TD target (exact).** ? = 0: target_t = 1[d_t = X_t], gross correctness only, because cost enters at decision time.
- **Update:** q(?_t, a_t) <- q + (1/4)(target_t - q).
- Then, if a_t = R: g <- agreement count of (y_t, y'_t). No X is used in g.
- **Terminal:** the same update at t = T, with no bootstrap.

R-HAC-MF is explicitly a **myopic contextual action-value learner** (consult #23, Q5). No ? = 1 variant is included.
- Consult #23: "A gamma = 1 variant needs a separately specified return, cost treatment, bootstrap and terminal rule."
