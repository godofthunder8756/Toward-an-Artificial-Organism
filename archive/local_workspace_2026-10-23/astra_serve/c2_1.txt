**Eligibility (seed must satisfy all; otherwise non-pass, not invalid).**
1. It is valid (Sec.4).
2. It passes FINAL Sec.3 transfer, recomputed on that seed: F-TRAINED A-FIT on TEST and E(F-TRAINED) <= 1/2 E(F-INIT).
3. B_prefix > eps0.
4. ||delta_k||_F > eps0 for all k (primary) or for the block in question (secondary).

**Per-seed GRADED-SPECIFIC(target).** Both conditions must hold:
- **(D) Directional:** AUC_REV > AUC_rand,j for **every** j = 1...32, i.e. rank 1 of 33.
- **(M) Materiality:** AUC_REV > eps0 **and** AUC_REV >= **B_prefix / 4**.
  - The dose-averaged excess must reach at least a quarter of the training-attributable prefix-function benefit.
  - A curve that rises linearly to the full benefit at alpha = 1 has AUC = B_prefix/2, so this is stricter than PR #7's endpoint quarter rule applied to a linear curve. It is a conservative choice (Sec.12).

**Descriptive curve diagnostics** (reported, never gating, and never used to pick a dose):
- Delta_REV(alpha) at each dose, and the per-dose rank of REV among the 33 curves.
- Monotonicity: the number of non-decreasing steps out of 5.
- Saturation index Delta(1/2)/Delta(1), using the E3-v2 ratio rule.
- The same curves for L_cont, L_prefix^XOR and E_total.
- Random-curve summaries: median, maximum and the full 32 values.
