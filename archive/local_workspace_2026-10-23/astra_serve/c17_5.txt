[c17 part 6/17]
  Hence ||E_j||_F = ||delta||_F and <G, E_j> = <G, delta> for the assembled matrix.

  The family for block k is the (62-dimensional) sphere {e : ||e|| = ||d_k||, <g_k,e> = <g_k,d_k>}. **d_k itself lies on this sphere.** "Beat all 32" therefore asks whether the learned displacement is extreme, in dose-averaged loss, among displacements with the same norm and the same tangent loss slope.
- **Scope of matching.** The match holds at the tangent, at W_T only. At finite alpha the paths differ through second- and higher-order terms. That difference is the object of the test, not a defect.
- **Two-sided match (disclosed).** The match is two-sided. When <g_k,d_k> < 0, matched controls are tilted toward local loss decrease relative to isotropic controls. Appendix B found this in 15/20 existing seeds. **Matched controls are therefore not uniformly more stringent than PR #8's isotropic controls** (Sec.4.4, O1).
