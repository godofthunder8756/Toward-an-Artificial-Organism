- Boundary: if an "adaptive confidence" rule also updates from post-check outcomes or recheck agreement, it is classified as R-HAC, not R-CONF.

### 4.2 R-HAC: history-dependent adaptive control

- **R-HAC-MF (model-free).**
  - Feature vector ?_t = (read class, k-window counts of recheck agreements, k-window post-check errors per check type, cost bin).
  - Action values Q(?, a) are learned by TD(0) or Q-learning from reward, with eps-greedy or softmax exploration.
  - Free parameters: k, learning rate, exploration rate and temperature. The fitted class is frozen with its feature list.
  - Its state is action values and window counts, with no explicit locus variable and no cost-general reliability estimate.
- **R-HAC-MB (model-based).** A Bayes forward filter over (I, E), possibly quantized to B bits, plus expected-value action selection given the current costs. This is the analytic optimum. It is conventional (POMDP belief-state control), and it is itself a locus estimator.
- **R-HAC-ALL(B).** All deterministic B-bit finite-state controllers over the same quantized observables and actions. It is finite at fixed B, and it is the full same-resource class for a certificate.

### 4.3 Discriminating probes against R-HAC-MF

- **I-COST revaluation.** Shift (c_r, c_s) to an unseen block value. An assessment-plus-cost policy re-optimizes immediately. Cached Q-values under R-HAC-MF need re-learning, unless the cost is in ? and the new value was trained.
- **I-SWAP** of the locus content (Sec.5): R-HAC-MF has no separable locus content, so its analogous swap acts on window counts.

### 4.4 Expected reduction status (stated before any computation)

R-HAC-MB **contains** the role by construction. A learned GRU approaching optimal reward will plausibly be **behaviourally and interventionally equivalent to R-HAC-MB**, as M1 was to A_thr in W_sym (Sec.3.2 precedent). Separation is plausible against R-CONF (by D1 and D3) and possibly against R-HAC-MF (through I-COST and I-SWAP). Separation from R-HAC-MB, or from R-HAC-ALL at B >= the candidate's state size, is **not** expected. Sec.8 states the consequence and Sec.10.4 asks Astra how it should be classified.
