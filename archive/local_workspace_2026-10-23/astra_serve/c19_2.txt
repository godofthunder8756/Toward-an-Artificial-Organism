## 3. Target (fixed)

T(theta) = max( L_prefix(theta) / (tau*PD(q*)), E_total_TEST(theta) / thr_total_TEST, E_XOR_TEST(theta) / thr_XOR_TEST ).

tau = 0.25. **Target reached <=> T(theta) <= 1**, i.e. L_prefix <= 0.25*PD(q*) AND both TEST A-FIT inequalities hold (consult #18 Sec.7).

**tau disclosure (mandatory wording):**

> "tau = 0.25 was chosen after prior results were known. Across the 30 existing CAND checkpoints (seeds 100-109, 200-209, 300-309), L_prefix/PD ranged 0.034-0.110, i.e. L_prefix/(0.25*PD) <= 0.44, and the XOR A-FIT term was the binding component of T in all 30. tau = 0.25 was therefore informed by, and is not binding for, the unrestricted reference."

- theta is the **final-step** parameters (step 4000).
- L_prefix is uniform over all 256 histories, including TEST, as evaluation. E is TEST-weighted (`component_excess`). All are computed in float64 from the float32 checkpoint, using the frozen evaluator.
