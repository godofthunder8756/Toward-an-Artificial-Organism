**The four-state version.** The DRAFT four-state world (I  in  {ok, faulty} x E  in  {clean, noisy}) is **withdrawn from the admission design**. It "can remain a later extension requiring its own history-level audit" (consult #21, Q8).

### 1.1 Referent: the designated processing channel (Astra Q3)

The supplied front-end F is admissible as "the designated processing channel". However, consult #21 (verbatim): "Calling it part of the body does not by itself make its estimated reliability second-order evaluation. An otherwise identical external sensor can generate the same observations."

**Consequence.** Channel identity alone earns nothing. The C4-relevant content must be the Sec.3 assessment of the learner's **own conditional correctness**. A belief about F's reliability counts only through that.

### 1.2 History-level symmetry: lemma L1 (Astra edit 5; analytic, to be verified exactly)

**Lemma L1.** Under W-REL-B with any transition law for Z, any policy that depends only on observable history, and any history containing **no RECHECK observation**, the likelihood of the history is the same for every Z path. Hence P(Z_t | history) equals the prior propagated by the transition law.

**Proof sketch.** Given Z_t, each trial's emission is independent of the past. Policies are functions of observables, so action-probability factors cancel in the posterior. The per-trial emission laws of (X, y) and of (X, y, y'') are identical in S and E: TV = 0 exactly (D1). That follows because each of these reads has independent world and internal flips with per-read flip probability eps. So every non-RECHECK factor is constant in Z_t, and the likelihood is constant in the Z path.

RECHECK is the only informative event. It factors through 10 distinct S/E likelihood-ratio classes (sizing count, `plan_counts.py`).

**Status.** This is an analytic proof. Its exact verification (spec Sec.1, A-SYM) is **pending approval and not run**. The single-trial statement in DRAFT D1, "no learner can distinguish...", is **not** carried into the persistent setting except through L1 (Astra edit 5).
