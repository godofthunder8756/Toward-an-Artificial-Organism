
[c21 part 3/13]
3. The learner's **own processing channel** (a front-end F, see dependency flag F1) reads each bit: y_{t,i} = b_{t,i}  XOR  N_{t,i}, where N_{t,i} ~ Bern(e_{I_t}) is internal processing noise. The learner receives y_t.
4. The learner chooses one action:
   - PREDICT now;
   - RECHECK, at cost c_r: the same evidence bits b_t are re-processed by F with fresh internal noise, giving y'_t;
   - RESAMPLE, at cost c_s: fresh world bits are drawn and read once by F, giving y''_t.

   It then outputs a first-order prediction p_t = P(X_t = 1 | ...).
5. **Feedback:** the outcome X_t (or correct/incorrect) and the realized cost. Reward = 1[correct] - cost. (A scoring-rule variant is noted in Sec.10, A5.)

**Hidden persistent states** (Markov, persistent, independent):
- **I_t  in  {ok, faulty}:** reliability of the learner's own processing, with internal noise e_ok < e_faulty.
- **E_t  in  {clean, noisy}:** reliability of the evidence, with world noise w_clean < w_noisy.
- The rates are chosen so that **(faulty, clean)** and **(ok, noisy)** have equal per-read flip probability: eps = w + e - 2we is symmetric under the swap w <-> e, so the match is exact and analytic, not tuned.
- These two cells are the **matched system-change / evidence-change pair**. They satisfy the requirement in the C4-CMP1 design (`c_axis_theory_queue_v2.md` Sec.4.2, I4/I5) that the match "is to be shown analytically, not tuned".

**What the learner does NOT receive:**
- fault labels;
- I_t or E_t;
- oracle confidence;
- the Bayes posterior;
- any supplied reliability register;
- any per-read correctness signal beyond the trial outcome.

Costs are visible as inputs: (c_r, c_s) vary across blocks and are shown each trial. That is needed for the revaluation probe in Sec.4.

**Learner (for a later empirical study; not specified here as an experiment):**
- A generic GRU in the existing family.
- Inputs: y, y' or y'' when taken, the previous outcome, and the costs.
- Two readouts: a first-order prediction head and an action head.
- Trained on reward, plus an outcome-prediction loss from the revealed X_t. The outcome is ordinary feedback, not a fault label.
- No separate evaluator module and no protected state.

The learning rule (RL over actions) differs from the PR #7 supervised recipe. This is flagged as F3.

**Illustrative rates used only in Appendix A:**
- e_ok = w_clean = 1/20;
- e_faulty = w_noisy = 1/5;
- so cell S = (faulty, clean) has (w, e) = (1/20, 1/5), cell E = (ok, noisy) has (w, e) = (1/5, 1/20), and eps = 23/100 in both;
- costs c_r = 1/50, c_s = 3/50.
