**Open questions:**
- **O1 (most important; feasibility finding).** On the 20 existing seeds, <G, delta> is **negative in 15/20**: 8/10 for seeds 100-109 and 7/10 for 200-209. cos(G, delta) ranges from -0.40 to +0.13. The first-order prediction <G,delta>/8 at alpha = 1/8 is between -1.39x and +0.23x the observed Delta_REV(1/8), which is positive in every seed. Locally, reversion **lowers** L_prefix to first order in most seeds. Its early-dose damage is therefore not first-order, contrary to the hypothesis in consult #16 Sec.3 and in my PR #8 review Sec.3.

  Two consequences follow:
  - (a) The two-sided matched family is tilted toward loss decrease in those seeds, so it is likely *less* stringent than isotropic there. FO_AUC magnitudes reach ~1e-3, against PR #8 margins of 7e-5 to 2.4e-3.
  - (b) A REPLICATED label against FO controls alone could be weaker evidence than PR #8 in exactly the thin-margin seeds.
