- What it does: majority-decodes the 126 program bits in `traces[0]`, splits them into nine 14-bit rules, and returns the action of the first enabled rule whose mask is contained in the observation. Otherwise it returns 9 (`ac5_program.py:L33-L41@6c6323b`).

## 1. Comparators, as equations

**E&S (Elliott & Shadbolt 1998).** Here s_xi^alpha is the number of synapses from afferent i (eye alpha) onto cortical cell x, and a is the recent average activity.
- Release: r_y = T0 + T1 * Sum_betaj s_yj^beta a_j^beta / Sum_betaj s_yj^beta. T0 is activity-independent (or exogenous) release and T1 is the maximum activity-dependent release.
- Dynamics (Eq. 1, see the transcription caveat above): ds_xi^alpha/dt = eps s_xi^alpha [ Sum_y Delta_xy r_y (a + a_i^alpha) / Sum_betaj s_yj^beta (a + a_j^beta) - 1 ]. Delta_xy is Gaussian diffusion of the trophic factor and a is constitutive uptake.
- Key property: afferents segregate only if T0/T1 is below a critical value.

**vO&W (van Ooyen & Willshaw 1999, as restated by the authors).** One target and n axons.
- dC_i/dt = (k_a,i L R_i - k_d,i C_i) - rho_i C_i
- dR_i/dt = phi_i - gamma_i R_i - (k_a,i L R_i - k_d,i C_i)
- dL/dt = sigma - deltaL - Sum_i (k_a,i L R_i - k_d,i C_i)/v
[c28 part 15/26]
- Growth lag: dphi_i/dt = (f_i(C_i) - phi_i)/tau, where f_i is increasing (a Hill-type form in the general case).
- Key property: positive feedback (more receptors, more uptake) gives competitive elimination. The target releases at a constant rate sigma. Activity can enter through the rate constants.

**DP (Di Paolo 2000).** A fully connected 8-neuron CTRNN.
- tau_i dy_i/dt = -y_i + Sum_j w_ji z_j + I_i, with z_j = 1/(1 + exp[-(y_j + b_j)]).
- Plasticity rules (one per connection, evolved):
  - R0: Deltaw_ij = delta eta_ij p_j z_i z_j
  - R1: Deltaw_ij = delta eta_ij p_j (z_i - z_ij^o) z_j
  - R2: Deltaw_ij = delta eta_ij p_j z_i (z_j - z_ij^o)
  - R3: Deltaw_ij = 0
- Facilitation p_j is a function of y_j + b_j. It is zero inside a fixed band (Fig. 2: from -2 to 2), ramps linearly to +/-1 outside the band, and saturates at +/-4. delta is a linear damping factor that keeps weights in range.
- Fitness during evolution rewards both phototaxis and time spent within the band.
- **Key property: the essential variables (the firing bands) are fixed and the same for every neuron, and evolution selects networks in which losing the band is coupled to losing the behavior.**

**E&B (Egbert & Barandiaran 2014), the IDSM.** Each node is N = <p, v, w>, a position and a velocity in normalized sensorimotor space plus a weight.
- A node is created when the local density ?(x) < 1.
- Eq. 4 (structure only): dN_w/dt = -(steady degradation) + (reinforcement that grows as d(N_p, x) shrinks).
- Motor influence is the velocity factor plus an attraction factor toward familiar sensorimotor states, weighted by omega(N_w) and normalized by ?.
- **Key property: structure persists only if the behavior revisits it, and the attraction factor actively pulls the agent toward familiar states.**

**L&vR (Li & van Rossum 2020); vR&P (van Rossum & Pache 2024).** The metabolic cost of plasticity is M_alpha = Sum_{i,j,t} |Deltaw_ij(t)|^alpha, assuming no spatial or temporal interaction in cost.
- L&vR: "synaptic caching". Updates go into a transient component and are consolidated into the persistent weight once they cross a threshold. Transient weights decay and cost a small amount to maintain.
- vR&P: competitive restriction. Only the largest-magnitude updates are applied, and plasticity is limited to connected input-hidden-output paths.
- **Key property: plasticity is charged to a budget, and competition decides which synapses get to change. Nothing in these models links that cost to behavior.**

**K&G (Keramati & Gutkin 2014).**
- Drive: D(H_t) = (Sum_i |h_i* - h_i,t|^n)^(1/m).
- Reward: r(H_t, K_t) = D(H_t) - D(H_t + K_t).
- An RL learner maximizes discounted reward, which is equivalent to minimizing discounted deviation from the setpoint.
- **Key property: there is a setpoint H*, and the policy has value-learning access to the internal state (or to a prediction of the outcome K_t).**
[c28 part 16/26]

## 2. UDTN, now written out completely (closes Astra Sec.4's "define the mechanism")

State per unit i: activity y_i and z_i, bias b_i, and resource r_i. State per synapse j->i: weight w_ij and stability s_ij.

- (U1) Dynamics: tau dy_i/dt = -y_i + Sum_j w_ij z_j + I_i, with z = sigma(y + b). This is the DP and Beer CTRNN form.
- (U2) Persistence: dw_ij/dt = Prod_ij - lambda(1 - s_ij)(w_ij - w), and ds_ij/dt = -s_ij/tau_s + kappa q_ij.
- (U3) Inflow: a constant, state-blind supply sigma_in enters **only at the output (motor) units**, split equally between them. This is the analogue of vO&W's target release sigma and of E&S's T0.
- (U4) Retrograde routing: unit i sends T_i->j = beta r_i * u_ij / Sum_k u_ik to each presynaptic unit j. A fraction theta is used on the way to maintain synapse ij, so q_ij = theta T_i->j, and the rest arrives at j.
- (U5) Plasticity: Prod_ij = eta g(r_min - r_i) H_ij. H_ij is a Hebbian or BCM term of DP's R0-R2 kind. g(d) = 0 for d <= 0 and rises for d > 0, so **plasticity is switched on by a resource deficit at the unit, not by its firing leaving a fixed band.** The plasticity cost c_p |Prod_ij|^alpha is charged to r_i (the L&vR M_alpha).
- (U6) Resource ledger, per unit and in total:
  - dr_i/dt = inflow_i + Sum_k (1 - theta) T_k->i - Sum_j T_i->j - c_a z_i - c_m Sum_j s_ij - c_p Sum_j |Prod_ij|^alpha - delta r_i
  - Total: d(Sum r)/dt = sigma_in - [computation cost] - [maintenance cost, including the theta share] - [plasticity cost] - [outflow delta Sum r]
  - Internal transfers cancel, and that has to be asserted each step to a declared tolerance.
- (U7) The routing signal u_ij comes in two named variants (named change 4):
  - **U-ACT (co-activity):** u_ij = |w_ij| z_i z_j. This is what v1.1 meant, and from now on it is called co-activity routing.
  - **U-FUNC (output-sensitivity routing):** u_ij = |w_ij| z_j * S_i, where S_i = E||Deltay_out||^2 / sigma_xi^2 is the sensitivity of the network's *output* to small perturbations xi injected at unit i. It is estimated by node perturbation, and the same estimator is applied to every unit.
    - S_i is defined without reference to any target or loss. It measures how much the output depends on unit i, not whether that output is good. That meets Astra's requirement that functional use be defined independently of the desired outcome.
    - Under Astra's frozen substrate rule, S_i is a generic transformation: it is non-local, but it is computed the same way for any organization. It is therefore admissible as substrate, provided it is declared and reported as non-local.
[c28 part 17/26]
    - **Known limitation:** perturbing one unit at a time exposes redundant pathways, because each of a redundant pair has a low S_i. It does **not** expose mutually cancelling pairs, because perturbing one member breaks the cancellation and gives it a high S_i. Cancelling pairs need group perturbation, which is left open.

## 2. Environment and organizations

- **Task.** A two-choice discrimination, where output y  in  {L, R} is determined by a task cue X that is always present. It is identical for every arm.
[c28 part 20/26]
- **Orienting action.** On each trial the agent also emits o  in  {1, 2}. That brings modulatory stimulus M1 or M2 into view. **Neither M carries task information**, so the task mapping X -> y does not depend on o.

- **Organizations A and B** are identical except for D. Under D_A, P is driven by M1; under D_B, by M2. Both are hand-constructed for G2, with no training.
- **Function match (required before any outcome is read), with plasticity off:**
  - (i) Task outputs are identical across a declared test battery, max |y_A - y_B| <= tol_f.
  - (ii) Mean firing rate per unit is matched within tol_z. This neutralizes DP's fixed bands.
  - (iii) Total resource inflow sigma_in is equal.
  - A pair that fails this match is INVALID, not a non-pass.

## 3. The reassignment intervention

At t_R, swap D on a running organization (D_A -> D_B), and touch **nothing else**: no change to w, s, r, the readout or the orienting weights. Check that the swap is byte-inert at the intact boundary (T-INERT): with o held fixed and plasticity off, the task output is unchanged.

- **Primary outcome (scalar, prespecified).** Deltapref = P(o = 2) over the final quarter of the decoupling window, minus P(o = 2) over the quarter before t_R.
- **Decoupling window.** W = [t_R, t_R + 3tau_s], where tau_s is the unsupported-stability time constant, fixed before any run. Outcomes are read only at the end of the window, not as an immediate collapse (Astra Sec.4).

## 6. Prespecified outcome rule [thresholds PROVISIONAL until G1 freezes them]

**SUPPORTS the dependency-specific-regulation claim** only if all of the following hold:
1. REASSIGN gives Deltapref > eps_pref.
2. YOKED-SENSORY gives Deltapref < eps_pref.
3. FREEZE-ORIENT abolishes the shift (Deltapref < eps_pref).
[c28 part 22/26]
4. INFO-CLAMP abolishes the shift in UDTN. This is a necessity check, not evidence for the claim.
5. POLICY-TRANSPLANT alone does not reproduce REASSIGN's trajectory signature, which is a rise in orienting variability during W before the preference settles.
6. Literal DP gives Deltapref < eps_pref. DP-R is judged by Sec.10.1 (RELOCATE, then N2) and not by this item.
7. **YOKED-RELIEF (Sec.10), read with DeltaV.** k_e is read only on admissible events (search before relief). UDTN's post-relief keep-probability stays >= kappa_hi on every relief event, while K&G's falls to <= kappa_lo by the declared final block of relief events. If UDTN's keep-probability also decays toward baseline, the distinction from K&G is NOT DEMONSTRATED, and under named change 7 the claim is retired to "an alternative implementation of homeostatic regulation". SATISFICE is reported separately, against R-RLMAX only.

Each comparison follows B1-style directional rules against the strongest control. The seed bar defaults to 8 of 10, and more than two invalid seeds gives UNRESOLVED; both are frozen per study.

Other outcomes:
- **One outcome the closest baseline would not predict:** under DP, with function and firing matched, Deltapref ~= 0. **A positive Deltapref under matched firing, abolished by FREEZE-ORIENT, is predicted by UDTN (N1) and not by DP.**
- **STIM-ONLY, read separately.** If P is retained under STIM-ONLY, routing is behaving as co-activity, and per named change 4 it has to be called that. If P is not retained under U-FUNC but is retained under U-ACT, N2 earns a distinct intervention prediction.
- **Retirement (named change 7).** If the K&G rival with matched observables reproduces every intervention signature, retire the claim "UDTN implements dependency-specific regulation by a route other than internal-state reward." Retained causal evidence stays recorded.
- **G2 failure.** If no hand-built UDTN satisfying the Sec.2 match produces Deltapref > eps_pref, report Q-EXIST NOT DEMONSTRATED. That means the architecture, as specified, cannot express the motivating phenomenon (see the credit-path finding in Sec.3 of the comparison).

## 9. The shortfall threshold r_min: who sets it, and why it is still a supplied condition

- **Who sets it.** The designer sets it. It is one scalar, frozen at G1, applied to every unit, and reported as **SUPPLIED** under named change 3. K&G's setpoint is set to the same value, so neither model gets an advantage from it.
- **What it does not specify.** r_min does not say *which* structures persist. Whether a unit falls short depends only on acquired, declared state: its weights, and whether something downstream that is driving the output still uses it. The organization-specific part lives in declared state, as Astra's substrate rule requires.
- **What it does specify, and why the admissible claim stays conditional.** r_min is a designer-chosen persistence condition. It decides *that* deficits trigger search, and how severe a deficit has to be. That is exactly the kind of supplied condition Astra's strongest objection is about, and no result here removes it. The admissible claim stays "dependency-specific regulation under fixed supplied dynamics", not autonomous value.
- **Guards against smuggling.**
  - (a) **R_MIN-SWEEP.** The primary outcome must hold across a declared range of r_min, frozen at G1, with the same seed bar at each level. If it holds only at a narrow tuned value, the result is labeled TUNING-DEPENDENT.
  - (b) **SMUGGLE control.** A per-unit r_min that targets P specifically, i.e. a designer-written preference. It shows what smuggling looks like, and the reported effect must not depend on any such per-unit setting.
  - (c) **Substrate check.** The same r_min and g run unchanged on an organization whose acquired dependencies make it fail (from the comparison, Sec.4).

## 10. YOKED-RELIEF: the discriminator against K&G

**Rationale.** K&G assigns credit: TD learning raises the value of an action only to the extent that, across experience, the action predicts drive reduction. UDTN (N1) assigns no credit. Its orienting search simply stops whenever the shortfall clears, whatever caused the clearing. So the two models should come apart when relief is real but *not contingent on* the action.

**Procedure.**
1. After t_R, put the agent back into shortfall repeatedly (E declared relief events).
2. At each event, clear r_P to r_min with an EXTERNAL pulse whose timing is copied from a yoked run, so it is independent of the current orientation. The pulse never overshoots r_min, which removes any K&G overshoot asymmetry.
3. Contingent sustaining through o is turned off during this phase.
4. Record the orientation o* held at each relief time, and whether the agent keeps o* until the next shortfall begins.

**Measures.**
[c28 part 24/26]
- **Keep-probability after relief**, k_e = P(keep o* | relief event e).
- **Orienting variability** in the window after relief, compared with the window before it.

**Predictions** (equations: UDTN U5; K&G Eqs. 1-2 with a TD learner):
- **UDTN.** Once the shortfall clears, g = 0, so plasticity stops and the orientation held at relief stays fixed. k_e ~= 1 on *every* event, and variability collapses after each relief. The kept options track o* and are roughly uniform across options, which is superstitious stopping with no learning across events.
- **K&G.** Each relief adds a positive TD update to the action that was current, so there may be a transient keep bias early on. Because relief is the same for every action, the action values converge and k_e decays to the baseline set by its declared exploration rate. Its post-relief variability returns to that baseline.
- **R-RLMAX.** Same as K&G.
- **DP, literal** (fixed activation bands on y+b, firing matched as in Sec.2 (ii)). The induced resource shortfall does not enter DP's equations, and matched firing keeps every unit inside its bands, so DP's plasticity never switches on. Its orientation never changes, so k_e ~= 1 **trivially**, and the Specialist is right that k_e does not separate it from UDTN. What does separate them is variability: DP shows no rise in variability during the shortfall, so DeltaV ~= 0.
- **DP-R** (the strongest DP rival). This is Di Paolo's gate with the essential variable moved from activation onto resource: plasticity is on while r_i is below a fixed bound and off once r_i is back above it. **On YOKED-RELIEF, DP-R predicts exactly what UDTN predicts**: k_e ~= 1 with no decay, and DeltaV > 0. **No behavioral measure in this arm separates DP-R from UDTN.** See Sec.10.1.
- **Trophic cascade.** No prediction, because there is no deficit-gated search over orientation.

**Added measure, the relief-locked variability contrast.** DeltaV = Var(o) in the shortfall window before relief, minus Var(o) in the window after relief, each measured against the declared baseline. A k_e result is admissible only on events where the window before relief shows search, meaning Var(o) is above baseline + eps_V. This stops a network that never searches from scoring "lock-in".
- UDTN: DeltaV > eps_V, and k_e >= kappa_hi with no decay.
- K&G: DeltaV -> 0 as its action values converge, and k_e decays to its baseline.
- Literal DP: there is no search before relief, so its k_e events are inadmissible and DeltaV ~= 0.
- DP-R: the same as UDTN.

**Separating outcome.** YOKED-RELIEF separates UDTN from K&G and from literal DP:
- From K&G: k_e stays high and does not decay.
- From literal DP: DeltaV > 0.

It does **not** separate UDTN from DP-R.

**Caveats.**
[c28 part 25/26]
- A K&G learner with a very small learning rate decays slowly. The learning rate and the number of events E therefore have to be frozen at G1, with E chosen so that the K&G reference decays below kappa_lo analytically.
- If K&G is given a prior that reliefs are contingent, it may lock in as well. Its prior is set to neutral and reported.
- Superstitious stopping is a *limitation* of UDTN, not a virtue. It is reported as such: it shows UDTN regulates dependencies without assigning credit, which bounds what the system "wants".

### 10.1 Separating UDTN from DP-R

DP-R comes in two forms, and they are handled differently.

- **(a) DP-R with its bounds placed by the designer on P's units.** This is the SMUGGLE control of Sec.9(b), relabeled. **RELOCATE** separates it from UDTN:
  - Use a second hand-built organization whose acquired weights make a different unit set, P', the vulnerable pathway. Apply the same uniform r_min and g, with no re-specification.
  - UDTN predicts search and Deltapref > eps_pref on P'.
  - DP-R(a), whose bounds are still on P, predicts no search.
- **(b) DP-R with a uniform resource bound on every unit.** Its gate is then the N1 gate itself. On every arm in this spec, (b) and UDTN differ only in the routing that sets r_i. U-ACT routing makes UDTN identical to DP-R(b). U-FUNC routing differs from it only through N2.

**Consequence, under named change 7.** If DP-R(b) with co-activity routing reproduces REASSIGN, YOKED-RELIEF and RELOCATE:
- N1 is retired to "Di Paolo's ultrastability with resource-valued essential variables". This is prior art, so it is no longer a novelty claim.
- The only thing left to separate them is N2, tested by STIM-ONLY (U-FUNC routing versus U-ACT routing).

The retirement is recorded as a result, not hidden.

## 11. ANTICIPATE: the second discriminator against K&G (proposed by Research Delegate)

**Rationale.**
- K&G, with gamma < 1 and the cue in its state, can learn to orient *before* a predicted shortfall.
- UDTN's gate g(r_min - r_i) is zero until the shortfall is actually present, so its search cannot start any earlier.

**Procedure.**
1. After t_R, a cue Q precedes each induced shortfall onset by a fixed delay Delta_Q.
2. Q is function-matched and support-neutral: it carries no task information, does not change P's drive or r_P, and is given to every model, with K&G receiving it in its state.
3. Shortfalls are induced on the same yoked schedule as Sec.10.

**Measure.** Pre-onset orienting change, A = P(switch o in [t_Q, t_onset)) minus the baseline switch rate.

**Predictions.**
- **K&G:** A > eps_A once trained.
- **UDTN:** A ~= 0.
- **Literal DP:** A ~= 0.
- **DP-R:** A ~= 0.

[c28 part 26/26]
**The cue-delay guard (this matters).** While UDTN's plasticity is on during a shortfall, Hebbian co-activity can bind a trace of Q to the orienting change. That could produce anticipation that looks like K&G's but needs no prediction of drive at all.
- Delta_Q must therefore exceed UDTN's longest declared activity trace, tau_trace = max(tau_i, tau_s-coupled eligibility). This is computed analytically from U1-U5 and frozen at G1.
- Each UDTN run also reports a **Q-TRACE check**: the correlation between Q and pre-onset orientation, with plasticity frozen outside shortfall windows. This confirms that no binding occurred.
- If UDTN shows A > eps_A while Delta_Q > tau_trace, the cause is unexplained. ANTICIPATE is then reported as NOT DISCRIMINATING and not counted for either model.

**Outcome item 8.** This is a K&G discriminator alongside item 7. It is not required for SUPPORTS. A K&G learner that shows A ~= 0 under the frozen gamma and Delta_Q invalidates the arm, because the reference failed, rather than counting as a success for UDTN.
