## 0. Framing (fixed)

This study is a **search for compensation and counterexamples to necessity. It is not a necessity certificate.**

The question it asks is narrow. Suppose every hidden-to-hidden matrix `weight_hh` is held at its random initialization W0 at all eight updates, and the input weights, both biases and the readout are trained instead. Can that remaining computation still reach the declared target T(theta) <= 1?

Consult #18, verbatim:

> "Compensation would not negate the existing causal result: parameters can contribute causally in one trained implementation without being indispensable to another."

> "One verified witness refutes absolute impossibility for that particular restricted class and target. An 8/10 rule establishes reproducibility, not the logical minimum needed for a counterexample."

> "Failure means only 'not reached within declared search.'"

A failure is not a certificate. The certificate Astra described is a validated lower bound inf T > 1 over the entire declared restricted domain, or a complete search of an explicitly finite class. This protocol does not attempt one (Sec.8.3).
