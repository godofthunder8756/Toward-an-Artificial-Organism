**Lemma L1.** Under W-REL-B with any transition law for Z, any policy that depends only on observable history, and any history containing **no RECHECK observation**, the likelihood of the history is the same for every Z path. Hence P(Z_t | history) equals the prior propagated by the transition law.

**Proof sketch.** Given Z_t, each trial's emission is independent of the past. Policies are functions of observables, so action-probability factors cancel in the posterior. The per-trial emission laws of (X, y) and of (X, y, y'') are identical in S and E: TV = 0 exactly (D1). That follows because each of these reads has independent world and internal flips with per-read flip probability eps. So every non-RECHECK factor is constant in Z_t, and the likelihood is constant in the Z path.

RECHECK is the only informative event. It factors through 10 distinct S/E likelihood-ratio classes (sizing count, `plan_counts.py`).

**[v3] Assumptions.** L1 holds under the Sec.1 independence and exogeneity assumptions (i)-(iv).
