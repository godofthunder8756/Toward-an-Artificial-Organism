## 13. Ambiguities resolved in this draft (for Astra to confirm)

1. **"32/48"** means the readout hidden width H. "Parameter-matched" means *trainable-count* matched (843 = 843), not stored-total matched (1035) and not matched in composition.
2. **Naming hazard:** the bare number "843" is banned in reports (Sec.1).
3. **Freezing mode:** the existing `freeze_recurrent=True` is F-INIT semantics. R-FIX needs a W_hh-only freeze (Sec.4). It is a new harness function, not a new architecture.
4. **Paired inits:** CAND and R-FIX-32 share the full init. R-FIX-48 shares W0 and the full GRU core, plus fc1.weight rows 0-31. Its other readout tensors differ (Sec.2).
5. **Episode streams:** the same generator seed 1000+s for all arms, and TEST-only rejection, as for PR #7 CAND.
6. **Validation selection and restarts:** none. One final-step checkpoint per run. This is the most conservative reading of "freeze validation selection, restarts". It also means a failure is weak evidence (Sec.7, Q4).
7. **L_prefix definition:** the `l_prefix` of graded_reversion_outcomes.py, uniform over all 256 histories (the definition that gives PD = 0.0138386). The TEST-only and probability-weighted variants are not used.
