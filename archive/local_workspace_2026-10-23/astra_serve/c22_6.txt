The three effects are separated and reported separately (consult #21, Q4):
1. **Downstream prediction changes caused by selecting different checks.** These are expected consequences of the intervention, and are reported.
2. **Direct prediction changes under identical check observations.** The intervention changes pa given the same y and y' (the D5 precision route). "The second category limits the dissociation claim; it cannot simply be declared irrelevant."
3. **Changes in checking while a prespecified first-order probe battery is preserved.** The battery is the pre-check p0 on every read pattern x a fixed history bank. Only this category supports C-SEL, and only to the extent category 2 permits.

**Tolerances: ILLUSTRATIVE, not proposed for freezing.** delta_p = 0.02, delta_acc = 0.005 and delta_LL = 2% are retained as placeholders only. Consult #21 (verbatim): "A maximum probability change of 0.02 can matter substantially to checking decisions; the demonstrated whole-trial value gap is only about 0.006. Freeze margins only after determining whether tolerated prediction changes could explain the checking effect."

Margins are to be derived later, from the decision sensitivity of each named rival to tolerated p changes (open question 4).

### 5.3 Identifying assessment content (Astra Q5) [replaces DRAFT Sec.5.3]

**Target.**
- The DRAFT decoder target (the posterior over the internal-fault factor alone) is withdrawn as incomplete.
- The analysis-only reference is the **posterior over the specified latent process**, P(Z_t | history). In W-REL-B this is complete for the latent process.
- The construct-level target is **the learner's own conditional correctness E[K_t(a) | history] per action** (Sec.3).
- Both are disclosed as analysis-only oracles, never training signals.

**Requirements.**
- Held-out identification.
- Rank and stability checks: subspace dimension, and stability across splits and seeds.
- Donor controls: random-donor, same-belief-donor and first-order-matched donor.
- Intervention specifications fixed **independently of the evaluation outcomes**.

Consult #21 (verbatim): "Decoder accuracy alone does not validate the transplant direction".

### 5.4 Criteria

- **C-REF (referent).** Under I-SYS versus I-EVID, checking propensities diverge in the predicted directions, beyond prespecified margins.
- **C-T2 (type-2 content).**
  - A_t predicts the learner's **own** conditional correctness K_t(a) per action, better than pre-check confidence s_t does, with a prespecified margin.
  - False alarms and misses are scored against the learner's own K_t (Sec.3), and track I-CORRUPT and I-SWAP.
- **C-SEL (selective causal content).** I-SWAP and I-CORRUPT produce directional assessment errors with the specified downstream checking consequence. This requires Sec.5.2 category 3, constrained by category 2, and a credited I-RESCUE as in Sec.5.1.
