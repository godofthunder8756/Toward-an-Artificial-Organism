**The principal mathematical findings follow.**

1. D1 is correct under its stated symmetry premises.

For a representative r and flip g, let:

```text
R_g = diag(r_x, r_y, chi_g)
S_g = diag(1, 1, chi_g)
G_(g.r) = R_g G_r R_g
```

Then:

```text
sum_g G_(g.r)
    = 4 diag(G_r[xx], G_r[yy], G_r[kk])

sum_g G_(g.r) S_g
    = 4 diag(G_r[xx], G_r[yy], 0)
```

Consequently, with the required invertibility:

```text
B_global = [c_x; c_y; 0]
B_mu_global = [c_x; c_y]
```

This establishes equality of the two predicted perturbed charts, and therefore equality of their finite forecast predictions wherever propagation is defined.

However, C4.2(d)'s expression

```text
L_a[:,kappa] * chi_a * c_tau * delta
```

is the first-order forecast discrepancy. It is not generally the exact finite discrepancy.

The exact discrepancy is:

```text
Phi(theta_a + S_g C delta, u)
    - Phi(theta_a + B_global delta, u)
```

The inherited Phi includes normalized Bayesian updates. It is not globally linear in theta.

Likewise, C4.2(c) should say that the chart increment is exactly `S_g C delta` on a non-crossing edit. `J_a delta` is its first-order forecast response.

This correction preserves the cancellation result. It changes the explanation of the residual.

2. D2 is correct with full-column-rank recovery. "Resolved" alone is insufficient.

Within a class:

```text
J_a = L_a S_g C
```

If the stacked full design has resolved rank 3, least squares uniquely recovers `S_g C`. Finite prediction is then exact on valid, non-crossing edits.

If the design has lower rank, a minimum-norm solution can reproduce fitting responses without reproducing the intended coefficient matrix or held-out responses.

Apply that distinction consistently to SYS-O6, SYS-PC, SYS-FK and SYS-NC2. C4.3's statement that D4 holds "for every resolved design" is too broad. Full-rank recovery, or a separate proof of prediction invariance across the null space, is required.

There is useful analytic support here. In the inherited W2 world, the transition has nonzero eigenvalues:

```text
1, 0.576, 0.570, 0.912
```

Observation likelihoods are strictly positive. The normalized continuation map is locally invertible on the interior simplex, and the three reported forecasts determine the terminal belief. Thus each valid baseline's exact 3-column L has full rank.

That supports the exact-rank premises. It does not establish numerical rank under the frozen singular-value thresholds.

3. D3's nonnegative gain follows from exact full prediction, not merely from model nesting.

When the full predictor is exact on the evaluated edits:

```text
L_full = 0
gain = L_mu >= 0
```

That conclusion is sound. In general, nested models fitted on fitting prefixes need not have nonnegative held-out gain. Keep negative gains for confounds and SYS-LIM.

Also correct the wording around `B_mu`: the adjustment to `C_mu` is generally nonzero. Saying the whole `B_mu` is "generally != 0" does not express the intended point.

4. SYS-LIM's formulas need explicit first-order qualification.

D6's coefficient formula is sound under P0:

```text
lambda = (s_1 K_1 + s_2 K_2)/(K_1 + K_2)
```

For an A-containing fit with the other orbit X:

```text
lambda = (K_A - K_X)/(K_A + K_X)
```

The stated first-order gain condition follows:

```text
(1 + lambda)^2 < 1  iff  K_A < K_X
```

But the loss ratio `(1 + lambda)^2`, and the wrong-sign factor 4, are Jacobian-level results. They are not automatically exact at eps_syn = 0.1 or 0.25.

Equality of a predicted chart with the actual chart does give exact finite recovery. Scaling a chart error does not generally scale the finite forecast error by the same factor.

5. The global cancellation identity needs direct checks.

Equal pooled losses alone do not establish equal predictors. Different predictions can have equal losses.

For SYS-PC global, check all three:

```text
B_global approximately [c_x; c_y; 0]
B_mu_global approximately [c_x; c_y]
Pred_full approximately Pred_mu on the required valid edits
```

Report loss equality as a consequence. Do not make it the sole operational definition of "GLOBAL CANCELLATION AS DERIVED."
