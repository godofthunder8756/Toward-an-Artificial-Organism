B. Answers Q1-Q5

Q1. Substrate operations, birth targeting and the closure sentence

The primitive/policy distinction is right, but "the four steps are substrate, and only their order violates the rule" is too narrow.

Abstract copy, equality comparison, write and erase operations can qualify as substrate. The actual routines also choose sources, destinations, budgets, preconditions and subsequent operations. Those choices need their own classification.

For example:

- Copying an addressed source to an addressed destination is generic.
- Always selecting the next description slot is a targeting policy.
- Comparing two strings is generic.
- Making a successful comparison authorize this particular pointer/MODE transaction is organizational coordination unless represented in declared content.
- Erasing an addressed region is generic.
- Automatically erasing the previous active slot is a policy.

The audit recognizes several of these distinctions in its prose. Its table should label the extracted primitives as substrate, rather than giving blanket clearance to the complete code sections.

For `ac9.py:108`, "every bank with demand" is not necessarily a priority ordering. It is a supplied production-targeting rule. On the description provided, I would not clear it as generic chemistry yet.

The relevant distinction is:

- A generic reaction acting on declared local substrates, receptors or demand markers can be substrate.
- A host operation identifying the program bank and every semantically designated memory bank, then distributing births to them, supplies organizational targeting.

The privileged inclusion of bank 0 needs explanation too. Show what "demand" reads, where it is represented, how targeting changes when that content changes, and how resource competition is resolved. Calling the rule chemistry does not settle this.

Fixed rule width, first-match interpretation and a fixed instruction vocabulary can be admissible syntax. A system need not have a universal programming language. However, forced enablement of designated bank rules must be disclosed as an expressivity restriction. It cannot be classified as harmless syntax solely because other fields remain mutable. The question is whether it hard-wires an organization-specific action that declared content cannot suppress or redirect.

The revised closure sentence is directionally correct but still overstates the W/C/B clause while birth targeting remains contested. It also risks preserving the headline through "closure holds conditional on external coordination."

Use the firmer statement:

"The audited system exhibits the reported component-production, repair and reconstruction behavior under supplied organizational coordination. Under the adopted substrate rule, production closure of the full declared network is not established."

Retain the individual empirical findings with their original scope. Do not automatically re-certify all C1-C5 criteria for W/C/B through this code-reading exercise.

Also qualify T1: constants are not inherently forbidden. Fixed reaction rates, instruction widths and numerical integration rules can be substrate. Their organizational role, not their being constants, decides the classification.

Q2. Is Track A finished, or should coordination move into maintained state?

The current architecture's closure question is answered under this rule: the reported behavior depends on supplied organizational coordination, and the full closure claim is not established.

Stop further closure experiments on that unchanged architecture. Keep U3 on hold unless its separate content-to-machinery causal claim is independently worth pursuing. U3 cannot internalize a schedule by demonstrating stronger dependence on it.

A damageable transition table is a plausible successor design, but moving Python branches into an array is insufficient. The successor must account for:

- Production, copying, repair and replacement of the table itself.
- Its triggers, addressing rules, budgets and exclusions.
- The mechanism that schedules its execution.
- Bootstrapping and inherited initial content.
- Whether corrupted but admissible tables execute without host restoration of the intended policy.
- Any protected interpreter state or hidden backup.

An inherited initial table is compatible with the declared convention. An indefinitely protected host copy that reinstalls it would preserve the problem.

This would be a material architectural change. Whether the project calls it a new "family" depends on its architecture taxonomy; that terminology should not determine authorization. Implementation and testing require a separate, concrete approval. A protected controller is not inherently necessary.

My recommendation is to close the present Track A claim and retain a short successor design sketch. Do not build the successor automatically.

Q3. Does Track B separate the candidate from both alternatives?

No. Four problems presently block that conclusion.

1. U-FUNC's sensitivity factor cancels.

The supplied equations define, for presynaptic unit j:

```text
u_FUNC(i,j) = abs(w_ij) * z_j * S_i

T(i->j) = beta * r_i * u(i,j) / sum_k u(i,k)
```

Because `S_i` is common to every term in that denominator, whenever it is positive:

```text
T_FUNC(i->j)
  = beta * r_i * abs(w_ij) * z_j
    / sum_k abs(w_ik) * z_k
```

U-ACT has the same problem with its common factor `z_i`:

```text
u_ACT(i,j) = abs(w_ij) * z_i * z_j
```

For positive `z_i`, its normalized transfer is exactly the same expression.

Thus, at the same state and with positive denominators, U-FUNC and U-ACT allocate identical transfers. Changing the magnitude of output sensitivity does nothing.

At `S_i=0`, U-FUNC instead gives an undefined `0/0`. A rule assigning zero transfer there would introduce an explicit zero-sensitivity gate. It would not establish the claimed graded output-dependence routing.

This is a mathematical blocker, not an empirical uncertainty. N2 cannot proceed in its present form.

There is a second limitation: local perturbation sensitivity is not equivalent to necessity or useful contribution. Redundant pathways can have substantial individual sensitivity, and saturated pathways can have low local sensitivity despite mattering under larger interventions. The statement that single-unit perturbation generally "exposes redundant pathways" is too strong.

2. YOKED-RELIEF does not imply the claimed K&G trajectory.

If actions do not affect relief, resource dynamics or costs, their expected returns may become equal. Equal action values do not determine whether an agent keeps its current orientation.

Keep-probability also depends on:

- The action-selection rule and tie handling.
- Persistent versus independently sampled actions.
- Exploration scheduling.
- Switching costs.
- State representation and temporal information.
- Learning rate and sampling history.

A controller with equal values and a persistence rule can keep its current action. A controller that samples uniformly can change it. Neither behavior follows from the drive-reduction reward alone.

Consequently, there is no justified general prediction that K&G's keep-probability decays below a chosen threshold after E events.

The UDTN prediction is also unsupported. Setting `g=0` stops the specified `Prod` term. It does not freeze recurrent activity, inputs, passive weight decay or the action readout. Therefore:

```text
plasticity stops != orientation remains fixed
```

Likewise, activating a Hebbian or BCM update does not guarantee exploratory variability. The model needs an actual mechanism linking the deficit to changes in orientation.

DP-R with the same resource gate and otherwise matched equations remains an implementation of the same ultrastable mechanism. YOKED-RELIEF cannot distinguish them by relabeling it.

3. ANTICIPATE needs an effective preventive action.

The original K&G anticipation example includes an action that changes the subsequent physiological trajectory and reduces the total deviation. It is not merely a cue followed by an unavoidable deficit and unavoidable relief. [eLife](https://elifesciences.org/articles/04811?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

If Section 10's action-independent resource trajectory carries into ANTICIPATE, early orientation has no specified homeostatic benefit. A cue alone does not create a reason to act.

If contingent sustaining is restored for ANTICIPATE, specify that explicitly, including action duration, preventive effects, costs and the cue-to-outcome state representation. Then anticipation can distinguish named predictive and reactive implementations. It would primarily reveal a limitation of the reactive candidate, not establish its novelty.

The delay guard is also invalid as written:

- A time constant is not a finite memory cutoff.
- Exponential traces remain nonzero beyond one time constant.
- Recurrent dynamics can retain information longer than individual neuronal time constants.
- Resource, stability and weight states can carry history too.
- Freezing plasticity outside shortfall windows does not freeze those states.

A correlation check cannot confirm that no binding occurred. Either prove a finite-support memory restriction, derive a quantitative residual-influence bound, or report memory as an unresolved route. Unexpected anticipation should trigger mechanism analysis, not automatic exclusion from the comparison.

4. Several other controls lack their claimed implications.

Matching mean firing rates does not establish that the DP gate remains inactive. The relevant gate input is the trajectory of `y_i+b_i`, including excursions outside its band. Equal means can conceal very different gate activation.

YOKED-SENSORY may reproduce the candidate's shift. For a deterministic model with identical initial state and all identical received inputs, identical internal evolution is expected. Removing the causal effect of its actions on the replayed stream need not change that realized trajectory. This arm can examine contingency, but "the yoked twin must not shift" is not generally justified.

STIM-ONLY may remove the retrograde resource path in both models when it removes the output connection. High activity alone does not supply trophic resource. The intervention must preserve the relevant resource route while changing the declared functional-dependence variable, or the interpretation is confounded.

What would be more informative?

First repair and simplify the mechanism, then use a two-stage comparison:

- Routing stage: construct matched local-activity cases with different downstream output dependence. Show analytically that the repaired routing law changes support while the co-activity law does not. Include the estimator's information, perturbations, storage and computational costs.
- Regulation stage: hold routing and resource dynamics fixed while comparing deficit-gated plasticity with a fully specified HRL controller. Use an explicitly effective anticipatory action and a separately specified action-independent relief condition.

This could identify contributions of routing and adaptation separately. It cannot establish non-reduction to every possible DP-style or HRL implementation. DP-R equipped with the candidate's routing and update equations will reproduce the candidate by construction.

N1 should already be described as resource-gated ultrastability with organization-dependent resource dynamics. The resource variable and its acceptable range are supplied; they are not acquired essential variables merely because different weights produce different resource trajectories.

Q4. Is this ready as a small hand-built protocol?

No. A hand-built instance would still execute unresolved dynamics and potentially answer the wrong question.

Before an execution proposal, complete these items:

- Resolve the U-FUNC cancellation and zero-denominator behavior.
- Choose the actual `H_ij`, gate, baseline weight, parameter values and action-generation rule.
- Specify a causal path from the vulnerable pathway's deficit to plasticity in the orienting circuit. Local deficits at P do not automatically activate gates elsewhere.
- Define resource nonnegativity, exhaustion and resource-limited spending.
- Define the stability domain. As written, `s_ij>1` reverses the sign of the purported decay term; either prevent that or justify it.
- Specify the output-sensitivity estimator's perturbation scale, observation horizon and operating distribution.
- Account for external relief pulses and stimulation in the resource ledger.
- Define probabilities and variability for the hand-built instance: randomness, input distribution, repetitions and time averaging.
- Separate a finite failed construction from an impossibility result.
- Recover the missing PI directive if any design choice still depends on it.

"No offline training" can be accurate. "Non-trained" is misleading if the experiment includes online synaptic adaptation or TD learning.

Also replace "acquired maintenance dependencies" with "hand-constructed maintenance dependencies" for G2. A supplied instance can support an existence demonstration and bounded intervention claims; it cannot demonstrate acquisition.

The generic 8/10 seed criterion is premature before specifying what seeds randomize and what population the claim concerns.

Q5. Best information per effort

The immediate priority is the algebraic audit of Track B, not another simulation or additional comparison arms.

I would order the work as follows:

1. Close Track A's current closure claim with the corrected statement. Resolve birth targeting by a short code explanation; no run is needed.
2. Record the normalization identity above and withdraw the current N2 prediction.
3. Determine whether one precise routing modification produces a non-canceling, resource-accounted distinction. Require a small analytic example before proposing behavior.
4. Specify the orienting mechanism and derive its predictions against DP-R and one named HRL implementation.
5. Only then choose between a bounded hand-built test and parking Track B.

Do not require a candidate to defeat increasingly general conventional mechanisms to preserve a functional result. Conversely, do not turn architectural stipulations into evidence of novelty.
