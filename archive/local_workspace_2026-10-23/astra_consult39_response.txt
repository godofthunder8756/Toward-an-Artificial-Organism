Two qualifications to Q-R4 are necessary.

First, SYS-PC global has:

```text
Pred_full = Pred_mu
```

It does not have:

```text
Pred_full = actual
```

An actual-valid edit can therefore have two invalid global predictions without contradicting the global cancellation identity. Those predictions are coverage exclusions. Check their finite forecast equality where both predicted beliefs are valid, independently of whether the other class predictors pass the common mask.

Second, a numerical guard mismatch does not necessarily imply that the `1e-9` recovery tolerance was exceeded. A tiny perturbation can place two otherwise close beliefs on opposite sides of the `1e-6` guard.

For a predictor required to recover the actual chart, retain a stopping guard-consistency check. Report the actual and predicted minimum probabilities, chart difference and available recovery residual. Describe the event as a guard inconsistency, not as proof of a large prediction error. Do not relax the guard or tolerance after seeing it.

**4. Stage order and headroom eligibility need correction.**

The P0 dependency problem is not confined to SYS-NC1. SYS-FK's product-chart L table is also needed before its P0 check, but system construction currently occurs later.

Use this order:

```text
S0  Preflight
S1  O1
S2  O2
S3  System construction, validity/admissibility, normalization,
    NC1 coefficient refit, c2/c3, midpoint, L/J/G tables and bounds
S4  Applicable P0 checks
S5  Response-map fits, rank reports, matrix identities and fit freeze
S6  Baselines and finite evaluation, with streaming identity checks
S7  Final aggregation and labels
S8  Reporting and post-run records
```

The NC1 coefficient refit is system construction. It is not one of the intervention-response-map fits that P0 must precede.

During S6, check guard consistency and finite identities when the required values become available. Accumulate maxima and counts. Do not wait until S7 to discover a stopping failure after completing all seven systems.

C7.3 also needs its eligibility gates restored explicitly. VALIDATION MET alone does not establish sufficient SYS-PC comparison coverage or a positive response denominator.

Before issuing a headroom threshold label, require:

- Available required maps and no unresolved required fit.
- The declared count and law-mass coverage in every required fold/class at the reference size.
- A positive scoreable denominator.
- `S > 0`.

Store diagnostic values where permitted, but do not award HEADROOM MET after a coverage failure. An empty identity domain is not a passed identity.

There is a smaller wording conflict in C7.2: an applicable NC1 P0 check is mandatory, while the text says confound systems "never enter VALIDATION." The intended distinction should be that confound gains and descriptive expectations do not enter validation. Mandatory integrity checks remain mandatory.
