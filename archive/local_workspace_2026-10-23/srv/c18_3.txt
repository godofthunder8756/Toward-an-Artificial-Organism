**On #3 specifically.**
- **Edit 6:** "For an exactly zero gradient, use the norm-matched isotropic draw; its first-order match is exact." That draw has no gradient component and a full randomized spread of radius ||d_k||. Setting r_k = ||d_k|| (rho = 1) is the natural value, so the block is genuinely non-collapsed.
- **Edit 5:** R_eff^2 = Sum r_k^2 over **non-collapsed** blocks, so including the block's full radius is correct. Excluding it would wrongly push a seed toward collapse when its family actually has full spread.
- **R4:** the code uses an `all(g == 0)` test, which is the R4 reading. It is not a G_ZERO threshold.
- Skipping the orthogonality check is necessary because gh_k is undefined. The first-order identity is checked instead (both inner products exactly 0).
- **In the data:** no seed has an exactly zero gradient, a NEGLIGIBLE FIRST-ORDER SCALE block, a collapsed or near-collapse block, a fixed block or a zero-displacement block. The minimum rho_k over 90 blocks is 0.813 and the minimum ||g_k||||d_k|| is 2.2e-4.
