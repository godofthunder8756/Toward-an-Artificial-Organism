# AC5 design decision: adaptation must change a maintenance commitment

2026-09-14. Design direction, not a frozen protocol or an implemented result.
AC4 extended follow-up is running separately with unchanged source.

## Why the next step is not another demonstrated action

AC1–AC4 construct increasingly coupled dependencies but supply the controller's
maintenance priorities through demonstrations. This cannot answer whether an
individual acquires or revises what it must maintain. AC3/AC4 rescue conditions
expose a concrete limitation: an externally replaced function can leave the
controller repeatedly attempting unnecessary production while repair suffers.

The conceptual distinction is supported by Di Paolo's separation of continued
self-production from regulation relative to viability. This motivates testing
changed regulation, not counting additional components as adaptation.
[Primary source](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf).
The following operational choices are ours, not claims that the paper endorses
this implementation or supplies a decisive test of autonomy.

## Proposed discriminating challenge

Use the same individual across phases. Initially its produced boundary retains
its machinery. An unannounced external retention substitute then makes B
production unnecessary. Later remove the substitute. A useful adaptive controller
must reduce wasted B-production attempts during substitution, maintain decision
information, and restore B production before irreversible leakage after removal.
No phase label, target policy or correct-action reward enters its state.

This initially tests learned relinquishment/reacquisition of an existing need,
not creation of a wholly new dependency. A later developmental experiment must
introduce an acquired functional organization whose maintenance cost and utility
depend on that individual's history. Keep these claims separate.

## Constraints on implementation

- Action values, expectations, eligibility traces and remembered observations
  are live acquired organization. Put them in the vulnerable, paid storage;
  do not leave a protected learner quietly reconstructing policy.
- Learning writes use interior W and usable energy/material, competing with
  repair. Majority repair may preserve a newly learned value after a paid write;
  the original demonstration is never an online repair target.
- A fixed biochemical learning law may be supplied. Its experience-dependent
  state must be declared and erased with the rest of the body.
- Internal observations can report local resources, damage and reaction outcomes.
  They must not reveal original bits, future survival or counterfactual correct
  actions. An engineered scalar viability reward must be described as such;
  it would not establish intrinsic normativity merely by being named intrinsic.
- Learning cannot rely on host snapshots, free rollouts, resurrection or an
  experimenter selecting only surviving training histories. If developmental
  scaffolding is used, expose its duration and all failed acquisitions.
- The frozen observer may score accuracy and counterfactual effects, but never
  feeds those targets into the live controller.

## Controls required before a positive adaptation claim

Compare live learner, learning-disabled same initial state, protected learned
state, shuffled learning feedback, and an explicitly informed switching control.
Match physical inputs and resource charges where appropriate. Record turnover,
policy/learner-state integrity, attempted and effective B reactions, leakage,
time to behavioral revision and survival across both transitions. Retain all
individuals, including failure to reacquire a necessary commitment.

A successful adaptive policy must outperform its frozen counterpart on the
specified transition without hidden phase information. A generic controller
that already senses retention and switches without learning is a necessary
alternative explanation; compare it rather than declaring learning mandatory.

## Architectural issue to resolve first

AC4's full truth table doubles with every sensor and stores little independent
history (24 permutations). Extending it with another binary flag is unlikely
to produce richer acquired organization. Evaluate a compact mutable rule/value
representation before freezing AC5. Distinguish this representation change from
an empirical gain: it needs its own state-boundary, payment and erasure tests.

This design does not replace the outstanding AC4 extended necessity test, nor
independent review, self-produced memory scaffold, or broader developmental work.
