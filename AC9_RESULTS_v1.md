## Failed hypothesis corrected

The first commentary proposed synchronized extinction of newly seeded regional
W as a possible cause: seeding initially gives three W identical lifetimes.
An observer-only replay reproduces all eight keep endpoints exactly and finds
no occasion when a region with remaining entry matter has zero interior W.
That proposed explanation is therefore unsupported for these observed failures.

First route losses occur at162,557,559,572,711,902 and1157; one history has none.
Further diagnosis must inspect renewal scheduling, remaining lifetimes, available
capacity and resource competition at the actual decision point. Do not change
seed lifetimes on the assumption that extinction caused the losses. Acquiring
an entry and having some W available do not guarantee timely renewal of all its
replicas and vulnerable metadata.

## Verification and limits

Four integrated tests verify paid core-to-region catalyst seeding and deposition,
empty-region demand, whole-state erasure and short replay. Five earlier memory
primitive tests remain applicable. The audit verifies all32 unique conditions,
snapshot hashes, incremental rows, energy/fuel/material accounts including bound
memory and expiry/replacement waste, and eight exact seed1000 reruns. Chronology
adds eight exact keep-history replays. These are same-author checks.

The first integration retains supplied core priorities, geometry, search and
cross-seeding laws. It does not yet include the broader design's fixed-allocation,
inert-entry, shuffled-placement or protected-memory controls. History is a sensory
location input during development, not autonomous site selection. No new-need,
developmental individuality or autopoiesis claim follows from this failed baseline.
