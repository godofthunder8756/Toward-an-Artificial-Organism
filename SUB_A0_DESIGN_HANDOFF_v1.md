## What A0 adds

Self-modification and unreliable-component signal restoration are established
precedents. The proposed new test combines shared damageability, task-only
development, damage-induced allocation to actual performing repair machinery,
and a causal consequence for future repair capacity/acquired skill.
No historical novelty or consciousness claim is made.

## Primary prospective specification

`A = (writes_R/opportunities_R - writes_T/opportunities_T) /
     (writes_R/opportunities_R + writes_T/opportunities_T)`

`D = A_PRESSURE - A_SHAM`

Require `A_PRESSURE > 0.10` AND `D > 0.10` in at least 23/32 planned seeds.
The null is seed-success probability <= 1/2. Ticks are repeated measurements.
No-allocation runs are flagged and retained as failures; all-R/no-T candidates
are unidentifiable and stop. Raw writes are not presumed useful repair.
Task value, bank size, exposure, address bias and ordinary control are explicit
alternative explanations, not hidden by the statistical null.

No objective rewards self-priority, survival, repair volume or homeostasis.
Task-gradient developmental/meta-training remains an externally supplied source
of instrumental priorities and must be declared.
