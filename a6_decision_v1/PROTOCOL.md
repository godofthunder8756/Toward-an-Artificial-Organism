## Above/below strategy and coverage

Prove conditional S1 slope and exact strict-risk-lattice lemmas first.
Use three formulations, two jobs each:

- F1: count-belief MIXTURE relaxation, both S3 slope orientations. S1 positive
  by its conditional competence lemma. Retain binary head ordering and encoder
  no-cross restrictions; drop S2 sharing with COMPONENT-wise dominance proof.
  Count assignments are continuous: an actual encoder can choose different
  messages for differently ordered histories with the same counts.
- F2: all 256 ordered histories with integer assignments, both S3 orientations;
  retain the same necessary restrictions and gate; check incumbent binary-head
  patterns with exact Farkas cuts where obtainable.
- F3: fixed legal heads, shared bounded quadratic utilities and actual
  finite-gain recurrent compilation, one job for existing bank heads and one
  for context-independent singleton-optimal heads. This is discovery ONLY.
  Coefficient boxes, fixed heads and count-symmetric assignments are not
  whole-family restrictions suitable for FAIL.

Every formulation includes all specialist limits and the joint limit.
The finite population lattice gives exact strict integer limits, not a chosen
epsilon. Relaxation jobs use normalized numerical rows for discovery, but retain
integer rows and rational box-corrected dual checking. Numerical infeasibility,
optimality, or a floating lower bound is never certification.

No fixed-message substitution, four separate deployed models, runtime oracle
tables, relaxed utility witness, or pointwise-dominance deletion after restoring
S2 sharing. No exact global optimum is required.

## Hard bounded effort

At most three formulations, six top-level discovery jobs, 100,000 aggregate
integer branch nodes per job, 900 seconds per entire worker process, 4 GiB
per worker, one job/thread at a time, no GPU, and 10,000 cuts per job.
Nested LP/MIP work shares its worker watchdog. Partial/aborted jobs count.
At most eight compiled candidate policies. These are cutoff values, not ETAs.
Windows Job Objects enforce memory and child-process termination; the parent
watchdog terminates only its own specific worker. If enforcement fails, do not
run discovery. Immutable start journals precede process creation.

Stop early only on an independently certified full-gate outcome. Record unused
budget and mathematical/tooling obstacles rather than inventing a certificate.
No extensions without human authorization.

margins, exact population risk and independent fresh-Arm replay for every gate.

orientation/branch coverage. A restricted or conditional bound is not FAIL.
Otherwise UNRESOLVED: record valid bounds, conditional results, candidate
failures, coverage limits and resource use; leave downstream learning blocked.
