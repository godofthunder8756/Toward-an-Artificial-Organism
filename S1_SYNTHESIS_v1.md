the cognition/coupling track (C1→C2→C3→X1). It reads the A3 verdict

## Verdict (one paragraph)

The project has reached a stable epistemic terminal, and it is not "we have not run enough
experiments." **Level (b)** adaptive autonomy is established (Gray-coded relinquishment
streak + allowance-42; adaptive relinquishment of obsolete routes under a self-maintained
decision state). **Level (c)** representation is partial (acquired route memory, interoceptive
only). **Level (a)** organizational closure is **UNRESOLVED** on one named modeling judgment —
J1, whether `advance()` and `prog.choose` are acceptable substrate or undeclared coordinators —
which A2 established no experiment can settle (infinite regress, not an empirical gap). The
**level-(d)** line (maintained belief → metacognition → HOT-2) is **FALSIFIED** on measured,
structural grounds: C2's estimate carries no cause information, C3 has no premise, X1 finds no
coupling to self-maintenance in either direction. The next decision is therefore **not another
experiment**. It is a stop/go decision gated on resolving J1 by argument: affirm the substrate
reading and a scoped partial-self-maintenance paper is warranted; affirm the organism-specific
reading and the next architecture change is to internalize the coordinator and interpreter,
gated on a v2 charter, not on new data.

## 1. Established findings, by claim level

These are the strongest wordings the frozen record earns, level by level (charter §2). No
lower level stands in for a higher one.

**Level (a) — autopoietic organization: PARTIAL, and the verdict is UNRESOLVED.** The
internal-state milestone is real and frozen: the 130-bit controller description lives in
vulnerable, paid-maintained state and is reconstructed, repaired, and repeatedly transferred
to successor storage through a real verify gate (AC79–AC89). The produced machinery is
load-bearing for viability and for the functions: W/C/B are finite-lived, produced,
autocatalytic components, and cutting their production kills the organism with the *content*
intact at death (AC10, AC91, AC92). Every one of the frozen component list C1–C9 meets
C1–C4 *under the §9 substrate classification* (A3 §2 table). But the decisive judgment J1 is
unpicked — the charter §9 declines it, the AC90 review left it open — so the criterion's
`unresolved` clause, not its `supported` clause, is what the evidence triggers. One contested
modeling judgment short of "supported (bounded)."

**Level (b) — adaptive autonomy: ESTABLISHED.** The strongest positive result in the frozen
record: a Gray-coded failure streak (not a reserve) plus the allowance-42 material budget
coordinates the paid reconstruction write with the paid decision write, and the organism
acquires and then *relinquishes* route content in response to its own viability conditions
(AC99/AC100/AC104/AC105; two-way relinquish/restore AC15/AC18/AC75). Strongest wording:
"acquired an instrumental priority for adaptive relinquishment," never "wants"/"needs."

**Level (c) — representation and learning: PARTIAL.** Acquired route memory (`mem.Memory`,
key→port entries bound from the organism's own contact outcomes) is damageable, maintained,
and flexibly consumed (relinquish vs restore). Known limit, stated not hidden: the observation
space is nine host-computed interoceptive threshold bits; there is **no content-bearing
perceptual representation** of an external world (charter §2, J5).

**Level (d) — consciousness indicators: FALSIFIED in the current architecture.** The one
candidate mechanism (the maintained second-order cause estimate, HOT-2 mapping) was built and
measured and is negative (§2). No candidate indicator is met.

**Level (e) — phenomenal consciousness: NOT ASSESSABLE.** No decisive test exists; no card in
this arc may claim progress on it (charter §2).

## 2. Failures — the falsifications, and what they were

The failures are the most informative part of the record, and they are structural, not
sample-size artifacts:

- **The economics wall (AC11/AC12/AC13).** When an intervention invalidates an acquired route,
  income from it goes to exactly zero, starvation stops the renewal spending, and the entry
  lapses *whether or not* the policy chose to relinquish it. `allocate` is bit-for-bit
  indistinguishable from `preserve`. The decision is downstream of an economics that already
  fixed the outcome.
- **Gate-shape falsifications (AC15/AC16/AC17).** A mean margin over a rival that partially
  succeeds measures how often it gets lucky; strict per-individual dominance is unsatisfiable
  when both arms reach the ceiling. Two consecutive versions died on the *shape of the gate*,
  not the mechanism.
- **Content self-production blocked (AC78).** The production signal is a locked, path-dependent
  fixed point; a single life is one sample (N_eff ≈ 1), and the responsive window is
  path-dependent at the same magnitude as the ranking it must resolve. Not required for
  autopoiesis, and not claimed.
- **Composition survival of one unseen final fails (AC101).** The behavioural arm of the
  internal-state composition does not transfer to an unseen final seed — recorded, not moved.
- **C2 — the maintained belief carries no information (AC106).** The "productive-after-failure
  → E_machinery" update rule is confounded by blind re-acquisition, so the estimate reads
  E_machinery in *both* causes; its causal role is partial and seed-dependent (holds 14/16 vs
  r2's 8/16 in the cut, failing exactly on the fast-streak corner); its proactive renewal is
  redundant with the frozen reactive renewal (97–171 wasted writes).
- **C3 — no metacognition premise.** C2's estimate has no varying first-order accuracy, so a
  reliability estimate has nothing to predict or control. Closed, not blocked.
- **X1 — no coupling.** Neither direction holds: the estimate's maintenance does not change
  viability (survival identical to r2 and scramble), and maintenance sustains no cognitive
  function (the organism relies on the host-side first-order machinery). The card's
  falsification, reached by its own pre-run scramble control.

Each of these is a falsification of a *specific mechanism* under a *specific operational
definition* — none of them is a verdict on autopoiesis or consciousness in general.

## 3. Remaining modeling judgments

Named in charter §9, not resolved here, and — the crucial fact — **not resolvable by
experiment**:

- **J1 (decisive).** Is `advance()`'s copy→verify→switch→remove transition logic and
  `prog.choose`'s rule-matching interpreter acceptable generic substrate, or always-available
  coordinators that assume a functional service without demonstrating its production? If
  substrate, C1–C9 pass under the substrate reading; if organism-specific, they fail C2. The
  verdict is unresolved on this one judgment alone.
- **J2.** "Format-level" vs "host-supplied functional service" — naming is not deciding.
- **J3.** Inherited instructions (permitted) vs undeclared external content.
- **J4.** Whether the produced boundary constitutes the system as a unity in space, or merely
  retains particles.
- **J5.** Whether a system with a host-computed observation over its own state can *represent*
  anything — this caps level (c) and every level-(d) indicator.

## 5. Novelty assessed against prior work

The charter's primary prior work is Maturana & Varela 1980 (autopoiesis), Montévil & Mossio
2015 (organizational closure), Di Paolo 2005 (adaptivity), and Butlin et al. 2023 (consciousness
indicators); the E3 literature review (`E3_LITERATURE_REVIEW_v0_1.md`) adds the maintenance
prior art (synaptic tagging, cascade models, EWC, ECC). The strongest contribution must be
compared against these *before* any novelty is asserted.

**The strongest contribution, honestly stated.** It is not a positive autopoiesis or
consciousness result — neither is established. It is a **worked example with a catalog of
structural negatives**:

1. A fully-specified, auditable simulation that demonstrates *partial* organizational
   self-maintenance and *established* adaptive reallocation, and that demonstrates — by the
   discipline of a pre-fixed criterion applied unchanged — that the residual gap to "closure"
   is a **named modeling judgment (J1), not an empirical gap**. The negative/philosophical
   finding is the contribution: even with a machine-checkable finite closure criterion and a
   working body, the substrate/coordinator boundary cannot be settled from inside the model.
2. A sequence of **structural falsifications** — the economics wall, the first-order-redundancy
   wall, the locked-fixed-point signal wall — each established by pre-registered controls, each
   a property of the world/mechanism design rather than of the 8 engineering seeds.

**The comparison.**

- *Against Maturana & Varela / Montévil & Mossio:* the concepts are theirs; the project adds no
  new concept. The finite closure criterion (C1–C4 over a frozen component list) is a
  *disciplined application* of "closure of constraints," not a novel operationalization. The
  project's addition is the negative demonstration that the criterion's residual is the
  substrate/coordinator boundary — a finding *about* the criterion's limits, not an advance on
  autopoiesis theory. **No conceptual novelty.**
- *Against Di Paolo (adaptivity):* the level-(b) adaptive relinquishment is an *instantiation*
  of adaptivity in a toy model, not an advance on it. The Gray-streak + allowance-42 is an
  engineering solution to the maintenance/adaptation conflict, novel only within this model's
  parameterization. **No theoretical novelty.**
- *Against Butlin et al. (indicator framework):* the C-track tested a HOT-2 candidate and
  falsified it. This is informative as a *negative* about a specific candidate mechanism, but
  it falsifies the project's own mechanism, not the framework. **No advance on the framework.**
- *Against the maintenance prior art (cascades, EWC, ECC, synaptic tagging):* the E3 review
  already concluded "adaptive correction effort is established territory, not generic novelty,"
  and "no absence-of-prior-art claim follows from that gap." The project's reconstruction and
  renewal machinery is a toy instantiation of territory the literature already occupies.

**Honest novelty statement.** The project advances no concept in autopoiesis, adaptivity, or
consciousness science. What it can legitimately claim is narrower and methodological: (i) a
worked, falsification-disciplined example of why a fully-specified simulated organism can
demonstrate partial self-maintenance yet still not earn a closure verdict — the residual being
a modeling judgment, not missing data; and (ii) a reproducible catalog of *structural* reasons
that a family of "acquired maintenance decision" and "maintained belief" designs fail. That is
a methods-and-negative-results contribution, and nothing in it should be described as an AI
breakthrough.

## 6. What could support a paper — and what it would require

Two candidate framings, with different prerequisites:

**A. A negative/structural-findings paper (defensible now, requires nothing further to be
resolved).** *"Three structural walls for acquired maintenance decisions and maintained belief
in a bounded simulated organism"* — the economics wall, the first-order-redundancy wall, the
locked-fixed-point signal wall. This is defensible *today* because each negative is a property
of the world/mechanism design verified in code, not a sample-size artifact (X1's stopping rule
applies to the whole catalog). Scope it as bounded to one toy model and one parameter regime;
make no autopoiesis or consciousness claim; the contribution is the falsifications and the
method (pre-registered rivals, byte-identity single-change licenses, engineering-before-protocol
discipline).

**B. A partial-self-maintenance paper (requires J1 to be resolved first).** *"Partial
organizational self-maintenance and adaptive reallocation in a fully-specified simulated
organism."* Defensible **only if** a review affirms the substrate reading of J1; then the
level-(a) claim is "meets the finite closure criterion bounded to the substrate
classification," and the positive substance is carried by the level-(b) adaptive result and the
produced-W/C/B load-bearing ablation (AC10/AC91/AC92). Without that affirmation, this paper
overclaims exactly the thing the track exists to prevent.

**What is not supportable as a paper:** any unqualified "autopoietic", "alive",
"self-sustaining", "conscious", "metacognitive", or "self-aware" claim — all are unresolved,
falsified, or not assessable in this record.

## 7. The next decision, and the evidence that would warrant each branch

The next decision is a **stop/go decision gated on resolving J1 by argument**, because no
experiment can settle it (A2's infinite-regress argument). There are three branches, each with
its own warranting evidence:

**Branch 1 — Resolve J1, then write up (the default).** A review/argument decision on whether
`advance()` and `prog.choose` are substrate or coordinators.

- *Evidence that warrants "supported (bounded)" + paper B:* an affirmative review decision on
  the **substrate** reading. This is an argument, not data; the composing evidence is already
  frozen and unchanged (A3 §2). No new experiment is needed.
- *Evidence that warrants a v2-charter architecture change:* an affirmative review decision on
  the **organism-specific** reading. This reclassifies `advance()`/`prog.choose` as components
  with no production edge (they fail C2), requiring `DEFINITIONS_CHARTER_v2.md` to add them to
  the component list, and the architecture change is to give them a production edge — i.e.
  internalize the coordinator mechanism and interpreter as produced/replaced components. The
  warrant for that change is the review decision itself, not new data; and the change is then
  gated on C1–C4 over the new list. *Caveat carried forward:* A2 established that each
  internalization pushes the substrate one level down; it does not close it. A reviewer should
  be told plainly that this change is not guaranteed to move the verdict.

**Branch 2 — Do not re-open the C-track.** The cognition/coupling line is closed, not blocked
(C3). Re-opening it is a *re-design*, not a re-test, and it is **not** the next step. It would
require, in engineering before any protocol: (a) an un-confounded update rule (C2's R1 — gate
the E_machinery conclusion on the entry still being *bound*), and (b) a first-order controller
that errs uniformly (C2's R2/R3 — a cut longer than the streak's reset horizon, or a
maintenance target the reactive renewal cannot reach), so the estimate's hold does something
the frozen streak does not. And even then, J5 (can a host-computed-interoceptive-observation
system represent anything) may cap it. None of this is authorized here; it is a different
question than the one the C-track asked, and the track's stopping rule holds.

**Branch 3 — Do not start a repair study.** No further allocation/relinquishment, renewal,
reconstruction, or robustness study is justified by the current record. The robustness items
(allowance-42 generalization, route-holding lower bound, survival bimodality) are bounds on
already-established claims, not reasons to change architecture; the AC15/16/17 and
AC11/12/13 lessons show that further studies in that family are most likely to re-measure a
known wall, not resolve one.

**The single sentence decision:** *Stop building; write up the structural negatives now; resolve
J1 by argument; write up partial self-maintenance only if J1 is affirmed substrate; do not
change the architecture unless J1 is affirmed organism-specific.*

## 8. Consciousness gating (the card's explicit control)

This synthesis makes **no** consciousness claim, and nothing here gates one. The level-(d)/(e)
boundary is absolute and is governed by the charter §2 and `CONSCIOUSNESS_ROADMAP_v1.md`, not by
this document. The C-track's negative is a falsification of one candidate mechanism (the
maintained cause estimate) under one operational definition, reached by its own scramble control
— it is **not** a verdict that the organism (or any system) is or is not conscious, and it does
not raise or lower the probability of any other consciousness claim. Any future level-(d) claim
must satisfy its own operational definition and its own discriminating experiments, and is
capped at "meets candidate indicator X at degree Y", never "conscious". This synthesis does not
cross that boundary and does not move it.
